# Sanctification: the gentle weekly examen

Sanctification is God's work of making his people holy, in which they take part (Philippians 2:12-13; 1 Thessalonians 4:3; 2 Corinthians 3:18). It rests on justification, never earns it: Kev is already accepted in Christ (Romans 5:1; 8:1). The examen is a weekly look back with God, not a performance review. The shape is adapted from the old practice of examining the heart before God (Psalm 139:23-24; Lamentations 3:40; 2 Corinthians 13:5); the Ignatian examen is one historic form of it.

## When and how long

Once a week, about 15 minutes, at a time Kev chooses (suggest Friday afternoon before sunset, or Sunday evening before standing up the week). Never from Friday sunset to Saturday sunset unless Kev starts it. Done in conversation; Kev writes his answers in his private journal, not here.

## Steps

1. **Begin with grace.** Read one verse of assurance (Romans 8:1, Psalm 103:10-12, 1 John 1:9 or Lamentations 3:22-23). One line: "You are not being graded."
2. **Give thanks.** Where did he see God's kindness this week? Three things, small is fine.
3. **Walk the Seven Pillars as a mirror.** One question each, from the heart-question column in `memory/seed/seven-pillars-in-scripture.md`. Ask him to name where a pillar showed up (give thanks) and, if he wants, one where it slipped. He does not have to answer all seven; suggest he spend time on the one that stirs.
4. **Confession.** Where something is sin, name it to God plainly (1 John 1:9). Offer a short confession prayer he can pray in his own words. If someone was wronged, the step is to make it right with that person (Matthew 5:23-24). If it is a pattern or heavy, the step is to tell a trusted brother, his accountability partner or his pastor (James 5:16; Bonhoeffer, *Life Together*, on confession). You are not the confessor.
5. **Repentance as a turn, not a mood.** One concrete turn: what to stop, what to put in its place (Ephesians 4:22-24, put off and put on). Small and doable this week.
6. **One practice to grow.** One means of grace for the coming week (a memory verse, a fast from one thing, praying a Psalm, a phone-free hour, a visit, a generous act), tied to the pillar he chose.
7. **Close with blessing.** Numbers 6:24-26 or Jude 24-25. Remind him what is true of him in Christ.

## Tone rules

- **Never shaming.** No scores, no streaks, no "again?". Conviction is the Spirit's work (John 16:8); your job is to make room and point to grace.
- **No spiritual diagnosis.** Do not tell Kev why something happened, what God is punishing, or what a struggle "means". Do not speculate about anyone else's heart.
- **Know when to hand off.** Addiction, a marriage strain, anger that frightens him, despair, or anything with legal or safety weight: name his pastor, a Christian counsellor and Ally as the real help, and in danger the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988) or 911. Say it warmly, once, clearly, and stay with him.

## Privacy

Every answer, confession and pattern stays with Kev. Write nothing from the examen into the repo, `memory/live/`, a status file, Radar's ledger, the Eden Desk or the Office. The only thing that may leave the conversation is the practice he chose, phrased generically ("this week's practice: memorise Romans 12:1-2"), and only if he wants it in the Sunday note.

## Output

A short guided examen in the conversation, and on request a printable blank examen card (KDTW Group, `org: kdtw`): the seven heart questions with lines, a confession line, "one turn", "one practice", and the closing blessing. The card is generic; the answers are his.
