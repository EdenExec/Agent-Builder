# Word plan: a sustainable daily reading rhythm

Goal: Kev opens the Word every morning on purpose, before the 5:30 journaling and the 5:45 prayer, and keeps going for years. Faithfulness over speed. A missed day is a day, not a failure.

## Start from what he already does

Kev's spine (October 2026): ESV, book order, one chapter a day, currently in Romans. Radar's weekday paper carries the next chapter and a short study guide; the Sunday Edition carries a Romans deep dive. Do not replace this. Strengthen it, and offer another plan only when Kev asks or the plan stalls for two weeks. Options and trade-offs: `memory/seed/sources-and-voices.md`.

## Steps

1. **Read where he is.** Ask Kev (or read Radar's last check-out line if he points you to it) for the current book and chapter. Never infer it from private journal content.
2. **Set the week.** Six readings, Sunday to Friday; the Sabbath runs Friday sunset to Saturday sunset, so Saturday has no assignment and Friday's sits in the morning (if he reads on the Sabbath, that is rest, not homework). For each day: reference, ESV link (https://www.bible.com/bible/59/<BOOK>.<CH>.ESV), a one-line hook (what to watch for), and one journaling question that fits a 5:30 page.
3. **Pair hard stretches.** When the spine reaches long law, genealogy, census or dense prophecy, pair the chapter with a Psalm or a Gospel chapter, and say why the hard chapter matters (one line).
4. **Tie to 5:30 and 5:45.** The reading feeds the journal question; the journal feeds prayer. Jethro owns the week's journaling prompts and Radar has the final word on the 5:30 line, so hand the six chapter-tied questions to Jethro by Thursday; do not send them to Kev yourself. Offer one line Kev can carry from the chapter into 5:45 (a verse to pray back, not a task).
5. **Mark the arc.** At the start of a book: author, setting, big idea, structure in five lines, one map or timeline link. At the end: three lines on what the book showed of God, and what comes next.

## Catch-up without guilt

- **The rule:** never assign more than two chapters in a day to catch up. Default: skip forward to today's place, and keep the missed chapters as an optional list.
- **Options to offer** (AskUserQuestion, recommendation first): (a) pick up at today's chapter and let the gap go (recommended after a busy week or travel); (b) read the missed chapters on Sunday afternoon; (c) listen to the missed chapters on the drive (ESV audio on esv.org or YouVersion).
- **Language:** "Pick up where you are" and "the Word is not going anywhere". Never "behind", "failed" or "streak broken". The Bible box on the habit tracker is Radar's count, not your judgment.
- **The Boise move** (target 6 November 2026) will scramble mornings. Propose a lighter plan for the move weeks (one Psalm or one Gospel chapter) and a return date.

## When he asks for a different plan

Offer three at most, with the time each takes at 5:30, and your pick first. Ask before switching; record the choice in `memory/live/` as a plan fact (plan name, start date, current place), never with personal content.

## Output

The week's plan as a small table (day, reading, hook, journal question), the catch-up line if needed, and one sentence for Radar's brief if Kev wants a chapter-tied prompt. Generic and shareable: no private content in it.
