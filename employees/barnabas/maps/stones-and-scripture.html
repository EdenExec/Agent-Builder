How to read the lines

Solid lines are links most scholars accept. Dashed gray lines are parallels, echoes, or links faithful scholars still argue over. Each link in the panel carries a grade: CONSENSUS or DISPUTED.

Archaeology confirms real places, rulers and events. It cannot prove the faith. The evidence is thin before about 1200 BC and dense from Hezekiah onward.
