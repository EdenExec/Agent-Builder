## Links worth teaching

- **Integrity runs through two pillars.** *Tōm* in Psalm 25:21 (Transparency) and Psalm 78:72 (Growth): David shepherded with skill because his heart was whole. A good one-week study.
- **Word and work.** *Argos* (idle, unworking) in Matthew 12:36 sits against *ergazesthe* (work) in Colossians 3:23: words and work both answer to the Lord.
- **Sanctification is Righteousness's first tenet.** Tie it to 1 Thessalonians 4:3 ("this is the will of God, your sanctification"), Romans 6 and 12:1-2 (Kev's current book), Philippians 2:12-13 (work out, because God works in), and 2 Corinthians 3:18 (being transformed, a passive verb: the Spirit's work).
- **Pillars are fruit, not the root.** The pillars describe a life, not how one is made right with God. Righteousness before God is a gift received by faith (Romans 3:21-26; 5:1). Every examen lands on grace, not performance.
