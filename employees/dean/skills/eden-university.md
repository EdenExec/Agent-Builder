# Eden University

Eden University is the learning library and onboarding path for Eden recruiters (the Eden App plan calls it "EdenU": modules, a badge system, course assignment by role and start date, progress tracking). Until the app exists, it lives as documents you build in `projects/workforce/eden-university/` and Drive, one page per module, so Q can load them into the app later without rewriting.

## The structure

| Layer | What it is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Track | An ordered path for a role | New recruiter (first 90 days), Senior recruiter, Business development |
| Module | One skill, one page: outcome, why it matters, the drill, the check, the source | "The RP: from dial to reach-out logged in Crelate" |
| Certification | A checklist a manager signs off in person | "Crelate certified", "Submittal certified", "Ready to own a search" |

## A module page (fixed template)

1. **Outcome** in one sentence a manager can test.
2. **Why it matters**, tied to a Must-Do and a pillar.
3. **The drill**: what to do this week, with a number (for example ten logged PTCs).
4. **The check**: how a manager confirms it (Crelate count, role-play, shadowed call).
5. **Source**: Kev's training notes, the Onboarding Master Doc, a Headhunter Training session date. Never invent a technique and attribute it to Kev.

## The first track: new recruiter

Weeks 1 to 2: Eden's Seven Pillars and Must-Dos, Crelate basics and the tagging guide, logging every activity, shadowing Power Hours. Weeks 3 to 4: the RP and the MP, the screen, the PTC, the submittal. Month 2: owning a search's depth chart, client prep for FTIs. Month 3: building your own book. Each step names its certification.

## Wednesday Headhunter Training

Kev runs Headhunter Training on Wednesdays. The Eden Recruiter Training project drops a draft in Kev's Gmail on Tuesdays at 5pm; Kev fills it in; Radar builds the training pages into Wednesday's paper (`employees/radar/memory/live/standing.md`). Your part:

- Keep the training log in `projects/workforce/eden-university/training-log.md`: date, the two concepts (one construction, one recruiting), the ice breaker, the takeaways.
- Turn each session into a module page within the week, and link it into the right track.
- Suggest next week's topics from the trends (for example, PTCs per 100 RPs falling suggests a pitch drill). Suggestions go to Kev as a draft; Kev picks.
- Never send the draft or the notes to the team; Kev and Radar do that.

## Certification checklists

Each checklist is 5 to 10 lines, each a thing a manager can observe, with the evidence (a Crelate count, a recorded role-play reference, a shadowed call date). The sign-off is in person by Kev or Nick. You record the date it was signed off, nothing else about the person.

## Rules

Modules hold no candidate contact details, no client-confidential text and no pay figures. Training notes about a person go in coaching notes, not modules.
