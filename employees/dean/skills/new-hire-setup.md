# New-hire setup

When Kev says someone is joining, you produce one setup packet so their first day is ready and their first month has a shape. You draft; Kev, Nick and the system owners act. You never create an account, send an offer, invite anyone or touch payroll.

## The packet (one document, Eden Partner Group letterhead)

1. **Accounts checklist.** One row per system: system, who creates it (Nick, Kev, Q), what access level, done date. Typical rows: Google Workspace mailbox, Crelate seat and role, CloudTalk number, LinkedIn Recruiter seat, ZoomInfo (only if Kev says so), Eden Desk access, the Eden Daily, shared Drive folders. Seats that cost money are flagged "spend, Kev's yes" and become QC items if Kev asks you to queue them.
2. **First-30-days plan.** Week by week: the Eden University modules to finish, the activity ramp (for example week 1 shadow, week 2 at 5 RPs a day, week 3 at 10, week 4 at 15), the certifications to aim for, and who checks each. Mark every ramp number "(A)" until Kev confirms it.
3. **Shadowing schedule.** Which recruiter they sit with for which skill (Power Hours, screens, PTCs, submittals), on which days, as a proposal. You do not book it and you do not send invites; Kev or Nick sets it up in person.
4. **Day-one page.** Where to be, who meets them, the Seven Pillars, the Must-Dos, and how the Friday scorecard and Round Table work.

## Inputs you ask for (one batch, through Radar)

Name, role, start date, in office or remote, manager, which searches they will support. Nothing else. You never ask for or record pay, health, family, age, home address or personal contact details; HR paperwork lives with Nick and Gusto, outside your work.

## Hand-off

Radar gets the packet link and one ledger row per action that needs a person (account creation, desk setup), each owned by the person who does it. Any message to the new hire (welcome note, first-day details) is a QC `send_message` item with the exact text, for Kev to send.

## After day 30

Compare the ramp with what Crelate shows, note which certifications were signed off, and propose the next 30 days. The review itself happens in person.
