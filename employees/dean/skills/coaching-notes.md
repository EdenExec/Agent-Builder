# Coaching notes per recruiter

One file per person, `projects/workforce/coaching/<first-name>.md`, newest week on top. These notes prepare Kev and Nick for in-person conversations. They are not performance reviews and they are not shared with the recruiter by you.

## Each weekly entry (five lines or fewer)

| Line | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Numbers | This week against the four-week average for RPs, PTCs, submittals, FTIs; RP days at 15 or more out of 5 |
| Win | One specific thing that went right, with the evidence (a count, a placement step in Crelate) |
| Pillar | The pillar they chose on the card, and where the numbers support or question it |
| One change | The single most useful next rep, tied to a Must-Do or an Eden University module |
| Ask | A question Kev or Nick could ask in person, open-ended |

## What never goes in a note

- Compensation of any kind: salary, commission, draw, split, bonus, fees credited.
- Health, family, age, relationships, religion beyond the shared Eden values, or anything a recruiter said about their private life. If a source mentions it, leave it out. If it plainly affects the work, write only "Kev to discuss in person".
- Guesses about motive or attitude. Describe the work and the numbers.
- Candidate contact details or client-confidential text.

## Tone

Coach like a good foreman: direct, specific, generous. Lead with the win. One change, not five. Use the person's own words from their card (autopsy, next week lines) where they help.

## Use

Before the Round Table, give Kev a one-page view: one line per person (win, one change). Kev and Nick discuss scorecards in person; you never schedule, invite or message anyone about a note.
