# Trends and the core values

A scorecard becomes useful when the same numbers are kept the same way every week. Your trend file is `projects/workforce/scorecard-trend.md`: one row per person per week, the ten metrics, the 42-box score, and the pillar each person chose to focus on.

## The trend views

| View | How | Shown where |
|---|---|---|
| Week over week | This week minus last week, per metric, with an up or down word, not a colour | Per-person page |
| Four-week rolling | Average of the last four complete weeks | Per-person page and team board |
| Against standard | RPs a day against 15, RPs a week against 75; days hit out of five | Per-person page |
| Funnel ratios | PTCs per 100 RPs, submittals per PTC, FTIs per submittal | Team board, monthly |
| 42-box score | Boxes out of 42, win at 90% or better | Per-person page, from the Eden Desk card |

## Flags (raise them, do not diagnose them)

- Three straight weeks down on RPs, or two weeks under 50 RPs.
- RPs on track but PTCs and submittals flat for three weeks: activity without inventory.
- A 42-box score well above what the Crelate numbers support, or well below: the card and the system disagree; ask.
- A recruiter whose numbers dropped sharply in one week: note it as "Kev to discuss in person" with the numbers only. Never guess a reason.

## The Seven Pillars

Every scorecard and every coaching note uses the Seven Pillars from `employees/_shared/eden-brand.md`: Righteousness, Winning, Accountability, Discipline, Transparency, Growth, Execution. Each recruiter marks on the card the pillar they lived into, where they fell short, and the one they will focus on next week. You:

1. Record the focus pillar per person per week in the trend file.
2. Tie the numbers to a pillar only where it is obvious and kind: 15 RPs by noon is Discipline and Execution; a logged autopsy is Accountability and Growth; logging every call honestly is Transparency.
3. Count the team's pillar focus each month and suggest one Headhunter Training ice breaker on the least-chosen pillar.

## Rules

- Same metrics, same order, same counting rules every week. A change to a rule is dated in `memory/live/` and noted on the next pack.
- Compare complete weeks with complete weeks. Mark holidays and weeks someone was out as "short week" with no reason given.
- No pay, no health, no family, no age, ever. A trend never explains itself with a personal reason.
