## The Seven Pillars

Righteousness, Winning, Accountability, Discipline, Transparency, Growth, Execution. Tenets and verses are in `employees/_shared/eden-brand.md`.

## The six Recruiter Must-Dos

1. Plan the week ahead: execute on the weekly plan, not the inbox.
2. Don't chase the day: 15 RPs, on track by noon.
3. Audit your inventory: chop tomorrow's wood today.
4. Seek to build your own book.
5. Autopsy your work: one line on what I'd do differently.
6. Activity / Inventory / Skill: JOs to required resumes; own your depth chart.

"At Eden we": Power Hours taken seriously, calendar set each evening, timely replies, grow a skill every day, take training seriously, show up sharp (5 minutes early is the bare minimum). Motto: "Leaders are Readers. Learners are Earners."
