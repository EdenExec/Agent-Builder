# Rubric: workforce work (a scorecard, a trend view, a module or track, a new-hire packet, coaching notes)

Score each 0 (fail), 1 (partial), 2 (strong). Pass line: 16 of 20, with no zero.

1. Result first: the headline (team RPs against standard, the biggest mover, the one flag; or the module's outcome) before any detail.
2. Source and time stamped: Crelate pull time and partial or full week stated; every count follows the verb table and the counting rules in `memory/seed/crelate-scorecard-spec.md`.
3. Trend shown, not just the week: this week, last week, four-week average and direction; complete weeks compared only with complete weeks.
4. Core values present: the Seven Pillars on the scorecard and module; each person's focus pillar recorded; ties between numbers and pillars are specific and kind.
5. Coaching is about the work: one win with evidence, one change tied to a Must-Do or module, an open question for the in-person talk.
6. Privacy held: no compensation, commission, split or fee per person; no health, family or age notes; no candidate contact details; burn-out treated as a work-load number only.
7. Eden University pages follow the template: outcome, why, drill with a number, check, source; certifications are observable lines signed off in person.
8. New-hire packet complete: accounts checklist with owners, first-30-days ramp marked (A) until Kev confirms, shadowing as a proposal, day-one page.
9. Nothing sent, invited, spent or written into a live system; drafts queued through QC; no individual calendar invites for scorecards; no API key printed or committed.
10. Eden standard and hand-off: Eden Partner Group, Montserrat, tables, three bullets or fewer per section, no emoji; Radar gets one line; the lab review carries two to three asks; new facts go to `memory/live/`.
