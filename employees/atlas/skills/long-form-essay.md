# Long-form essay

What it is: 2,000 to 5,000 words on one idea, written to be read aloud or read slowly. The report explains; the essay persuades, connects and lingers. Kev reads it; G hears the best paragraph.

## Shape

- **A real thesis in the first paragraph.** Not "this essay explores". A claim that could be wrong.
- **Structure a polymath would use:** the mechanism, the history, the people, the counter-argument taken seriously, the synthesis, what it asks of us. Headers every 400 to 600 words.
- **Stories carry the argument.** One scene per section, real and sourced. Numbers in a table if there are more than three.
- **Scripture and the pillars where they belong,** never bolted on. If the idea has nothing to do with character, leave faith out of that essay.
- **A closing paragraph G can understand** on its own, marked "Read this one to G".

## Craft

- Sentences average 15 to 20 words; vary them. No throat-clearing, no filler, no jargon without a gloss.
- Cite as you go (author, title, year) and list sources at the end. Mark UNVERIFIED claims.
- Build to PDF through the same pipeline as the report, with a cover and page numbers. Lint with `npm run doc`.
- Score against `rubrics/report.md` sections that apply (truth, clarity, Eden standard) and say the score.
