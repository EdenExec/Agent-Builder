## What to teach

1. **Join, or Die.** 1754 cartoon; the Albany Plan of Union was turned down. Years in London speaking for the colonies; in 1774 he stood silent while an official berated him for about an hour, and turned toward independence. 1776: Committee of Five for the Declaration (Jefferson wrote most of it), signed at 70.
2. **France and peace.** Sailed to France in late 1776; the fur cap, a celebrity in Paris. The 1778 alliance brought French soldiers, money and ships; Yorktown, 1781. Signed the Treaty of Paris in 1783. The only person to sign the Declaration, the Treaty of Paris and the Constitution.
3. **The Constitution.** 1787, aged 81, the oldest delegate, sometimes carried in a chair. Motion for daily prayer (June 28): "God governs in the affairs of men. And if a sparrow cannot fall to the ground without His notice, is it probable that an empire can rise without His aid?" (Matthew 10:29; not voted on). The two-house compromise. The rising sun on Washington's chair. "A republic, madam, if you can keep it." Honest note: he owned enslaved people when younger, changed his mind, led the Pennsylvania Abolition Society (1787 to 1790), petitioned Congress in 1790. Died April 17, 1790; about 20,000 at the funeral. His mock epitaph: the old book, revised and corrected by the Author.

## Questions to end on

1. **What did the snake cartoon say?** Join, or die.
2. **Why was the snake cut into pieces?** Each piece was a colony; alone they'd die, together they'd live.
3. **How old was Ben when he signed the Declaration?** Seventy.
4. **Which country did Ben win over as an ally?** France.
5. **What did he wear in Paris instead of a wig?** A fur cap.
6. **How old was he at the Constitutional Convention?** Eighty-one, the oldest delegate.
7. **What did he say about a sparrow?** If a sparrow can't fall without God's notice, a nation can't rise without His help.
8. **Was the sun on Washington's chair rising or setting?** Rising.
9. **What did he tell Mrs. Powel?** A republic, if you can keep it.
10. **What did Ben change his mind about?** Slavery: he came to fight to end it.
