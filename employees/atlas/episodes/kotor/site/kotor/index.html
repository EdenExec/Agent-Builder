Kotor Old Town Walk

Seven stops, seven short stories, at your own pace. Made for Kim and Dave by Kev.

7Stops
⅓ mileWalking
19 minListening

How it works

  1. Start outside the Sea Gate, the Old Town's main gate, on the water side facing the harbor.
  2. At each stop, tap the big play button. Each story is two to three minutes.
  3. The story ends by telling you where to walk next. Tap Walk here if you want the map.

Tap this on the ship's Wi-Fi before you go ashore. The stories then play even with no signal in Kotor.

Whole route map All stops, one file

Good to know

  • Getting to Kotor: the ship's schedule shows it docking at Bar, about 40 miles down the coast. Kotor is 1¼ to 1½ hours each way by road. The ship's Kotor excursion, or a driver booked ahead, is easiest. Ask to be dropped at the Old Town's Main Gate (the Sea Gate). If the ship ends up docking in Kotor itself, the gate is just across the road from the pier.
  • All aboard: leave at least two hours to get back to the ship from Kotor. The coast road can be slow.
  • Free: the squares, the lanes, St. Luke's and St. Nicholas. Small tickets: the Cathedral, the Maritime Museum, and the fortress climb.
  • Optional extra, with care: the climb to the Fortress of St. John. Roughly 1,300 uneven steps, very little shade, and close to 900 feet up. Stopping at the little church of Our Lady of Remedy, partway up, gives a lovely view for much less effort. Bring water and go slowly, especially coming down.
  • Comfort: the old stone is polished and slippery, so wear shoes with grip. Cover shoulders in St. Nicholas. Montenegro uses the euro.
