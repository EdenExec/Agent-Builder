## The three ideas

- **Born in India.** Chaturanga, played by the 6th century: the four parts of an old Indian army. Foot soldiers became pawns, horses stayed knights, elephants became bishops (still called elephants in some languages), chariots became rooks. The king's counselor stood beside the king and moved one square diagonally.
- **Through Persia, Baghdad and Spain.** Persia about 600 AD (chatrang, later shatranj). "Shah!" warned the king; "shah mat" (the king is helpless) became checkmate. The oldest recorded game is from 10th-century Baghdad. Into Europe through Spain and Italy; by about 1000 AD across much of Europe. The Lewis chessmen: found 1831 on the Isle of Lewis, Scotland, about 93 walrus-ivory pieces, made about 1150 to 1200, probably in Trondheim, Norway; rooks as berserkers biting their shields. British Museum (82) and National Museum of Scotland (11).
- **The queen's upgrade.** Valencia, Spain, 1475 to 1500: the queen goes from one diagonal step to any direction, any distance; the bishop gets stronger too. Earliest record: the Catalan poem Scachs d'amor, 1475. Plus the rice-and-chessboard legend (a legend; the doubling math is real: 2^64 - 1, about 18.4 quintillion grains).

## Ten questions for the truck

1. **Where was chess born?** India, as chaturanga.
2. **What does chaturanga mean?** Four parts: the four parts of an army.
3. **What was the bishop before it was a bishop?** An elephant.
4. **What was the rook?** A chariot.
5. **Where does "checkmate" come from?** Persian "shah mat": the king is helpless.
6. **How did chess reach Europe?** Through Spain and Italy, by about 1000 AD across much of Europe.
7. **What are the Lewis chessmen made of?** Mostly walrus ivory.
8. **When did the queen get strong, and where?** About 1475, Valencia, Spain.
9. **How many grains on square 16 in the rice legend?** 32,768. Doubling gets huge fast.
10. **Pawn, knight, bishop, rook, queen: what was each one first?** Foot soldier, horse, elephant, chariot, the king's counselor.

## Try this

Pick up each piece and tell Dad what it used to be (pawn, foot soldier; knight, horse; bishop, elephant; rook, chariot; queen, counselor). Then look up the Lewis chessmen together and find a berserker rook.

## For Kev

- The "India first" story is the mainstream view; some historians have argued for other origins (China's xiangqi is a cousin). The episode says "most of the clues point to India".
- The rice legend is told as a legend in the script. 18.4 quintillion grains at ~25 mg each is about 460 billion tonnes, hundreds of years of world rice harvests.
