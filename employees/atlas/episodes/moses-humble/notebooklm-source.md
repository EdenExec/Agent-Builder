# NotebookLM source: Moses: the most humble man on earth (Exodus 3, Numbers 12)

Audio Overview customization: "Two hosts, Atlas (a warm, grandfatherly teacher) and Finn (a curious ten-year-old), talk to G, a ten-year-old boy in the room, about Moses and humility. Keep it fit for ten, faith-friendly, under 20 minutes, end with one sentence to remember."

## Teaching points

- **Prince, runaway, shepherd.** Exodus 1-2: Pharaoh's order, the basket in the reeds, Pharaoh's daughter, 'I drew him out of the water'; Acts 7:22 'learned in all the wisdom of the Egyptians... mighty in words'; Exodus 2:12 he killed an Egyptian in secret anger and fled to Midian; forty years with Jethro's sheep (Acts 7:30).
- **Who am I?.** Exodus 3: the bush not consumed; 'Who am I?'; 'Certainly I will be with thee'. Exodus 4:10-14: 'slow of speech' (like Ben, 'a bad speaker'); 'send someone else' kindles God's anger. Humble says 'I can't, but God can', and goes.
- **Humble in the hard places.** Exodus 18: listens to Jethro's advice and shares the load. Numbers 11:29: 'would God that all the LORD's people were prophets'. Numbers 12:1-13: Miriam and Aaron speak against him; 'very meek, above all the men... upon the face of the earth'; he prays 'Heal her now, O God'. Numbers 20: strikes the rock; Deuteronomy 34: sees the land from Nebo; 'face to face, as a man speaketh unto his friend' (Exodus 33:11); a prophet like me (Deuteronomy 18:15).

## Facts to keep straight

1. **Where did baby Moses float?** In a basket among the reeds of the Nile.
2. **Who adopted him?** Pharaoh's daughter.
3. **Why did he run away?** He killed an Egyptian in anger and Pharaoh wanted him dead.
4. **How long was he a shepherd?** Forty years.
5. **What did he say at the bush?** Who am I?
6. **What did God answer?** I will be with you.
7. **Why was 'send someone else' not humble?** It was saying no to God after God promised help.
8. **Who gave Moses advice?** Jethro, his father-in-law.
9. **What did he do when Miriam spoke against him?** Prayed for God to heal her.
10. **What does God call their friendship?** Face to face, as a man speaks to his friend.

## Try this

Ask Dad, Mom or Grandpa: what's one thing I could do better? Then do it, without arguing.
