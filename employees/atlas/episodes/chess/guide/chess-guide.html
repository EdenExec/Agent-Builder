Chess Field Guide

Meet the six pieces, then learn the rules with a picture for each one. Goes with the episode "Chess for beginners." Print it, cut out the cards, and quiz each other at the table.

Meet the army

The Kinga.k.a. The Boss
PointsThe game
On the board1 per side

Moves: One square, any direction.

Power: Castling: once a game he tucks into the corner behind a rook.

Watch out: Slow, and he can never step into check.

Raja in India, the Shah in Persia: "shah mat" became checkmate.

The Queena.k.a. The Powerhouse
Points9
On the board1 per side

Moves: Straight or diagonal, as far as she wants.

Power: Rook and bishop in one piece.

Watch out: Too valuable to lose cheap. Don't bring her out early.

Started as the weakest piece, one step diagonally. Spain made her strong in the 1400s.

The Rooka.k.a. The Bulldozer
Points5
On the board2 per side

Moves: Straight lines: forward, back, left, right.

Power: Owns open lines. Castling partner for the king.

Watch out: Stuck in the corner until the pawns move.

Ratha, the chariot, in India.

The Bishopa.k.a. The Long Shot
Points3
On the board2 per side

Moves: Diagonals, as far as it wants.

Power: Hits from across the whole board.

Watch out: Stays on one color forever.

Gaja, the elephant, in India.

The Knighta.k.a. The Jumper
Points3
On the board2 per side

Moves: An L: two squares one way, one to the side.

Power: The only piece that jumps. Always lands on the other color.

Watch out: Slow to cross the board.

Ashva, the horse, in India.

The Pawna.k.a. The Little Soldier
Points1
On the board8 per side

Moves: Forward one (two on its first move). Captures one square diagonally forward.

Power: Promotion: reach the far side and become a queen.

Watch out: Never goes backward.

Padati, the foot soldier, in India.
