# NotebookLM source: Jesus: the King who washed feet

Audio Overview customization: "Two hosts, Atlas (a warm, grandfatherly teacher) and Finn (a curious ten-year-old), talk to G, a ten-year-old boy in the room, about Jesus and humility. Keep it fit for ten, faith-friendly, under 20 minutes, end with one sentence to remember."

## Teaching points

- **The towel.** John 13:3-5: 'knowing that the Father had given all things into his hands' he rose from supper and washed the disciples' feet; Peter's refusal and reversal (13:8-9); 'I have given you an example' (13:12-15). Luke 22:24-26: at the same supper they argued who was greatest; the greatest as the youngest, the leader as one who serves.
- **The lowly King.** Matthew 18:1-4, the child in the middle; Luke 2:7, the manger; Matthew 21:5 and Zechariah 9:9, lowly on a donkey; Matthew 11:28-29, 'meek and lowly in heart'; Mark 10:45, not to be served but to serve and give his life a ransom.
- **Down, then up.** Luke 22:42, 'not my will, but thine'; Philippians 2:5-9, he made himself of no reputation, a servant, obedient to the cross, 'wherefore God also hath highly exalted him'; the resurrection; Luke 14:11, he that humbles himself shall be exalted.

## Facts to keep straight

1. **What job did Jesus do at the supper?** Washed his friends' feet, a servant's job.
2. **What did he know right before?** The Father had given all things into his hands (John 13:3).
3. **What did Peter say?** Never wash my feet; then, wash my hands and head too.
4. **What did the disciples argue about that night?** Who was the greatest (Luke 22).
5. **Who did Jesus stand in the middle?** A little child (Matthew 18).
6. **Where was he born?** In a manger, an animals' feeding box (Luke 2:7).
7. **What did he ride into Jerusalem?** A young donkey (Matthew 21, Zechariah 9).
8. **What is a ransom?** A price paid to set someone free (Mark 10:45).
9. **What did he pray in the garden?** Not my will, but yours (Luke 22:42).
10. **What happened after he humbled himself?** God raised him and gave him the name above every name (Philippians 2:9).
