## What to teach

1. **Printer and fixer.** Pennsylvania Gazette from 1729. Poor Richard's Almanack 1732 to 1758, up to 10,000 copies a year ("Early to bed and early to rise...", "Well done is better than well said"). A subscription library, the Union Fire Company, the Franklin stove (1742), which he refused to patent: "as we enjoy great advantages from the inventions of others, we should be glad of an opportunity to serve others by any invention of ours." Retired from running the shop at 42 (1748) to study electricity.
2. **The kite.** He named charges positive and negative and called linked Leyden jars a battery. June 1752, with his son William: a silk-handkerchief kite with a pointed wire, hemp string, a key, a silk ribbon to hold, standing under a shed. Lightning did not strike the kite; charge from the cloud came down the wet string, loose fibres stood up, a spark jumped to his knuckle, and he charged a Leyden jar. Dalibard in France drew sparks with an iron rod in May 1752 following Franklin's instructions. Georg Richmann was killed in 1753 trying something similar: never fly a kite in a storm.
3. **Gifts that last.** The lightning rod, bifocals, the glass armonica (Mozart and Beethoven wrote for it), the Gulf Stream chart, none patented. Chess: one of the first known chess players in the colonies; "The Morals of Chess" (1786): foresight, looking over the whole board, caution, and not being discouraged.

## Questions to end on

1. **What was Ben's almanac called?** Poor Richard's Almanack.
2. **Finish it: Early to bed and early to rise...** ...makes a man healthy, wealthy, and wise.
3. **What did Ben start so people could borrow books?** A subscription library.
4. **Why didn't he patent his stove?** We enjoy other people's inventions, so we should share ours freely.
5. **What two words did he use for electric charge?** Positive and negative.
6. **What was tied to the kite string?** A key, and a silk ribbon to hold.
7. **Did lightning strike the kite?** No. Charge came down the wet string.
8. **What did the lightning rod do?** Gave lightning a safe path to the ground.
9. **Name two more inventions.** Bifocals, the glass armonica, the Franklin stove.
10. **What's the rule about kites?** Never fly one in a thunderstorm.
