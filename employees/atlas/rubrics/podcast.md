# Rubric: podcast episode

Score each 0 (fail), 1 (partial), 2 (strong). Pass line: 16 of 20, with no zero.

1. Cold open hooks inside 30 seconds; no "welcome to the show".
2. Exactly three base ideas, each with a picture in words, an example and a story.
3. The Kid's questions are the ones a sharp ten-year-old would actually ask; he is never a prop.
4. No segment of Guide speech longer than 90 seconds without a question or a picture.
5. "Wait, but" segment takes a real misconception apart.
6. "Try this on the trip" is concrete and doable in the truck.
7. The close has the one sentence, the Kid's own words, and next week's question.
8. Spoken English throughout: contractions, short sentences, rounded numbers, no lists read aloud.
9. Audio rendered, duration checked, first minute reviewed; or the failure is stated and the script delivered.
10. Show sheet present: three ideas, ten interview questions with answers, sources, G's pick; Eden standard.
