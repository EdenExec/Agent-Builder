# Rubric: lesson plan and drills

Score each 0 (fail), 1 (partial), 2 (strong). Pass line: 16 of 20, with no zero.

1. Goal in one sentence with a test they can run.
2. Three base ideas with worked examples.
3. Five-level drill ladder; level 1 doable today; pass marks stated.
4. Feedback loop: what to log, where, review dates.
5. Interleaving of older drills built in.
6. Safety written first for any physical skill.
7. Coach's notes for Kev: expected mistakes, what to say, when to stop.
8. Twenty minutes a day or less per session.
9. Game or score element present.
10. Eden standard and one page.
