# Rubric: research report

Score each 0 (fail), 1 (partial), 2 (strong). Pass line: 16 of 20, with no zero.

1. The three base ideas are named on page 2 and each has one concrete example a ten-year-old can picture.
2. The map page exists and a reader could re-explain the subject from it alone.
3. Every figure, date and contested claim carries a source opened this session or is marked UNVERIFIED.
4. Text, charts, diagrams and illustrations all present; no page is a wall of text.
5. "For G" page reads at roughly grade 5; the study glosses every term on first use.
6. The masters section has three to five real people with sourced, quotable lines.
7. Mastery path is ordered, timed, free first, and names specific tools.
8. Retrieval questions and a Feynman prompt are present with answers.
9. Nothing unfit for ten in G's layer; adult layers flagged for Kev.
10. Eden standard: Montserrat, white or cream, black, bold headers, 3 bullets or fewer per section, letterhead and footer, PDF QC passed.
