# Curriculum map (Kev's brain dump, 6 October 2026, organised into tracks)

| Track | Subjects Kev named | First base-level ideas Atlas would start with |
|---|---|---|
| History | history in general | cause and effect; sources and how we know; the long arc (empires, faith, technology) |
| Science | science in general; survival-critical concepts for the decades ahead | energy, matter and life; the scientific method; water, food, shelter, power, first aid |
| Math | new ways to get better at math | number sense and estimation; patterns and proof; math as a language for the real world |
| Reading and writing | how to be better readers and writers | reading like a writer; the sentence; structure; reading aloud; writing every day |
| Social | sociology and psychology; social constructs | why people do what they do; groups and status; habits; persuasion and its ethics |
| Art and music | art, music, appreciation of the finer things | looking slowly; what makes a thing beautiful; the masters; taste as a learned skill |
| Character | how to become better men and gentlemen; how to lead like Jesus; mastering new concepts | service, courage, manners; servant leadership from the Gospels; the learning loop itself |
| Strength | how to get strong | progressive overload; sleep, food, consistency; safety first; the big lifts and bodyweight basics |
| Outdoors | what you are fishing for and better fly fishing; farming and building a ranch | the fish, the water, the bug; reading a river; casting mechanics; soil, water, animals, seasons; ranch systems |
| Firearms (knowledge) | what parts of guns are necessary for each use case | how a firearm works; the parts and what each is for; action types by use (hunting, sport, defence); safety and law; history. No manufacturing or modification instructions |
| Games | chess | the board as a system; tactics before strategy; endgames first; study with Lichess |
| Money | creating wealth and using it to help others; growing businesses; money as an elevator; the financial model of a business; real estate | income, expenses, assets, debt; compounding; a business as a machine (revenue, margin, cash); rent, leverage, risk; stewardship and generosity |
| AI | getting better at AI by building games | prompts as instructions; feedback loops; building, breaking, improving; what models are good and bad at |
