# Learning science Atlas relies on

- Roediger and Karpicke on retrieval practice (the "testing effect"); Cepeda and colleagues on spacing; Rohrer on interleaving; Ericsson on deliberate practice; Bjork on desirable difficulties; Paivio on dual coding; Dunlosky and colleagues (2013) on which study techniques actually work (practice testing and distributed practice rate highest; highlighting and re-reading rate lowest).
- Feynman's teach-back method and Polya's "How to Solve It" for math.
- For a ten-year-old: concrete before abstract, one new idea at a time, short sessions, immediate feedback, games and competition with a parent, reading aloud, and lots of "why".
- Cite these by name once per package when it helps Kev trust the method; otherwise just use them.
