# Trusted starting sources by track (open and cite; never quote from memory as fact)

- History: primary sources via Project Gutenberg, Internet Archive, national archives; Oxford and Cambridge short introductions; Dan Carlin for narrative hooks (check facts).
- Science: NASA, NOAA, USGS, CDC, university open courseware (MIT OCW, Khan Academy); textbooks by title.
- Math: Khan Academy, Art of Problem Solving, Polya; Desmos for pictures.
- Reading and writing: Strunk and White, Zinsser "On Writing Well", Mortimer Adler "How to Read a Book", Librivox and Gutenberg.
- Psychology and sociology: APA, peer-reviewed meta-analyses first; name the replication status of famous studies.
- Art and music: museum collections (Met, National Gallery, Rijksmuseum open access), Wikimedia Commons; Gombrich "The Story of Art"; Copland "What to Listen for in Music".
- Character and faith: the ESV Bible, the Gospels first; Ken Blanchard "Lead Like Jesus"; biographies of servant leaders.
- Strength: Starting Strength and bodyweight progressions; pediatric guidance for youth training (supervised, technique first, no maxing).
- Fly fishing and ranch: state fish and game departments (Idaho Fish and Game), USGS stream gauges, extension services (University of Idaho Extension), Orvis learning center, local fly shops.
- Firearms knowledge: NRA and state hunter-education safety material, manufacturers' manuals for parts and function, history from museum collections.
- Chess: Lichess study and practice, Silman "Endgame Course", Chernev.
- Money and business: SEC and Fed explainers, Buffett's letters, "The Richest Man in Babylon" for G, standard accounting primers; real estate from county assessor data and HUD; always mark figures with dates.
- AI: the Claude and Anthropic docs, Karpathy's videos, the family's own build logs.
