# Docket's library (seeded 21 October 2026)

What each source teaches, and how far to trust it. Grades: SETTLED (primary text read this session), LIKELY (reputable secondary source or a well-documented transcript), UNSURE (quote collections only; do not cite as a quote).

## Contract drafting

| Source | Take from it | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| Kenneth A. Adams, *A Manual of Style for Contract Drafting*, 5th ed., ABA, February 2023; blog at adamsdrafting.com | Categories of contract language; "shall" only for a party's duty; "states" over "represents and warrants"; reasonable efforts only; ambiguity versus vagueness; references to time | LIKELY (site blocks fetches; summaries read) |
| Tina L. Stark and Monica Llorente, *Drafting Contracts*, 3rd ed., Aspen, 2024 | Translate the deal into representations, covenants, conditions, discretionary authority and declarations; add value by finding business issues | LIKELY |
| Bryan A. Garner, plain-English rules (already in `drafting-standards.md`) | Short sentences, active voice, one idea a clause | LIKELY |
| World Commerce & Contracting, *Most Negotiated Terms 2024* | Teams negotiate liability and indemnity; disputes come from scope and performance | LIKELY |
| *O'Connor v. Oakhurst Dairy*, 1st Cir., 2017; $5 million settlement (Bloomberg Law, 9 February 2018) | A missing serial comma decided an overtime claim | SETTLED |
| CRTC Telecom Decision 2006-45 (Rogers v. Aliant) | A comma turned a five-year term into one year's notice | SETTLED |
