# Drafting standards (seeded 17 October 2026)

How Docket writes and redlines. Short, plain and testable beats long and clever.

## Style
- Active voice, short sentences, one idea per clause. "Eden shall invoice the Fee on the Start Date." not "The fee shall be invoiced by Eden following the occurrence of the Start Date." ("Shall" only for a party's obligation; see `skills/drafting-craft.md`, 21 October 2026.)
- Define a term once, in a definitions list or at first use, in bold, and use it the same way everywhere. Never use two words for one thing or one word for two.
- Numbers as figures with units and a source: "$30,000", "30 days after the Start Date", "9:00 a.m. Pacific". Dates unambiguous (17 October 2026). State business day rules.
- Lists for parallel items; tables for fees, dates and deliverables. Cross-refer by number, and check every cross-reference after edits.
- No "hereinafter", "witnesseth", "said", "such", "aforesaid", "and/or". Use "and" or "or" and say which.

## Redline conventions
- Show the clause number, the current wording, the proposed wording, one line of reason, and a rank: MUST, SHOULD or NICE.
- Never change what you were not asked to change without saying so. Keep a clean copy and a marked copy.
- When the other side's wording is fine, say so; pushback is a budget.

## Verdicts
SIGN: no must-fix issues. FIX FIRST: fixable issues, listed. DO NOT SIGN: the terms are unfair, illegal, unclear on money, or exposes Eden beyond what Kev accepts. NEEDS COUNSEL: outside lawyer required, with the three questions to ask.

## Ambiguity word list (rewrite on sight)
reasonable, appropriate, timely, promptly, as needed, etc., including but not limited to (when scope matters), support, best efforts, final, approved (by whom), complete (to whose standard), material, significant, soon, regularly, industry standard, cost-effective, user-friendly, and any number without a unit.

## Confidence grades for any point of law
SETTLED (statute or controlling decision read, dated), LIKELY (reputable secondary source, dated), CONTESTED (courts or states split), UNSURE (not verified). Anything UNSURE cannot drive a recommendation to sign or not to sign.
