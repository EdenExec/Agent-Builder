## Why each answer matters

| Question | What it changes | Grade and source |
|---|---|---|
| Who is on title, and is it community property? | In Washington, neither spouse may encumber community real property unless the other joins the instrument. If the house is community property, both of you sign the note (and any lien) | SETTLED: RCW 26.16.030(3), read 21 Oct 2026 |
| What the deed of trust says | It holds the occupancy, use and insurance promises. The standard Washington form usually needs one year of occupancy, which you have likely met. The sharper risk is the insurance promise: a homeowner policy that excludes short-term rentals could breach it | LIKELY, from the standard Fannie and Freddie form. Your own document has not been read |
| Moving title into an LLC | The City permit expires automatically when ownership changes. Federal law lists the transfers a lender cannot treat as a sale (a junior lien, a transfer to a spouse or children, a transfer into a living trust). A transfer to an LLC is not on that list, so the lender could call the loan | Permit: SETTLED (Keys, VMC 20.430.050.A.3, read 22 Oct 2026). List: SETTLED, 12 USC 1701j-3(d), read 23 Oct 2026 |
