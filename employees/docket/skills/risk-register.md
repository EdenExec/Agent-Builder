# The risk register

One living file, `projects/legal/risk-register.md`, owned by you and read by Radar. It is how Kev sees what could hurt Eden before it does.

## Each row

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ID | R-001, R-002, ... never reused |
| Risk | One sentence: what could happen |
| Where | The contract, process or entity it lives in |
| Likelihood | 1 to 5 |
| Severity | 1 to 5, by dollars, reputation and time lost |
| Score | Likelihood x severity |
| Dollars | Plausible exposure, with how you got it (gate any number) |
| Owner | Kev, Nick, Joel, Radar, Tally, or Docket |
| Next step | One action with a date |
| Counsel | NO, WATCH or YES (outside counsel needed) |
| Status | open, mitigated, accepted by Kev (date), closed |

## Scoring bands

16 to 25: act this week and tell Kev now. 9 to 15: plan this month. 4 to 8: watch. 1 to 3: record and move on. Severity 4 or 5, or any exposure over $25,000, is **counsel YES** by default.

## Standing watchlist (seed rows)

Client agreements with no written guarantee or fee trigger; slow-paying clients where the contract gives no remedy; 1099 contractors who look like employees; commission plan undefined or unsigned; investor notes unsigned or unreconciled; Boise lease and Idaho registration; auto-renewing software and notice dates; candidate data handling and confidentiality; entity naming mismatches between contracts, invoices and bank accounts (EES versus EPG); any advance or payment made outside the normal system.

## Rhythm

Review the whole register weekly before the lab review. Add a row the first time something worries you; closing needs a reason. Radar gets one line for any row that scores 16 or more or turns to counsel YES. A risk Kev accepts stays on the register marked accepted with the date, so the decision is visible.

## Output

The register file, a three-line summary for Radar, and the lab-review slide. No client-confidential text or personal data in the register; reference the document instead.
