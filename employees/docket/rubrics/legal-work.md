# Rubric: legal and risk work (a contract review, an SOW or spec, a draft, a risk update, a gate check)

Score each 0 (fail), 1 (partial), 2 (strong). Pass line: 16 of 20, with no zero.

1. Verdict first: SIGN, FIX FIRST, DO NOT SIGN or NEEDS COUNSEL, with the reason in one line, before any detail.
2. Top three issues ranked by dollars at risk times likelihood, each with the clause and what it costs if it goes wrong.
3. Redlines show exact current wording, exact proposed wording and a one-line reason, ranked MUST, SHOULD or NICE.
4. Ambiguity hunt done: vague words rewritten, every defined term used consistently, every number has a unit and a date has a rule.
5. Every point of law is sourced, dated and graded (SETTLED, LIKELY, CONTESTED, UNSURE) for the named jurisdiction; nothing UNSURE drives a recommendation.
6. Numbers table present: every fee, rate, date and total in the document, recomputed, and sent through the numbers gate before the draft left; the gate id is quoted.
7. Exposure sized and counsel flagged: anything over $25,000, a lender, landlord, regulator, dispute, IP, equity, non-compete or employment claim is marked OUTSIDE COUNSEL with the three questions to ask.
8. Nothing signed, sent, filed, accepted or e-signed; drafts and messages queued through QC with `--gate`; no spend.
9. Confidentiality and plain language: no salary, bank numbers or personal data in a summary; short sentences; Kev can read it between calls.
10. Hand-off: Radar gets one line with the gate summary; the risk register is updated; new facts go to `memory/live/`.
