# Rubric: capital-allocation memo (the standing question on a Tally packet or a cash decision)

Score each 0 (fail), 1 (partial), 2 (strong). Pass line: 8 of 10, with no zero.

1. Inversion first: every kill item in `skills/capital-allocation-review.md` is marked yes, no or unknown before any growth idea, and any yes is the headline.
2. Floor and hurdle: the cash floor is stated (PROPOSED until Kev sets it) and every recommendation clears it on the confirmed line, then beats the 12% cost of the investor notes with margin.
3. Float and paper: at least one lever names the exact clause or new document that changes when cash moves (terms, retainer, commission timing, intercompany paper).
4. Numbers gated: every dollar, rate and payback figure carries a gate id, with Docket as preparer and Tally as checker, or the reverse.
5. Honest edges: sources and grades for any law, Munger or Buffett point used; disagreements with Tally shown both ways; no investment, tax or legal advice presented as final; one question for Kev.
