# Jurisdiction pointers

These are WHERE to look, not rules. Never quote a number from this file. Open the live source, record the URL and date, and mark anything unconfirmed UNVERIFIED.

## Boise, Idaho
- City of Boise Planning and Development Services: zoning, design review, permits. Boise City Code, Title 11 (Unified Development Code). Confirm the current title and chapter numbers on the city site.
- Parcel and zoning lookup: City of Boise zoning map and Ada County Assessor parcel viewer.
- Overlays to check: foothills and hillside, wildland-urban interface, floodplain, historic districts, design review areas.
- Neighbouring cities (Eagle, Meridian, Garden City, Star, Kuna, Nampa, Caldwell) each have their own code. Never reuse Boise rules.
- Ada County Highway District (ACHD) controls most roads, driveways and street frontage requirements in Ada County.
- Utilities: Idaho Power, Intermountain Gas, Suez or local water, sewer provider. Confirm availability for the lot.
- Building code: Idaho adopts International Residential and Energy codes by edition. Confirm the adopted edition and amendments.
- HOA or covenants: request CC&Rs and architectural review rules.

## Washington State
- Rules are local: city or county planning department for the parcel.
- Building code: Washington State Building Code including the Washington State Energy Code. Confirm current edition.
- Growth Management Act urban growth areas affect lot size, utilities and septic.
- Critical areas ordinances: wetlands, steep slopes, geologic hazards, fish and wildlife. Shoreline Master Program near water.
- Wildfire: WUI code application varies. Confirm locally.
- Ask the jurisdiction about pre-application meetings.

## Always
- Survey and title commitment: easements, boundaries, encroachments.
- Geotechnical report for foundations.
- Licensed architect or engineer of record for permit documents where required.
