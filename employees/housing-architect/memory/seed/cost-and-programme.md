# Cost and programme sanity checks

All figures are UNVERIFIED planning heuristics. Market costs move fast. Replace with local builder quotes as soon as any exist, and never present these to the client as a quote.

- Always separate: land, site work and utilities, building shell, interior finishes, landscaping, furnishings and technology, permits and fees, design fees, contingency.
- Site work on foothills or rural lots can be a large and uncertain share. Slope, rock, retaining, long driveways, well and septic, power extension.
- Rule of thumb: finish level and complexity drive cost per square foot more than size. Compare a programme's area to budget as a range, and say how wide the range is.
- Contingency: carry a stated percentage and say what it covers.
- When the budget and programme disagree, offer three levers: reduce area, simplify form, reduce finish level. Show the effect of each qualitatively unless real numbers exist.
- Phasing options: shell now, finish later; build main house first, outbuildings later.
