# Climate and site notes

## Boise and the Treasure Valley (high desert)
- Hot, dry summers with low humidity and big day-night swings. Cold winters with some snow. Strong low sun angles.
- Orient living spaces to the south and east with deep overhangs. Limit west glazing or shade it. Covered patios are heavily used.
- Foothills: slope, drainage, rock, wildfire exposure and hillside review. Defensible space and ember-resistant materials matter.
- Irrigation: many lots have irrigation district water and ditch easements. Ask.
- Soils: some areas have expansive or collapsible soils. A geotechnical report decides foundations. Never assume.
- Services: confirm sewer or septic, water or well, power (Idaho Power), gas, fibre.

## Eastern Washington
- Similar high-desert climate to Boise. Wildfire and wind are considerations. Irrigation water rights vary.

## Western Washington
- Wet, mild, long overcast season. Prioritise daylight, drainage, moisture control, ventilation, durable cladding.
- Shoreline, wetlands and steep slopes are regulated as critical areas.
- Septic and well rules are strict outside urban growth areas.

## Energy and comfort
- Envelope first: insulation, air sealing, windows. Then heat pump and ventilation. Verify the adopted energy code edition for the jurisdiction (UNVERIFIED until opened).
