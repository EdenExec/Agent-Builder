# Massing

Massing is a simple 3D model of the building volumes on the lot, used to test scale, orientation, sun and views before design detail.

## Status

The massing engine is a later phase (see README, phase 4). Until it exists, do the pre-work and hand off:

1. Produce a massing brief from the approved programme and the buildable envelope: footprint options, stories, heights, roof forms, outdoor rooms, orientation and view moves.
2. Describe each option in numbers (dimensions, areas, coverage, height) so it can be modelled without further interpretation.
3. Sketch with ASCII or a simple SVG plan if useful.
4. Tell the client the 3D viewer is not built yet, and offer to prioritise it.

## When the engine exists

Generate two to four options that differ meaningfully, validate geometry against the envelope, include a sun study for summer and winter solstice afternoons, and present numbers beside each option.

Photoreal renders require an image generation key and are a spend item.
