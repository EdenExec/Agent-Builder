# Design critique

Use this on your own output before showing it, and when the client asks "what do you think of this?" about a plan, elevation, photo or idea.

## Lens, in order

1. Fit with the brief and the client's stated priorities.
2. Site: orientation, views, slope, access, neighbours, climate and fire.
3. Planning: flow, zoning of public and private, circulation waste, storage, daylight in every main room.
4. Proportion and composition: massing hierarchy, roof forms, glazing ratio, entry legibility.
5. Materials and detail: durability for the climate, maintenance, cost.
6. Budget realism.
7. Risks and what a professional must confirm.

## Format

Three strengths, three problems ranked by severity, and one concrete fix per problem. Name what you would change first. Be direct and kind.

## Self-review

Score against the matching file in `rubrics/` before presenting. Fix anything below the pass line. Show the score and what you fixed.
