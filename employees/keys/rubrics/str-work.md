# Rubric: short-term rental work (market read, rules checklist, insurance and HOA read, furnishing or turnover plan, listing draft, tracker or pro forma)

Score each 0 (fail), 1 (partial), 2 (strong). Pass line: 16 of 20, with no zero.

1. Result first: can we do it, what it earns, what it costs, and the three decisions for Kev, each with a pick, before any detail.
2. Sources: every rule, rate, tax and market number has a source link, the date read and a grade (SETTLED, LIKELY, CONTESTED, UNSURE); nothing UNSURE drives a recommendation.
3. Jurisdiction named: City of Vancouver or Clark County confirmed or marked as the open first check; state rules (RCW 64.37) and taxes covered.
4. Legal edges flagged for Docket: permits, licences, insurance wording, HOA covenants, the mortgage clause, manager and cleaner agreements, each as a review request with the question.
5. Market numbers honest: comps listed by link, median shown, asking versus booked made clear, low, base and high cases.
6. Tracker clean: `ledger.csv` columns in the fixed order, valid categories and statuses, sources on forecast rows, receipts and QC ids on actual rows, nothing deleted.
7. Numbers gate: the pro forma or rate card that drives a decision went through a gate with Tally as checker and the id is quoted.
8. Nothing booked, signed, listed publicly, bought or sent; every spend, send and publish queued with `npm run qc -- submit keys ...`; no vendor, HOA, insurer, city office or guest contacted.
9. Family privacy held: only the address and house facts; no children's names, schedules, Florida address, personal photos, account or ID numbers.
10. KDTW standard and hand-off: KDTW GROUP (`org: kdtw`), Montserrat, tables, three bullets or fewer per section, no emoji; Radar gets one line; the lab review carries two to three asks; new facts go to `memory/live/`.
