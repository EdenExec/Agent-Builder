# Listing drafts and pricing strategy

Listings are drafts until Kev approves a `publish` QC item. You write them in `projects/600-se-101st-str/listing/`; nothing goes onto VRBO or Airbnb by your hand.

## The listing draft (one per platform)

1. **Title** within the platform's character limit, leading with the guest's reason (space, location, a standout feature).
2. **Summary and description:** the space, sleeping arrangements, the neighbourhood by distance to PDX airport, downtown Vancouver, the waterfront, Portland and the Gorge (distances from a mapping source, rounded).
3. **Amenities** checked against the furnishing list; only what is truly there.
4. **House rules:** occupancy cap per the permit, quiet hours, parking, no parties or events, pets policy, smoking, check-in and check-out times, the posted local contact as RCW 64.37.030 requires.
5. **Photo plan:** shot list by room, daylight, no people, no family photos or personal items, no house number in frame.
6. **Permit and licence numbers** where the city requires them on the listing; blank and flagged until issued.
7. **Cancellation policy and minimum stay,** with the reason for the choice.

## Pricing strategy

- **Base rate by season** from the market research: a monthly table of nightly rate, weekend premium, minimum stay and cleaning fee.
- **Launch:** a modest discount for the first few bookings to earn reviews, with an end date.
- **Length of stay:** weekly and monthly discounts; a 30-plus-night mid-term option priced separately (different tax and permit picture).
- **Dynamic pricing tools** (PriceLabs, Wheelhouse, the platforms' own): compare cost and fit; a subscription is a QC spend item.
- **Floor and ceiling:** the lowest nightly rate that still clears cleaning and variable costs per stay, and a ceiling for big event dates.
- **Fees and taxes:** what the guest sees, what the platform takes (host-only fee versus split fee), and which taxes the platform collects.

Every price ties back to the pro forma in `str-tracker.md`. The go-live rate card goes through the numbers gate with Tally.

## Output

The two listing drafts, the photo shot list, the rate card by month, and the launch plan. Publishing either listing, creating a platform account or entering anything into one is a QC item (`publish` or `write_external`) with the exact text.
