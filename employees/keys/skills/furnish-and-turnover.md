# Furnishing, turnover, cleaning and local management

The family will be in Florida, so the house must run without them. This playbook turns the house into a guest-ready rental and lines up the people who keep it that way.

## Furnishing plan

1. **What stays, what goes.** With Kev, list what the family is taking, storing or leaving. Personal items, documents and family photos are never left in the rental; a locked owner's closet is an option to propose.
2. **Room-by-room list.** For each room: furniture, linens (three sets per bed), towels, kitchen kit, small appliances, safety items (RCW 64.37.030: smoke and CO detectors, fire extinguisher, posted exits and emergency numbers), smart lock, noise monitor (no indoor cameras), Wi-Fi, workspace. Columns: item, quantity, keep or buy, unit cost estimate with source, link. Estimates are (A) until quoted.
3. **Budget.** Low, base and high totals by room. The total is Kev's decision; every purchase is a QC spend item, grouped by order, with vendor and amount.
4. **Design.** A calm, durable, easy-to-clean look that photographs well. If Kev wants Marlowe's eye on the interior, ask through Radar.

## Turnover

- **Checklist** (one page, printable, also in the cleaner's app later): strip and remake beds, bathrooms, kitchen, floors, restock consumables, check damage and missing items with photos, reset thermostat and lock code, report within an hour.
- **Consumables par levels** and a restock rhythm.
- **Damage and lost-item process:** photo, platform claim window, who files.

## Cleaning and property management options

Compare three ways to run it, with costs as a share of revenue or per turnover:

| Model | Who does what | Typical cost basis |
|---|---|---|
| Full-service local property manager | Listing, pricing, guest messages, cleaning, maintenance | A percentage of booking revenue |
| Co-host plus cleaner | Co-host handles guests and on-site issues; cleaner per turnover | Co-host percentage plus a fee per clean |
| Self-managed from Florida | Kev or an agent handles messages and pricing; local cleaner and handyman | Fee per clean plus hourly repairs |

For each candidate company or person: name, service area, what is included, fees as published or UNVERIFIED, reviews, licence and insurance, contract term and exit. You research from public pages only. Asking for a quote or proposal is a `send_message` QC item with the exact text. Their agreements go to Docket before Kev signs.

## Output

The furnishing list with budget, the turnover checklist, the management comparison with your pick first, and the setup spend lines in the tracker as forecast rows.
