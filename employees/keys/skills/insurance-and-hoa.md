# Insurance and HOA checks

The question this answers: is the house covered and allowed to be rented by the night, and what does it cost to make it so.

## Insurance

1. **Read the current homeowner's policy** (Kev supplies it; Drive). Find the business-activity, rental and "residence premises" language. Most homeowner's policies exclude or limit cover once the owner moves out or rents by the night. Quote the clause; do not summarise from memory.
2. **The state minimum.** RCW 64.37.050: primary liability cover of at least $1,000,000 for the dwelling, or every stay booked through a platform that provides equal or greater primary liability cover. Platform host protections (Airbnb AirCover, VRBO's liability programme) are not the same as a policy; record what each says and when it applies.
3. **Options to compare.** A landlord or short-term rental policy (dwelling, contents, liability, loss of rental income), an endorsement on the current policy, or a specialist STR insurer. For each: what it covers, limits, deductible, exclusions (damage by guests, pets, hot tubs, pools), annual premium, and whether it is primary.
4. **The mortgage.** Lenders usually require the right insurance and may restrict a change of use. The deed of trust clause goes to Docket.
5. **Vacancy and absence.** With the family in Florida, check vacancy clauses, winterisation requirements and the local contact the insurer expects.

Getting a quote means contacting an agent or insurer: that is a `send_message` QC item with the exact request text. You never bind, buy or cancel a policy.

## HOA and covenants

1. Find out whether the property is in an HOA or carries recorded covenants (CC&Rs): Kev first, then the title report and the Clark County recorder.
2. If there is one, read the CC&Rs, bylaws and rules for leasing limits, minimum lease terms, rental registration, signage, parking, trash and noise rules, and fines. Note the amendment process: an HOA can change the rules later.
3. Any restriction or ambiguity goes to Docket with the clause quoted. Never contact the HOA without a QC-approved message.

## Output

A one-page cover and permission read: result first (covered or not, allowed or not), a comparison table of insurance options with premiums as quoted or UNVERIFIED, the HOA finding with clauses quoted, the questions for Docket, and the premium lines added to the tracker as forecast rows.
