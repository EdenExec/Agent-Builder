# Market starting map (seeded 19 October 2026)

Where demand for an east Vancouver whole-home rental is likely to come from, and where to look for numbers. No figures are seeded on purpose: every rate and occupancy number is gathered fresh, sourced and dated.

## Likely demand drivers (verify each)

| Driver | Why it might matter for 98664 | How to check |
|---|---|---|
| Visiting family and friends | Large family groups prefer a house to hotel rooms | Comp reviews mentioning reunions, holidays |
| Medical and travel-nurse stays | PeaceHealth Southwest Medical Center (Vancouver) and Legacy Salmon Creek; 30-plus-night demand | Furnished Finder and mid-term listings nearby |
| Airport and business travel | Portland International Airport (PDX) is across the Columbia River, a short drive | Distance from a mapping source; comps marketing PDX proximity |
| Outdoor weekends | Columbia River Gorge, Mount Hood, Mount St. Helens; summer peak | Seasonal calendar sampling |
| Portland events | Concerts, sports, conventions, holidays | Event calendars against comp pricing spikes |
| Washington-side taxes | No state income tax draws some movers; relocating families need a furnished base | Mid-term listings, relocation demand |

## Where to look

- Free: public VRBO and Airbnb search results and calendars; county assessor for the subject's facts; Visit Vancouver USA and Travel Portland for events and visitor data; long-term rent comparables on major rental listing sites for the lease alternative.
- Paid (QC spend item first): AirDNA market and rentalizer reports, PriceLabs market dashboard, Rabbu.

## Seasonality to test

Pacific Northwest whole-home rentals often peak in summer (June to September) and holidays, and soften from November to February with rain. Treat this as a hypothesis to test against calendars, not a fact.
