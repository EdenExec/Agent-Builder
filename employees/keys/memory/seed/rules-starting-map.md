# Rules starting map (seeded 19 October 2026)

A map of where to look, not a source. Each line was found in a quick search on 19 October 2026; the official pages could not be opened that day, so nothing here is SETTLED. Verify every line against the adopted code, statute or agency page before relying on it.

| # | Rule | Where it lives | What the search said | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | City of Vancouver regulates short-term rentals | VMC Chapter 20.835, adopted by Ordinance M-4433 (December 2023); program announced January 2024 | STRs allowed as a limited use in legally established dwelling units; before this, residential-zone STRs were only allowed as bed and breakfasts (VMC 20.830) | LIKELY |
| 2 | City STR permit and business licence | City of Vancouver business and special licences; Washington Business Licensing Service | A short-term rental permit plus a city business licence; one-time permit fee reported as $250 by the city in January 2024 and $265 by a third-party guide (conflict: confirm) | UNSURE |
| 3 | Neighbour notice | VMC 20.835 | A courtesy notice to abutting owners before applying (third-party guide) | UNSURE |
| 4 | Owner occupancy | VMC 20.835 | A third-party guide says there is no owner-occupancy requirement; not confirmed in the adopted text. Critical for this house, since the family will live in Florida | UNSURE |
| 5 | Operating limits | VMC 20.835 and the draft | No STR in an accessory structure, RV, outdoors or an MFTE building; city may inspect (VMC 17.08); permit ends on change of ownership; nuisance limits at the property line | LIKELY (draft) |
| 6 | State STR law | RCW 64.37 (2019, HB 1798): .010 definitions, .020 taxes, .030 consumer safety, .040 platforms, .050 liability insurance | Operators remit applicable taxes unless the platform does; give guests contact information during the stay; safety requirements | LIKELY |
| 7 | Liability insurance | RCW 64.37.050 | Primary liability cover of at least $1,000,000 for the unit, or book every stay through a platform providing equal or greater primary cover | LIKELY |
| 8 | Lodging and sales tax | Washington Department of Revenue, "Lodging information and rates" (location-code table) | Stays under 30 nights are lodging: retail sales tax at the Vancouver location rate plus any special hotel and motel tax; transient rental income reporting. One undated article put Vancouver's lodging tax at 4%. | UNSURE |
| 9 | Platform collection | Airbnb and VRBO Washington tax pages; DOR marketplace facilitator rules | Platforms may collect and remit some or all Washington taxes; what remains for direct bookings must be confirmed | UNSURE |
| 10 | Jurisdiction | Clark County property information and GIS | Whether 600 SE 101st Ave is inside city limits decides whether rows 1 to 5 apply or Clark County code does | UNSURE: first check |
