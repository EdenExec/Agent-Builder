# Calling for the PB South Vertical Transportation Specialist

**Who we want:** the owner-side elevator expert for a very large Miami high-rise. Either a **VT consultant/engineer** (WSP, Lerch Bates, Syska, VDA, JH, Persohn/Hahn, Edgett Williams) or an **OEM construction PM/manager** (Otis, KONE, Schindler, TK Elevator). New-install high-rise, 30+ floors. Relocation to Miami is open.

**Stop signs (end the call politely):** service or maintenance only; sales only; no new-install high-rise; a GC PM with no technical elevator depth.

**Ask every candidate**

| # | Question | Listen for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Which high-rise elevator projects have you run? | Floors, cars, speeds, role |
| 2 | Owner, consultant or OEM side? What did you personally own? | Design, submittals, install or testing |
| 3 | How do you sequence installation on a 30+ floor tower? | Hoistway turnover, construction elevators |
| 4 | Which codes, any QEI, which inspectors, any in Florida? | A17.1 / B44, A17.2, AHJ closeouts |
| 5 | A time the elevator package threatened the schedule? | Specific action and result |
| 6 | Change orders or backcharges with the elevator sub? | Protected the owner's money |
| 7 | In South Florida? If not, can you relocate, and when? | Real willingness and a date |
| 8 | What do you earn now, and what makes a move worth it? | Internal only; do not write pay in the sheet, Hub or any client note |

**Pitch in two lines:** elevators are the whole job here, not a side job, on a Miami tower from a builder running some of the biggest in the market. An owner-side voice with authority to get the plan right from the start.

**Rules:** never call Related staff (PB South's partner on Project X). Do not name PB South to a lead beyond what your recruiter approved. Record name, role, status and the answers to 1 to 7 only.

**Hand back:** after each call, post the result to the recruiter who owns this JO (Kev confirms the owner; see Casey's owner proposal). Anyone who meets Must Dos 1 to 4 on the call is a "call me today" to the owner.
