# Rubric: board work (a voice-note intake, a board pass, a Scout brief, a client review, a follow-up draft)

Score each 0 (fail), 1 (partial), 2 (strong). Pass line: 16 of 20, with no zero.

1. Result first: what changed on the board (counts by new, updated, on hold, killed, filled), then what Kev must answer, before any detail.
2. Nothing dropped: every line of the voice note or call is a row change, a depth move, a decision, an ask, a Scout request or an "I couldn't place this" with Kev's words.
3. Names checked: every company and person matched against the Hub and the seed; sound-alikes corrected with "to confirm" and asked once in the read-back; nothing guessed silently.
4. Rows right: correct status words (active, on-hold, filled, closed, killed), board_note in Kev's words, condition for holds, depth links to real seats, source and date on every change; one lead per JO and no recruiter assigned that Kev did not name.
5. Written safely: read before write, one batch, `if_version` on every existing document, failed pins re-read and redone, no deletes, a dated record file in `projects/team-hub/`.
6. Scout briefed: every new active JO without leads queued and briefed the same working day with the JO, Kev's words, the seat and who is already on it; none for on-hold, killed, unsigned or discreet-by-name; Moss as referral asks only; the hand-off is a ticket on Scout's feed.
7. Flags honest: no owner, no leads, quiet 7 days, 14-day rotate (standby excluded), stale 14 days and waiting on fee agreement, each from this pass's data, in one short post; no owner, rank or status changed because a flag fired.
8. Hard rules held: nothing sent, booked, spent or published; client follow-ups are drafts for Kev with Jenny owning tone; Kev's Done never treated as approval; nothing on the Sabbath (Friday sunset to Saturday sunset).
9. Confidential held: Baker and the INL work only as "Client · discreet"; no cross-client names in anything a client sees; names, role and status only; no pay, phones, emails, addresses or enrichment.
10. Eden standard and hand-off: tables, three bullets or fewer per section, no emoji; Radar gets one line plus proposed ledger rows; `now.json` updated; confirmed name corrections go to `memory/live/`.
