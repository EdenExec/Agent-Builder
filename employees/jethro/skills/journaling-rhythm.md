# Journaling rhythm

> **Lane note: Radar owns the 5:30 brief.** The journaling prompt is the first line of Radar's 5:30am brief (`employees/radar/skills/morning-brief.md`, step 3) and Radar has the final word on it. You supply the prompts for the week; Radar picks or adjusts them to the day's chapter and yesterday's check-out. You do not send anything to Kev in the morning yourself.

Kev's day opens: 5:30 brief and journaling, pray 5:45, office by 6. Journal first, paper after. Your job is to make that first five minutes easy to start and worth doing.

## The weekly prompt set (due Friday by 1:50pm)

Six prompts, Sunday to Friday. The Sabbath runs Friday sunset to Saturday sunset: no prompt, no brief, no check inside it.

Each prompt is one sentence and ties to something real in that week:

| Source | Example prompt |
|---|---|
| The Scripture chapter (ESV, book order, Kev is in Romans) | "Romans 12:2: where is one place I am being conformed instead of transformed this week?" |
| A C12 Forum change in progress | "What made handing off the invoice review harder than I expected?" |
| A coaching session that day or the day before | "What do I want Micah to help me see today?" |
| The book on the reading plan | "Which idea from chapter 4 did I actually use yesterday?" |
| The balance wheel spoke lowest last month | "What would one more point on Rest & Retreat look like this week?" |
| Gratitude and the Seven Pillars | "Where did I see Growth in someone on the team yesterday? Give thanks for it." |

Hand the six to Radar as a plain list with the day each belongs to. Prompts carry no private content from past entries: refer to a theme, never quote what Kev wrote.

## The weekly check (Friday, count only)

- Look at whether the Eden Journal (Notion data source `collection://61f6bc1d-5d84-43c3-b946-5bf602a35897`, or wherever Kev confirms his morning journaling lives, (A) to confirm) has entries this week. Count days with an entry, Sunday to Friday. Read dates, not content.
- Until you have Notion read access, ask Radar for the count.
- Report it once a week, on the lab-review deck and only as a plain number ("journal days: 4 of 6"). No colour coding, no streak, no comparison with other weeks unless Kev asks.

## No guilt mechanics

- No streaks, badges, red marks, "you missed" lines or catch-up prompts.
- No pushes, emails or reminders beyond the prompt already in the brief.
- A quiet week gets a shorter, easier prompt the next week ("One line: what am I thankful for this morning?"), not a nudge.
- If Kev says he journals on paper or elsewhere, take his word; the count is his to keep or drop.
- Never on Saturday. Sunday prompts are lighter and tied to worship at Main Street.

## Privacy

Journal content is Kev's. You read it only when he asks you to, or to find the commitments in a coaching or Forum entry. You never summarise it for anyone, never quote it in a ledger row, a slide, the repo or a message, and never let another agent read it.
