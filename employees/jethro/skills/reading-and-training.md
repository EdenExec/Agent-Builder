# Reading and training (one book at a time, into practice)

"Leaders are Readers. Learners are Earners." (Eden-Core-Values.) Kev, 21 October 2026: "reading and training for C12 growth development (putting C12 concepts into practice)." The goal is not pages read; it is one thing done differently each week because of what he read.

## The plan

One book at a time, never a stack. The plan lives in `projects/growth/reading-plan.md` (the repo may hold titles, dates and pace; Kev's own notes and reflections stay in his Notion or Drive).

| Column | Rule |
|---|---|
| Book | Title and author |
| Why now | The C12 Forum change, balance-wheel spoke or alignment area it serves |
| Pace | Chapters per week that fit his week (A: 20 to 30 minutes a day, after Scripture, never in place of it) |
| Start, target finish | Dates |
| Practice line | One note-to-practice per week (below) |
| Status | Reading, finished, dropped (dropping is fine; say why in one line) |

## Choosing the next book

1. Start from need, not a list: the alignment area most Behind, a Forum counsel line, a coaching theme.
2. Prefer what C12 itself uses or recommends, and proven leadership books Kev's peers would recognise. Give one pick and one alternative, each with two lines on why it fits now and how long it takes.
3. If Kev already owns or is reading something, that wins.
4. Buying the book (print, Kindle, Audible) is a `spend` QC item with the vendor and amount: `npm run qc -- submit jethro spend "Buy <title> (Kindle)" "<link, format, why now>" --amount <n>`. Never buy.

## Note to practice (weekly, Friday)

For the chapters read that week, one line each:

| Line | |
|---|---|
| The idea | One sentence, in Kev's words if he wrote any |
| Where it applies | A specific place in Eden or at home this coming week |
| The rep | One action with a day ("Wednesday Headhunter Training: open with the five-minute review the book suggests") |

The rep becomes a proposed ledger row handed to Radar (format in `c12-follow-through.md`, Source "Reading <title> · Jethro"). If a rep is for the team (a training change), Dean prepares the material; Kev decides.

## Training beyond books

C12 workshops, talks and podcasts Kev mentions follow the same rule: one idea, one place, one rep. Registration or fees are `spend` QC items. Atlas (`employees/atlas/`) teaches subjects to Kev and Garnet; if Kev wants a book explained or a study episode made, that request goes to Atlas through Radar.

## Gentle by design

No pages-per-day quota, no streak, no catch-up guilt. A missed week gets a smaller pace, not a lecture. The Sabbath is rest: no reading check-ins from Friday sunset to Saturday sunset.
