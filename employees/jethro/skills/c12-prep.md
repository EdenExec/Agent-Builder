# C12 prep (show up sharp)

The C12 Forum meets the third Tuesday of each month. Kev walks in with his balance wheel scored, his 5-point alignment marked, his wins and prayer requests ready, and the one issue he wants the group's counsel on. The framework is in `memory/seed/c12.md`.

## Dates and the 48-hour rule

| Forum | Prep pack ready (48h before) | Built by (Sabbath kept) |
|---|---|---|
| Tue 17 Nov 2026 | Sun 15 Nov, in the Sunday Edition | Fri 13 Nov, 1:50pm, with the lab review |
| Tue 15 Dec 2026 | Sun 13 Dec | Fri 11 Dec |
| Tue 19 Jan 2027 | Sun 17 Jan | Fri 15 Jan |

Forty-eight hours before a Tuesday is Sunday, and Saturday is the Sabbath, so the pack is always built by Friday and Radar files it for Sunday morning. Add a row for each new month the same way.

**Location flag.** The Forum has met in Vancouver, Washington. Kev moves to Boise around Friday 6 November 2026, so the 17 November Forum may be a drive or flight back, a virtual seat, or a transfer to a Boise-area C12 group. Until Kev decides, every prep pack and every lab review carries this as an open question, and any travel (flight, hotel, mileage) is a `spend` QC item. After the move, Kev's local time is Mountain (America/Boise); state the time zone on every time you write.

## The pack (one private page, KDTW Group)

| Section | What goes in | Who fills it |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The one issue | One question Kev wants the group's counsel on, with your recommendation of which issue and two lines of context | You propose, Kev picks |
| 2. Balance wheel | The eight spokes with last month's scores (if Kev recorded them) and blank boxes for this month: Walk with God, Discipling Others, Marriage & Family, Personal Finances, Biblical Community, Fun & Recreation, Fitness & Nutrition, Rest & Retreat | Kev scores; you never score for him |
| 3. 5-point alignment | Revenue Generation, Operations Management, Organizational Development, Financial Management, Ministry, each Behind / On / Ahead, with one fact per line from this month's lab reviews and ledger | You draft the facts, Kev marks |
| 4. Wins | Three wins since the last Forum: Forum changes done (from `c12-follow-through.md` 30-day checks), coaching commitments kept, ledger rows closed, Eden numbers from the lab reviews | You draft, Kev edits |
| 5. Prayer requests and praise | Blank lines, plus a reminder: "How can I pray for and serve my peers?" | Kev only |
| 6. Last month's changes | Each change from the last Forum: done, in progress, dropped | You, from the check-backs |
| 7. Logistics | Date, start time and time zone, location or link, travel note | You, from the calendar facts Radar gives you |

## Steps

1. By Wednesday of the week before the Forum, ask Radar for: the calendar entry, last month's pack (from Kev's private Drive folder), the open and closed ledger rows with Source "C12 Forum" or "coaching", and the latest lab-review status lines across the roster.
2. Draft sections 1, 3, 4, 6 and 7. Leave 2 and 5 for Kev, with last month's numbers beside the boxes only if Kev wrote them down.
3. Pick the one issue. Candidates: a Forum change that failed twice, the alignment area most Behind, a decision Kev has deferred for weeks on the ledger. Give your pick first and one alternative.
4. Hand the draft to Radar by Friday 1:50pm for Kev's private Drive folder and a one-line pointer in the Sunday Edition ("C12 Tuesday: pack ready, 2 boxes to fill, about 10 minutes"). The page itself never appears in the Edition, the Eden Desk or any work surface.
5. Monday evening: if the pack still has blank boxes, one line in Radar's check-out batch, nothing more.

## Never

- Score the balance wheel or write a prayer request for Kev.
- Put scores, prayer requests or the one issue in the repo, a ledger row, a lab-review slide or a message to anyone.
- Book travel, register for an event or message the Forum chair. Those are QC items.
