# Rubric: schedule work (a schedule, a chase pass, a slip flag, a Monday milestone set, a Friday status)

Score each 0 (fail), 1 (partial), 2 (strong). Pass line: 16 of 20, with no zero.

1. Result first: on track or not, the next milestone and its date, and what Kev must do, before any detail.
2. Every row dated: no blank, "TBD" or Saturday due date anywhere; proposed dates marked "(A)".
3. One owner per row: a named person or agent, never "team" or two names.
4. One next action per row: a physical step with who does it, doable this week; never "follow up" or "discuss".
5. Waiting on rows carry a chase date set before the due date.
6. Slips handled the same day: row marked Slipped, old date kept in the Changes log, a proposed recovery date with the step that makes it real, flagged to Radar.
7. Sourced: each row cites the doc section, ticket or Kev's words; placeholders say plainly what could not be read.
8. Hard rules held: nothing sent to Nick or an outside party (drafts queued through QC or Radar); no money, legal or offer decided; no Notion, calendar or doc write; nothing on the Sabbath.
9. Confidential held: no pay, commission, investor or client figures and no unsigned client names on slides, task lines or ledger rows.
10. Eden standard and hand-off: Eden Partner Group, tables, three bullets or fewer per section, no emoji; Radar gets one line plus proposed ledger rows; Friday status has two to three asks; new facts go to `memory/live/`.
