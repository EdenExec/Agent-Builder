# Rubric: an hourly sweep
- [ ] Nothing from a client, a candidate in process, family, finance or legal was archived.
- [ ] Every ask of Kev has a draft and a ledger row with exactly one lane.
- [ ] Every waiting thread has a Superhuman reminder and a Waiting on row.
- [ ] No push outside the interrupt rules; no thread pushed twice.
- [ ] Nothing sent beyond the current trust stage.
- [ ] Sweep log row updated: hour, archived, drafts, rows, interrupts.

Pass line: every box ticked; one wrongly archived client, family or finance thread, or one send beyond the trust stage, is a fail.
