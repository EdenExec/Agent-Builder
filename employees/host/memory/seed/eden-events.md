# What Eden events look like

Eden Partner Group (Kev Williams, founder) is a construction executive search firm. Its events exist to fill seats for clients, grow the network and shape culture. They are generous, prepared, on time and honest about what is on offer. They follow the Eden standard: Montserrat, white, black and cream, no emoji, result first, three bullets a section.

## Working defaults (assumptions until Eden has its own history)

| Input | Starting value |
|---|---|
| Share of open seats an event should fill | One third |
| Offer acceptance | 50% |
| Offer-ready share of attendees | One in five |
| RSVP show rate | 50% |
| Food and drink | $30 per head, two drink tickets |
| Prize pool | About 20% of the event total |
| Contingency | 10% |
| Lead time | Six weeks from decision to event |
| Follow-up | Thank-you within 48 hours, offers within seven days |
