# Run-of-show

The run-of-show is the day, minute by minute, with one owner per line. It fits one page and is printed for every helper. Build it after the venue is approved (`skills/plan-an-event.md`, step 6).

## Shape

| Time | What happens | Owner | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-1 day | Confirm headcount, parking and directions with the venue; final reminder text; pack the kit | Named helper | Approved QC sends only |
| T-90 min | Venue walk-through, tables set by station, signage up, music low | Event lead | Floor plan |
| T-45 min | Staff briefing: roles, the pitch, the raffle rule, who answers what | Event lead | Role sheet |
| T-0 | Doors; check-in (find the name, issue tag and ticket, note the referrer) | Check-in owner | RSVP list, tags, stubs |
| T+30 | Welcome from the host and one culture voice, two minutes each, no slides | Host speaker | A three-line script |
| T+45 | Floor opens: stations by site or role, depth charts on tables, food and drink flowing | Recruiters, client leads | Depth charts printed |
| T+105 | Raffle: count, draw, announce, photo of winners | Raffle owner | Bowl, stubs, prize table |
| T+135 | Close: thanks, what happens next and when, the 48-hour promise | Host speaker | Next-steps line |
| T+150 | Lights up; reconcile tickets and the sign-in sheet; collect names | Check-in owner | Sheet |
| T+180 | Debrief ten minutes: three things that worked, three to fix | Event lead | Notes into `events/<slug>/debrief.md` |

## Rules

- Every line has exactly one owner by name or role. A line with two owners has none.
- The floor opens within 45 minutes of doors; guests who wait are guests who leave.
- Speeches total under ten minutes. Culture voices say something true, in their own words; Host drafts a prompt, not a script.
- Keep a bad-weather and a low-turnout plan: how the room is rearranged if fewer than half RSVPs show (collapse stations, bring the raffle forward), and what is said at the door.
- Photos only with consent; no candidate's private detail goes on a public post.
- Safety: first aid and exits known, an alcohol limit enforced by the venue, a named person who ends the evening.

## Output

`events/<slug>/run-of-show.md` (the table with real times and names), one printed page, plus the check-in sheet layout (columns: name, RSVP yes or walk-in, referrer, tickets issued, stage in Crelate).
