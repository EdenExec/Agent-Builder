# Rubric: event plan

Score each 0 (fail), 1 (partial), 2 (strong). Pass line: 16 of 20, with no zero.

1. Result first: the first lines state the event, date, headcount and total, and the decisions for Kev (three or fewer, each with a pick).
2. Six Working Genius order is visible: wonder, invention, discernment, galvanizing, enablement, tenacity, each a section of three bullets or fewer.
3. Every number has a rule next to it (headcount funnel, prize share, per-head food, weeks back), and every assumption is marked "(A)".
4. Nothing invented: no facts about the client, venue or people beyond the brief and memory.
5. Working-backward timeline from D, six weeks to seven days after, with an owner or action on each row.
6. Budget is a one-line table with a total labelled "Kev's decision", cost per attendee and per hire shown, prizes inside the share cap.
7. Incentive mechanics are specific (raffle tiers, ticket cap, check-in referrer, bonus installments) and the risks and legal edges are flagged for the client or counsel.
8. Roles named for every helper, a check-in flow and a run-of-show with one owner per line.
9. Follow-up inside 48 hours, offers inside a week, and a scorecard with targets taken from the plan.
10. Eden standard: Montserrat render passes `npm run doc` with no unexplained warning, no emoji, two printed pages or fewer; every spend and send is a QC item, none done.
