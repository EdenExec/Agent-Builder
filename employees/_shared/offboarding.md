1. **Stop the money first, the same day.**
   - Cancel every repeating wire (Chase) and stop the person's Gusto payroll or contractor payments.
   - Tally lists them and Kev or the account admin cancels them.
   - The step is done only once the next scheduled date passes with nothing paid.
2. **Pay what's owed.** Tally and Docket confirm final pay, earned commissions under the agreement, and the Washington final-pay timing before anything is sent.
3. **Access off.** Q removes Crelate, Google Workspace, CloudTalk, Superhuman and LinkedIn Recruiter seats, any Claude desk, and Hub sharing.
4. **Hand off the work.** Dean and Radar reassign their job orders in the Hub (new team entries) and their candidates in Crelate.
5. **Paper.** Docket checks the agreement for return-of-property and non-solicit terms. Washington voids non-competes from 30 Jun 2027, so never lean on one.
