# MPCs to market (Monday picks)

Done means Kev opens one page on Monday morning and sees 3 to 5 MPCs worth marketing this week, why each one places, who to take them to, and a blurb he can edit and send himself.

## When

Monday, after the 7:15am read pass (routine `routines/mpc-to-market.md`). If Monday is a holiday Kev has marked, Tuesday. Never on the Sabbath.

## Picking

From today's Warm inventory list, the people tagged `MPC` who are:

- not in process (Casey's lane),
- not tagged `Do-not-market`,
- not employed by a current client (`memory/seed/cadence-and-lanes.md`),
- touched in the last 30 days, so we know they are still open. A strong MPC past 30 days is not picked: it gets a touch ask first and can be picked next Monday.

Rank by, in order: a seat they fit at a client type Eden is hiring into now (Team Hub `jobs`, read-only), a screen in the last 60 days, a market with a Scout map, and how long since they were last marketed (`memory/live/marketed.md`). Pick 3 to 5. Fewer than 3 qualify: say so and say why (most often: MPCs not touched in 30 days).

An MPC who fits an active JO is not a market pick: that is a submittal question. One line to Casey through Radar ("<Name> fits jobs/<id>: owner to look?"), and pick someone else.

## For each pick

| Part | What goes in it | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Name, title, current company (internal page only), owner recruiter, last touch | Today's list |
| Why they place | Two or three lines of fact: the title tag, years in the role, project types and sizes, sector, what the screen said if readable, relocation if stated | Crelate (read-only), the screen, the owner's ask reply |
| Target client types | Company types from Scout's map buckets (GC, Mechanical, Electrical, Drywall, Concrete, Civil, Owner Builder, CM, Owner's Rep) for the metro | `employees/scout/maps/`, `employees/scout/skills/market-map.md` |
| Target companies | Three to six names from the map's "where they sit" and "who is moving" for that metro, with the reason in a few words | Scout's map; until a map exists, Scout's sheets in `employees/scout/sheets/` and the client list |
| Blurb | The draft below | You |

- **Never a target:** the candidate's own employer or its affiliates; a discreet client by name (Baker is "Client · discreet" in this file; Kev names it himself if he wants it); a company Kev has said not to market to.
- **Existing clients are fair targets** (Kev markets to clients), marked "client" in the table.
- **No map for that metro:** say so, use the sheets, and file a one-line request to Scout through Radar ("Market map for <metro>: an MPC to place there").
- If you cannot source "why they place", ask the owner (one ask, within the cap): "Two lines on why <Name> places, for Kev's MPC picks Monday." Never invent experience, dates, titles or certifications: "confirm with candidate".

## The blurb (draft for Kev's review)

80 to 120 words, Eden Partner Group voice, plain and specific. Jenny (`host`) owns the final tone; Kev sends.

- Opens with the title and the one thing that makes them placeable ("Ground-up high-rise superintendent, 15 years, last three towers over 30 floors in South Florida").
- Then: project types and sizes, sector, the metro and relocation, availability ("open to a conversation; notice period to confirm").
- **Never in a blurb:** the candidate's name, their current employer, comp or pay expectations, age, family, health, contact details, or any client's name. A blurb that would let a reader identify the person is too specific: widen it.
- Ends with the line: "Review before it goes to the client: read every field, pay line and name."

## The page

File: `employees/ember/drafts/mpc-picks/<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`, internal. Result line first ("4 MPCs to market this week: 2 supers Miami, 1 PM Boise, 1 MEP PX DC; 1 more held for a touch"). One section per pick with the table and the blurb. Then "Held" (who almost made it and why) and "Asks posted" for any why-they-place asks.

Then append each pick to `memory/live/marketed.md` (date, name, tag, targets proposed). Nothing is marked marketed until Kev says he sent it.

## Hand-off

Your final message carries the result line and the file path, so Radar's morning sweep puts "MPC picks: yes or no per name" on Kev's list. Kev's yes on a pick is not a send: Kev sends himself, or tells Radar and Jenny to draft it to a named person, and that becomes a `send_message` QC item for him to approve. You never submit a send on your own.

## Rules

- Never send, post, share or publish a blurb or the picks page. Never put a blurb on the Team Hub or a client surface.
- Do-not-market wins over every other tag.
- No comp anywhere on the page, even internal: comp lives in Crelate, and Kev looks it up himself.
