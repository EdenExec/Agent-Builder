# Rubric: finance work (a close, a budget vs actual, a forecast)

Score each 0, 1, 2. Pass line 16 of 20, no zero.

1. Result first: three lines on top say where Eden stands vs budget and where cash will be.
2. Books status is stated first: closed or not, and what is open.
3. Basis is explicit: accrual for the P&L, cash for cash; never mixed in one column.
4. Variance column present, favourable or unfavourable named, 10% line applied.
5. Proof is real: the two-totals check, Sales by Customer tie-out and basis check pasted, and a cleared numbers gate (Docket re-derived every figure) quoted by id.
6. Every forecast row has a source and a confidence label; no unsourced row.
7. Read-only: nothing written to QuickBooks, Gusto or a shared sheet without an approved QC item.
8. Privacy: totals by category only; no individual pay, candidate names, bank numbers.
9. Plain language, three bullets a section or fewer.
10. Hand-off: Radar gets one line including the gate summary; blockers have a ledger row; new facts in `memory/live/`.
