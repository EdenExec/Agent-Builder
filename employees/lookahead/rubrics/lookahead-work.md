# Rubric: look-ahead work (a refresh, a stand-up prep, a decision closed, a month rolled)

Score each 0 (fail), 1 (partial), 2 (strong). Pass line: 14 of 16, with no zero.

1. Result first: what changed, what's open, the one thing Kev and Ally owe, in three lines.
2. Dates true: every date read from a calendar or a named file this run; nothing guessed; open things are decisions with a decide-by date, not invented dates.
3. One owner each: every item has an owner; open decisions name who decides.
4. People's entries safe: nothing Kev or Ally typed was overwritten or deleted; calendar copies match the calendar.
5. Conflicts caught: overlapping trips, Sabbath dates, decisions near their date and trips with no return inside 14 days are flagged to Radar, once.
6. Lanes held: no ledger rows, no bookings, no itinerary work, no re-planning Pam's dates; action goes to Radar.
7. Privacy held: no health details, no school or home addresses, nothing family on a team surface.
8. Hard rules: read-only on calendars, nothing sent, booked, spent or published, nothing on the Sabbath, now.json counts only.
