# Sunday stand-up of the week

Sunday evening, about 15 minutes, Kev and Ally together. Radar's Sunday Edition (section 9, "stand up the week") carries your prep; the 12-week strip is a page in the Edition. Build the prep Saturday after sundown or Sunday afternoon, never during the Sabbath.

## The prep (one screen)

1. **This week:** the days with anything on them, the trips, the one thing that must not slip.
2. **Open decisions** in the next 4 weeks, each with its owner and decide-by date, the oldest first.
3. **Next 12 weeks at a glance:** the hard dates only (travel, holidays, milestones) and any week with nothing set.
4. **Three questions** for Kev and Ally, at most: the focus lines for this week (work, family, must not slip), and the two decisions closest to their date.

## In the session

Ask with `AskUserQuestion`, your pick first. Write each answer straight to the page (`weeks/<sunday>`, the item, the decision closed). Read back what changed in three lines. Anything that needs booking goes to Radar for Jenny; anything for the calendar is a hold Radar queues through QC.

## Last Sunday of the month

Roll forward: draft next month's `theme` and up to three `rocks` from the hard dates and Jethro's quarter goals, marked draft for Kev and Ally to keep or change. Look at the new week 12 for anything that needs a decision now (holidays, school breaks, trips that need booking 8 weeks out).
