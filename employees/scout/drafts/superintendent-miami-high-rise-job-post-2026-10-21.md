**Title:** Superintendent, High-Rise (Demolition through Vertical Construction)
**Company line:** Eden Executive Search, hiring for a confidential general contractor
**Location:** Miami, Florida (on-site)
**Type:** Full-time, direct hire, project-length assignment

## Post

A confidential general contractor is adding Superintendents for a high-rise tower in Miami. You will come in early to run the demolition scope before the project goes live, then stay on as the building goes vertical. This is a long-term project, and we want people who will be there through the structure, not just the start.

**What you will do**
- Run site demolition: sequencing, subcontractors, safety, neighbor and city coordination, and handoff to new construction
- Move into vertical construction on the tower: structure, hoisting and crane logistics, floor-by-floor sequencing
- Lead trade partners, hold the schedule, and keep the site safe and clean
- Work with the project team on look-aheads, RFIs, inspections and quality control

**What we are looking for**
- Ground-up high-rise superintendent experience (tower or comparable multi-story concrete or steel)
- Demolition or sitework experience is a strong plus
- Already living in the Miami metro, or willing to relocate and live there for the length of the project
- OSHA 30; a track record of running safe, on-schedule sites

**Why this one**
- A long project, with demo first and the full tower after it
- Steady work in one market, with no jumping between jobs

**To apply:** Easy Apply, or send your resume. We respond to every qualified applicant.
