**Title:** Quality Control Leadership, Oil and Gas Concrete (Louisiana and Houston)
**Company line:** Eden Executive Search, hiring for a confidential concrete contractor
**Location:** Louisiana and Houston, Texas (on-site; state your base in the application)
**Type:** Full-time, direct hire

### Post

A confidential concrete contractor is building out its Quality Control teams for oil and gas work in Louisiana and Houston. We are hiring across the QC ladder, from Deputy Director down to QC Manager and QC Coordinator, so there is a seat for the person who has been running a QC program for years and for the person ready to step into a first QC lead role.

**Roles open**
- Deputy Director of Quality Control
- QC Manager
- QC Coordinator

**What you will do**
- Run or support the project QC program for industrial concrete: inspection and test plans, hold points, and submittals
- Coordinate testing, third-party inspection and the owner's QA team
- Track nonconformance reports, corrective actions and closeout documentation
- Work with field leadership to catch problems before the pour, not after
- At the Deputy Director level: set QC standards across projects, build and lead the team, and be the contact for the client's quality leadership

**What we are looking for**
- Concrete quality control experience on industrial, oil and gas, petrochemical, LNG or similar heavy projects
- ACI certifications (Field Testing Technician Grade I or Strength Testing Technician) are a plus; Special Inspector or similar for senior roles
- Experience with owner QA requirements and formal documentation
- Based in, or willing to relocate to, Louisiana or the Houston area

**To apply:** Easy Apply, or send your resume. Tell us which role and which city you want.

3. Highest QC role you have held: Coordinator / Inspector / Manager / Director (pick one)
4. Which location? Louisiana / Houston / Either (pick one)
5. Are you legally authorized to work in the United States? (Yes/No) Must-have: Yes

## 2. VDC / BIM

**Title:** VDC / BIM Manager (level to confirm)
**Company line:** Eden Executive Search, hiring for a confidential general contractor
**Location:** Miami, FL; West Palm Beach, FL; Reston, VA; or remote
**Type:** Full-time, direct hire

### Post

A confidential general contractor is hiring a VDC / BIM professional to lead virtual design and construction on large vertical projects. Work from Miami, West Palm Beach or Reston, Virginia, or remotely.

**What you will do**
- Own the BIM model coordination: collect trade models, run clash detection, and drive resolution in coordination meetings
- Build and maintain the BIM execution plan and model standards for each project
- Support the field with model-based layout, quantities and 4D sequencing
- Work with project engineers, superintendents and the design team to turn the model into field answers
- Keep models, drawings and issue logs current in the project's common data environment

**What we are looking for**
- Experience running VDC or BIM coordination on a commercial, multifamily, high-rise or similar GC project
- Revit and Navisworks; experience with clash detection and model coordination
- Comfort leading coordination meetings with trades and design teams
- 4D or quantity takeoff experience is a plus
- Based in or near Miami, West Palm Beach or Reston, or fully remote

**To apply:** Easy Apply, or send your resume and a short note on the tools you use.
