# C12 follow-through (read the Forum notes, enact the change)

> **Lane note: Radar owns the ledger.** You do not keep a to-do list. Every change you pull from a Forum becomes a proposed ledger row that you hand to Radar; Radar writes it into Daily Catch-Up Items (`employees/radar/skills/ledger.md`) and it appears in Kev's lanes like any other row. You keep only a check-back schedule (row title and dates) and you chase through Radar. The same rule covers coaching commitments and reading insights.

Kev, 21 October 2026: "read my notes after C12 forums and enact change." The Forum is worth what changes afterwards. Your job is the day after: from notes to one to three concrete changes, owned, dated and checked.

## When

- **Forum day + 1 (Wednesday).** Read the notes and propose the changes in time for that day's 5:30pm check-out. The October 2026 Forum was Tuesday 20 October, so its follow-through is due now.
- **+7 days.** First check-back on each change.
- **+30 days** (lands just before the next Forum). Second check-back; the result feeds the next prep pack's wins or the one issue.
- Never on Saturday.

## Steps

1. **Find the notes.** Kev's Forum notes live in the Eden Journal (Notion data source `collection://61f6bc1d-5d84-43c3-b946-5bf602a35897`, rows tagged C12 or dated Forum day) or a Drive doc he names (search Drive for "C12" and the Forum date). Until you have Notion read access, ask Radar for the rows (Radar reads the journal every night). If there are no notes by Wednesday noon, ask once, gently, through Radar: "Any C12 notes from yesterday? Two lines is enough." Never reconstruct a Forum you did not see.
2. **Read for change, not summary.** Mark every line that implies something should be different: a decision, a commitment Kev made to the group, counsel he received, a pattern he named, a book or tool recommended.
3. **Pick one to three changes.** Rank by impact on the balance wheel or the 5-point alignment area Kev marked Behind, and by how soon it can be started. Park the rest in a "Parked" line for the next prep pack (not on the ledger).
4. **Make each change concrete.**

   | Field | Rule |
   |---|---|
   | Change | One line with a verb: what is different from when |
   | Why | The Forum note it came from, by reference ("C12 Oct, counsel on approvals"), never quoted at length |
   | Owner | Kev, or the agent or person who moves it (Radar, Dean, Tally, Joel, Nick). A work change routed to a person still needs Kev to tell them; you never contact anyone. |
   | First step | The next physical step, doable this week |
   | Done looks like | Observable: a meeting held, a rule written, a role handed over, a number moved |
   | Dates | Start, 7-day check, 30-day check |
   | Pillar | The Seven Pillar it lives into (Righteousness, Winning, Accountability, Discipline, Transparency, Growth, Execution) |

5. **Hand the rows to Radar** in this format, one per change:

   ```
   Item: <change, Kev's words where possible>
   Category: Formation (or the work category if it plainly lands in Eden)
   Lane: Mine | Scheduled | Waiting on
   Owner: Kev | Radar | <person>   (the ledger has no Cadence owner; Source names you)
   Due: <start or first-step date>
   Bill rate: Formation (Enabling if it changes how Eden runs)
   Source: C12 Forum <date> · Cadence
   Next Action: <first step, who does it>
   ```

   Rows carry the action, never the private content. No prayer requests, no scores, no quotes about family, health or money. A family or health change gets a neutral title for Kev only ("C12 Oct · family commitment, see your note") and nothing else.
6. **Check back.** At +7 days ask Radar for the row's status (or read the ledger view if you have access): started, stuck or done. Stuck gets one suggestion for a smaller first step. At +30 days: done, keep going, or drop. A drop needs Kev's yes; Radar marks it.
7. **Close the loop.** Done changes become wins in the next prep pack (`c12-prep.md`). A change that failed twice is a candidate for "the one issue" at the next Forum.

## Overlaps to route, not own

- A change about recruiter one-to-ones, scorecards or training goes to Dean as the preparer; Kev still holds the conversation (scorecards are discussed in person, no individual invites).
- A change about money, pricing or the books goes to Tally (and through the numbers gate if it carries a number).
- A change about Kev's day shape, inbox or calendar (for example "no email before 9") goes to Radar, who changes the rhythm or the sweep rules with Kev's yes.

The extract (changes, parked items, check-back dates) is a private document Radar files in Kev's private Drive folder, KDTW Group. The repo gets nothing from a Forum except, at most, a line on the lab-review deck such as "C12 Oct: 3 changes, 1 started" with no content.
