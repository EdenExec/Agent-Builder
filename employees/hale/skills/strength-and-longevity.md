# Strength and longevity (Kev's training plan)

The aim is a body that is strong, fit and moving well at 50, 70 and 90, built on a plan Kev actually runs in his real week. Read `memory/seed/guideline-starting-map.md` (Movement) and `memory/seed/household-frame.md` (the week) first.

## Before writing a plan

1. **Screen first.** Ask, privately: any known heart, lung, kidney or metabolic condition, any symptoms on exertion (chest discomfort, unusual breathlessness, dizziness), current injuries, any medication that affects heart rate or blood pressure. Any yes means "clear it with your doctor first" in line one, per ACSM pre-participation screening, and the plan waits or stays light. Never decide medical clearance yourself.
2. **Map the week.** From Kev's rhythm: which slots are real (before 5:30, lunch, after the 5:30 check-out, Sunday afternoon), how long each is, what equipment is there (home, gym, hotel, nothing). The Sabbath runs Friday sunset to Saturday sunset: no session depends on it.
3. **Baseline, simply.** Only what he wants to measure: a few working sets he knows, resting heart rate from a device he already owns, a walk or ride he can repeat. No paid tests, no purchases.

## The plan (minimum effective dose first)

| Block | Weekly target | Grade | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strength | 2 to 3 full-body sessions, 45 min or less: a squat or lunge, a hinge, a push, a pull, a carry; 2 to 4 hard sets each, 5 to 12 reps, 1 to 3 reps in reserve | SETTLED (ACSM, PAG) | Technique before load |
| Easy aerobic ("zone 2" framing) | 120 to 180 min at a conversational pace (can talk, not sing): walking, rucking, cycling, rowing | SETTLED for the minutes; the "zone 2" label UNSURE | Counts toward the 150 to 300 guideline minutes; walks with family count |
| Hard intervals (VO2 max) | One session: for example 4 x 4 min hard with 3 min easy, after 4 weeks of base | LIKELY | Only after the screen; stop rules in `memory/seed/red-flags.md` |
| Mobility and balance | 10 min, 3 times: hips, thoracic spine, ankles, shoulders, single-leg balance | LIKELY | Pair with the 5:30 routine or the evening wind-down |
| Daily movement | A step baseline, then add about 1,000 a day until a steady 8,000 or more | LIKELY (Paluch 2022) | Calls on foot count |

Fit it to the week: for example strength Monday, Wednesday, Friday before the office; intervals Tuesday; easy aerobic on calls and on Sunday afternoon with the family. Mark every slot "(A)" until Kev confirms it.

## Progression

- Add load or a rep when every set hits the top of the rep range with good form; one change a week.
- Every fourth to sixth week, or in a week like the move, deload: half the sets, same movements.
- A missed week restarts at the last comfortable load, not the plan's place. No catch-up sessions.
- Pain that changes how he moves, or any stop-rule symptom: stop, rest, and if it persists past a few days, see a clinician (physical therapist or doctor). Hale does not diagnose injuries.

## Outputs

- **The plan** (one page): the week grid, each session's movements and targets, the progression rule, the deload week, the stop rules. Source in the scratchpad, rendered with `npm run doc -- <file> --out <scratchpad>` (`org: kdtw`), delivered into the private store. Never committed.
- **A four-week review** in the private store: sessions done of planned, the lifts' trend, how he feels, one change.
- **Questions for the doctor** when anything in the screen or the review needs one, added to the next appointment's list (`skills/care-coordination.md`).

## Checks before handing it over

- Fits the real week, with no Saturday dependency and a plan for the move.
- Every target graded; popular framing (Attia's "centenarian decathlon", zone 2) labelled as framing.
- The screen was done and any yes was routed to a clinician.
- Nothing bought or booked: a gym, a coach, a device or an app is a `spend` QC item with no health detail in it.
- No personal number written into the repo, the paper or a shared page.
