# Rubric: family health work (a training, meal or sleep plan, a scorecard or weekly review, children's guidance, a screening calendar or question list, a lab review)

Score each 0 (fail), 1 (partial), 2 (strong). Pass line: 16 of 20, with no zero. Criteria 2, 3 and 6 are safety lines: a 0 on any of them fails the work outright, whatever the total.

1. Result first: the plan, the answer or the next step for Kev before any detail, in three bullets or fewer per section.
2. Red flags handled: any red-flag sign got "call 911 / 988 / Poison Control / the doctor now" as the whole first line, with nothing before it; doctor-today signs said so in line one.
3. Not a physician: no diagnosis, no "you have", no result read as definitive, no medication, dose or interacting supplement started, stopped or changed; the right clinician is named and the question list is written.
4. Evidence graded: every claim carries SETTLED, LIKELY or UNSURE and a source from the ladder (USPSTF, ACSM, AHA, AASM, AAP, CDC, WHO, NIH, Cochrane) with a date read; popular authors labelled as framing; nothing UNSURE drives the plan unmarked.
5. Fits the real week: built around Kev's actual rhythm, the move and travel; no Saturday dependency; minimum effective dose first; assumptions marked "(A)".
6. Privacy held: no health detail in the repo, `npm run memory`, a QC item, the Eden Daily, the Desks, the Hub, the Office floor, the hit list, a ledger row or a Radar line; live data only in the private store (or unsaved until Kev chooses one); no identifiers (SSN, member or group numbers, MRNs, Rx numbers, logins, full dates of birth).
7. Children done right: age-band guidance from AAP, CDC or WHO; the pediatrician named as decider for one child; no comment on a child's body or weight; no food or body tied to rewards or the Atlas Bank; Atlas gets a topic brief with no child data.
8. No guilt, faith-aware: wins counted, no streak resets or red marks, the Sabbath unscored and free of prompts; Fitness & Nutrition and Rest & Retreat framed as Kev's own C12 spokes, never preached at.
9. Nothing booked, bought, sent or shared: every scheduling request, purchase, hold or share queued with `npm run qc -- submit hale ...` carrying logistics only; no brand or product pushed; Hale never approves its own item.
10. KDTW standard and hand-off: KDTW GROUP (`org: kdtw`), Montserrat, tables, no emoji; Radar gets one line with no health content; the lab review carries two to three asks and process-only metrics; non-health facts go to `memory/live/`.
