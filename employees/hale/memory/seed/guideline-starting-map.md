# Guideline starting map (seeded 21 October 2026; re-read each source before relying on it)

The baseline numbers Hale starts from. Each line carries its grade and source as of the seed date. Guidelines change, and several US bodies revised advice in 2025 and 2026: before a line drives a plan or a calendar, open the source, confirm it is current, and update this file with the date read. Generic only: nothing here is about a person.

## Movement

| Who | Baseline | Grade | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adults | 150 to 300 min a week moderate aerobic, or 75 to 150 vigorous, or a mix; more brings more benefit | SETTLED | Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans, 2nd ed. (HHS 2018); WHO 2020 |
| Adults | Muscle strengthening of moderate or greater intensity, all major muscle groups, 2 or more days a week | SETTLED | Same |
| Adults | Resistance training 2 to 3 days a week, 2 to 4 sets of 8 to 12 reps for most, progressive overload | SETTLED | ACSM Guidelines for Exercise Testing and Prescription (current edition) |
| Older adults | Add balance and multicomponent training | SETTLED | PAG 2nd ed. |
| Adults | Higher cardiorespiratory fitness (VO2 max) tracks with lower all-cause mortality, with no upper plateau seen | LIKELY (observational) | Mandsager et al., *JAMA Netw Open* 2018; Kodama et al., *JAMA* 2009 meta-analysis |
| Adults | Grip strength tracks with mortality and cardiovascular events | LIKELY (observational) | Leong et al., *Lancet* 2015 (PURE) |
| Adults | More daily steps, lower mortality, levelling near 6,000 to 8,000 a day over 60 and 8,000 to 10,000 under 60 | LIKELY | Paluch et al., *Lancet Public Health* 2022 |

Also: CBT-I is first-line treatment for chronic insomnia, before medication (SETTLED; ACP 2016, AASM 2021): a clinician's referral, not Hale's program. Melatonin for children only on the pediatrician's advice (SETTLED as AASM advisory, 2022). Caffeine within about six hours of bed shortens sleep (LIKELY; Drake et al., 2013). Screens off an hour before bed and devices out of bedrooms (AAP advice; benefit LIKELY).

## Children (AAP Bright Futures; CDC)

- Well-child visits on the Bright Futures periodicity schedule: many in the first two years, then yearly from age 3 (SETTLED, AAP).
- Developmental milestones: CDC "Learn the Signs. Act Early." checklists (2022 revision, to age 5; set at what 75% of children do by that age). Developmental screening at 9, 18 and 30 months, autism screening at 18 and 24 months, done by the pediatrician (SETTLED, AAP).
- School age and teens: obesity screening from 6 with behavioural support offered (USPSTF B, 2024), anxiety 8 to 18 (USPSTF B, 2022), depression 12 to 18 (USPSTF B, 2022), yearly well visits with vision and hearing per Bright Futures (SETTLED).
- Puberty usually begins between about 8 and 13 in girls and 9 and 14 in boys (SETTLED, AAP HealthyChildren); early or late signs are a pediatrician question.
- Dental: first visit by age 1, then about every 6 months, fluoride toothpaste from the first tooth (SETTLED, AAPD and AAP).
- Screens: under 18 months none except video chat; 18 to 24 months only high-quality with a parent; 2 to 5 years about one hour a day of high-quality content, watched together (SETTLED, AAP 2016). From 6, a family media plan with consistent limits that protect sleep, activity, homework and meals; AAP now frames older children's use by quality and fit rather than a single number (LIKELY).
