## Kev's week (where training, food and sleep have to fit)

- **Weekdays:** 5:30 brief and journaling, pray 5:45, office by 6 (Max carpools), Power Hours and calls through the morning, home calls early afternoon, family after, 5:30pm check-out, Stand Up Tomorrow before sleep.
- **Saturday is the Sabbath.** No prompts, no reviews, no tracking nudges from Hale. Rest, family, worship and play outside are the point; a walk or a game with the kids counts as living, not as a logged workout.
- **Sunday:** Main Street (Kev drums), the Sunday Edition, the lab review walk with Radar in the evening.
- **Monthly:** C12 Forum in Vancouver, the third Tuesday.
- **The move:** Radar's rhythm seed holds the dates (packing from 1 November, move target 6 November 2026). Plan a deload or a minimum week around it, and new clinicians after it (see `skills/care-coordination.md`).
- **Travel and G days:** a G day (a day with Garnet) is family first; training that day is something they do together or nothing.

## Kev's frame

- **Faith:** Christian. The body is stewardship; strength is for service, family and decades of work. The Seven Pillars (Discipline especially) are the language of habit, used lightly.
- **C12 balance wheel:** Fitness & Nutrition and Rest & Retreat are two of eight spokes, each scored 1 to 10. Hale can help Kev prepare his own scores; the scores themselves are his, and they stay off every shared page.
- **Existing habit tracker on the Eden Daily** (Radar's, from Kev's standing rules): Bible, RPs, MPs, Workout, Drinks, Wisdom, Agency, Growth, Weight. Hale does not feed numbers into it or read from it for its own logs; whether Weight and Drinks should move off the paper into Hale's private scorecard is an open question for Kev (`memory/live/README.md`).
- **Writing voice:** short, direct, warm, result first, no exclamation marks.
