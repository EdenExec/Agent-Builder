## Result at a glance

Hold one evening job fair in West Palm Beach on Thursday 19 November (D), built to put 60 qualified people in the room and fill 6 of the 18 open seats. Proposed budget is $3,750, Kev's decision. (A) marks an assumption; nothing is sent, booked or spent without Kev's QC approval.

- Vinny asked on 9 October ("Let's have a job fair?"). Kev answers once, after the decisions below.
- Seats: City Place, Phillips Point, Hibiscus Garage (West Palm Beach); Project X, Project Y (Miami). Split by site unknown (A).

## Decisions for Kev

| Decision | Radar recommends |
|---|---|
| 1. Date and venue | Thursday 19 November, 5:30 to 8:00 pm, private room near the West Palm Beach office. Starts the D-6 weeks clock now; clears Thanksgiving. |
| 2. Budget and who pays | $3,750 total. PB South pays venue, food, drinks and the bonus; Eden pays prizes, promotion and team time (about $1,100). Vinny agrees before anything is announced. |
| 3. Signing bonus | Flat X on the offer of any attendee who joins, half at 30 days and half at 90. Vinny and Carol set X against current PB South pay. |

## Wonder: the questions

- Who is in the room: supers, project managers and engineers for the 18 seats, plus interns.
- What PB South needs by when: which seats need a January start, which can wait.
- What makes a super drive across the county on a weeknight: food, a real prize, leaders, a fast offer.

## Invention: the ideas

- Raffle with tiered tickets for bringing people; Yeti cooler as grand prize, gift cards below it; free food and two drink tickets each.
- Signing bonus tied to attendance; intern offers for spring and January starts if the schedule allows.
- Mateo, Gio and Amar as culture voices (roles not in my notes, A); Eden recruiters on site with the depth charts.

## Discernment: the judgment calls

| Call | Radar recommends, and the rule it comes from |
|---|---|
| Headcount | 60 in the room, 120 RSVPs. 18 seats less any with offers out, divided by 3, is 6 hires; 50% acceptance means 12 offers; 1 in 5 offer-ready means 60 guests; 50% show-up means 120 RSVPs. Ratios are (A): swap in Crelate history. |
| Venue | Neutral private room for 80 standing, not a jobsite (insurance, safety). The employer pays for the hire, Eden for the pull. Add a Miami evening only if RSVPs lean south. |
| Prizes | $800, 20% of the budget (A): Yeti about $300 (quote needed), 2 cards at $100, 6 at $50. |
| Bonus risks | Pay-and-leave (installments); resentment among current staff (add a staff referral bonus); clawback wording (PB South counsel); bonus-only attendees (fit decides). |

## Galvanizing: the rally

- Vinny invites, with a line from a culture voice; Kev writes to the Eden network. Every send goes through QC.
- Referral mechanic: 1 ticket for attending, plus 2 for each guest who checks in naming you, capped at 10 guests. Free entry; PB South counsel confirms Florida raffle rules (A).
- Email and social pushes at D-4, D-3, D-2 weeks and D-1 day; recruiters call every depth-chart name.

## Enablement: the help

- Nick: RSVPs, check-in, scorecard in Crelate. Nida: invitee lists, orders, printing, ticket stubs.
- Recruiters: outreach, interviews, offers in a week. Carol (role A): offer letters, bonus wording.
- Check-in: name, tag, ticket, referrer. Raffle: count at 7:15; winner must be present.

## Tenacity: the finish

- Run-of-show: 5:30 doors; 6:00 Vinny and a culture voice; 6:15 interview stations by site; 7:15 raffle; 7:45 close with next steps.
- Follow-up inside 48 hours to every guest; offers out inside 7 days with the bonus.
- Scorecard at D+7: 120 RSVPs, 60 attendees, 12 offers, 6 seats filled, $625 per hire.
