## Checklist per candidate

| Stage | Items |
|---|---|
| In interviews | Next interview date confirmed; candidate still interested; feedback from client collected; counter-offer risk noted |
| Accepted, start pending | Offer signed; resignation given; counter-offer risk; background or drug test done; first-day logistics (where, when, who); start date confirmed with client and candidate |
| Day 1 to 30 | First-day check; week-1 call; day-30 check-in |
| Day 31 to 60 | Day-60 check-in |
| Day 61 to 90 | Day-90 check-in; ask for a referral; move to Done |

A candidate who drops is set to Out with the reason, never deleted.
