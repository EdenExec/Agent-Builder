## 1. Submittal packet (with Radar, rolled out to the whole team)

Every candidate going to a client gets one packet, in this order:

1. **Eden cover page.** Clean, Hannah's framework from Hoffman: basics up front, bullets or a simple table.
   - Name, title, years of experience
   - $$$: current or desired base, bonus, vehicle allowance, ESOP, ownership or similar golden handcuffs
   - Availability; actively interviewing elsewhere (yes/no)
   - Google search link: a Boolean of name variations, to show a first-pass background check
2. **Pre-screen scorecard** under the overview: the candidate against the client's 7-10 Must Dos (core elements of the job). Must Dos come from the client on the intake call; otherwise we build them and send to the client for approval. Every JO from now on gets a Must Do list.
3. **Original resume**, in its own format.
4. **References** with "Please do not contact without the candidate's clear consent", plus relevant notes, transcripts and info from our screening calls.

Why: clients are asking for better candidate quality and match, and more references.
