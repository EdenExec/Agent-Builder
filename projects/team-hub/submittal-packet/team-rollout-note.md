From today, every candidate we send to a client goes with the same packet, in this order:

1. Eden cover page: name, title, experience, pay (current or desired), availability, whether they are interviewing elsewhere, and a Google search link as our first-pass background check.
2. Pre-screen scorecard against the client's Must Dos.
3. The original resume, untouched.
4. References, marked "Please do not contact without the candidate's clear consent," plus the relevant notes and transcripts from your screening call.

Every JO now gets a list of 7 to 10 Must Dos. Get them from the client on the intake call. If the client does not have a list, we write one and send it for approval before we score anyone.

Why: clients are asking for better quality, better match and more references. This is how we show it.

Packets live in Drive, one folder per client. Never in a text, a ticket or the Hub. I read each one before it goes out.
