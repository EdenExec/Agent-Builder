## Privacy

- Phones live only on the lead sheets (and Crelate). Never in chat, docs, the Lead Log, tickets or email.
- No personal email, no comp, no age or anything protected.
- Sheets are internal, Eden only. Never client-facing.
- ZoomInfo data is for Eden's own recruiting: never share outside Eden, never resell, never bulk unsolicited outreach.

## Crelate

Lead lists load into a bucket in the Crelate Leads / Buckets column ("Client - Role - City Leads"), never onto an active job order. The BPO team (Nida) loads them overnight from the master folder. You never write to Crelate.

## Sabbath

No work Friday sunset to Saturday sunset.
