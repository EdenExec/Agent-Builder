# Must Dos · PB South Vertical Transportation Specialist (Miami)

## Part A · Client wording (for John's approval)

**The seat:** the owner-side elevator expert on a very large Miami high-rise elevator package. Protects PB South's schedule and money on the most schedule-critical system in the tower: design review, submittals, install sequence, construction elevators, testing and turnover to the AHJ. Not a general CM. Two profiles fit: a **VT engineer or consultant** (strong on design, traffic and code) or a **VT project manager from an elevator OEM** (strong on install, schedule and field).

| # | Must Do | What "meets" looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | High-rise elevator expertise | 30+ floor new construction (residential, office or hotel). Can name the project, cars and speeds. New install, not only modernization. |
| 2 | Owner-side or OEM construction-lead role | VT consultant, or OEM construction manager, PM or installation superintendent. Not service or sales only. |
| 3 | Reads and challenges the design | Has reviewed shop drawings and submittals. Knows hoistway, pit, overhead, machine room vs MRL, and the structural and electrical tie-ins. |
| 4 | Code fluency | ASME A17.1 / CSA B44 and A17.2. Has closed out with authorities having jurisdiction; Florida Building Code and Miami-Dade a plus. QEI a plus. |
| 5 | Schedule and sequence on a live tower | Has planned hoistway turnover, installation and construction elevators around the GC schedule, protecting the top-out to TCO path. |
| 6 | Testing, acceptance and closeout | Has witnessed acceptance and safety tests, run punch lists and closed out with the AHJ and owner. |
| 7 | Commercial sense | Has handled change orders, bid leveling and backcharges with the elevator subcontractor, and can show how they protected the owner's money. |
| 8 | South Florida, or ready to be there | Based in SE Florida or will relocate (relocation is open). Full time on site in Miami. |

**Nice to have:** destination dispatch, traffic analysis or simulation, double-deck or high-speed (1,000+ fpm) cars, supertall experience.
**Does not fit:** service or maintenance only, sales only, no new-install high-rise experience, or a GC PM for whom elevators were one trade among many.

## Part B · Internal only (Eden recruiters and callers; never in the email to John, a screen share or any client paper)

### Screener (ask every candidate)

1. Which high-rise elevator projects have you run? For each: floors, number of cars, speeds, your role.
2. Owner side, consultant side or OEM side? What did you personally own: design, submittals, install, testing?
3. Walk me through how you sequence elevator installation on a 30+ floor tower. When do hoistways turn over, and how do you handle construction elevators?
4. Which codes do you work to? Any QEI or other certification? Which inspectors (AHJ) have you closed out with, any in Florida?
5. Tell me about a time the elevator package threatened the schedule. What did you do?
6. How have you handled change orders or backcharges with the elevator subcontractor?
7. Are you in South Florida? If not, is moving to Miami workable, and when could you start?
8. What do you earn now (base, bonus, vehicle), and what would make a move worth it? **Internal only. Never written on any client document, scorecard or the Hub.**

### Pitch (for recruiters)

- **Scale:** a very large elevator scope on a Miami high-rise with PB South. Elevators are the whole job here, not a side job.
- **Why move:** an owner-side voice on a landmark tower with the authority to get the elevator plan right from the start.
- **Standing rule:** never call Related staff (PB South's partner on Project X).
