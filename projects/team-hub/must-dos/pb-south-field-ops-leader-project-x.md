# PB South · Field Operations Leader · Project X (Miami)

Seat brief for Scout's lead list, from the PB South interview call (Bobby Hubner with Jeff Rosser and John Fedeli; Kev opened the call and dropped off), transcript Kev shared 26 Oct 2026. Seat facts only: no candidate details here. PB South and Related names stay off the lead sheet.

## The seat

- **Title:** Field Operations Leader (Kev frames it as a Construction Executive seat: Director of Field Operations or General Superintendent level, with an executive path).
- **Where it sits:** above the General Superintendent, below the senior executive. Bobby: "somebody on the operations side above the general super, kind of really digging into the schedule, working with the APM staff, and just making sure we're pulling everything together... with a heavy operational mindset." "That extra layer between the senior executive and the general super." Walks the field, but mostly in the office keeping everyone aligned. It is how Bobby runs his large New York jobs.
- **Team:** a joint venture. Related Development is development manager and co-builder; staffing is 50% PB South, 50% Related, run as one team. Final interview is in person in New York with Bobby and Ahmad (EVP, Related Development).

## The project

- Class A commercial office high-rise in Miami for a very high-end financial firm (Kev: Citadel). "The most complex office building ever to be built in Miami."
- Base contract about $1 billion (Kev's figure: $1.1B supertall). A site across the street, about 95% likely to go to the same team, adds about $450 to $500 million.
- Heavy MEP: about five mechanical rooms up the building, and medium voltage brought up the riser instead of 277 to keep feeder sizes down, which is unusual for commercial high-rise.
- Next door: a 40 to 50 story residential tower after demolition, a separate job run by one team (see the Demo Super Must Dos in `projects/team-hub/jo-meetings/2026-10-26-jo-meeting-and-pb-south.md`).

## Must-haves (from the call)

1. High-rise experience: Miami high-rise, or mega projects elsewhere (Kev). Bobby asked for high-rise references and a detailed project list.
2. A heavy operational mindset, at operations-manager or general-superintendent-over-supers level.
3. Complex MEP: data center, hospital or other heavy-mechanical backgrounds count in a candidate's favour (Bobby said so directly).
4. Schedule command: P6 is the platform with a schedule consultant already on board; supers build detail and breakout schedules (MS Project or similar). "Understanding it, using it, and managing it, and not throwing it in the file cabinet."
5. Willing to relocate to Miami. Start early January (JO meeting notes); interviews run through year end.
