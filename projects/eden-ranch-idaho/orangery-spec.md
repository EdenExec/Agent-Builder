# Orangery spec (KDTW GROUP) - Eden Ranch, Idaho

Status: draft for Kev. Provisional: no parcel chosen. Every number below is a design proposal or UNVERIFIED, not a code or engineering fact. Not an architect of record, structural or mechanical engineer's work.

## Recommendation

One attached, south-facing glass room off the kitchen and living wing, 14 ft deep by 28 ft long (about 390 sq ft), on the main level, with a stone or concrete floor and a shed roof that rises toward the south.

## The room, in numbers

| Item | Proposal | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Footprint | 14 x 28 ft, about 390 sq ft | Room for 8 to 10 large potted trees plus a path and a bench |
| Height | 13 ft at the house wall falling to an 8 ft south eave, 3 ft overhang | Roof drains away from the house; tubbed citrus tops out around 6 to 8 ft (UNVERIFIED). Corrected 15 Oct: the first draft sloped the roof toward the house, which would drain onto the wall. |
| Orientation | Long side faces south, glass 60 to 70 percent of south wall | Winter sun is low; the roof overhang shades summer sun |
| Roof | Insulated opaque north half, glazed south half | Cuts heat loss and keeps the room from cooking in July |
| Glass | Double or triple glazed, low-E, thermally broken steel or timber frame | Winter nights in the Treasure Valley are cold; UNVERIFIED |
| Floor | Polished concrete or stone over insulation, with hydronic loops | Thermal mass, easy to wash, trees in pots can be wheeled |
| Heat | Radiant floor from the house boiler plus a small backup heater; target 50 F at night | Citrus survives cool nights but not hard frost; UNVERIFIED for each variety |
| Vents | Operable ridge and low vents, shade cloth or exterior blinds | Passive cooling for hot dry summers; fans as backup |
| Water | Hose bib, floor drain, 1 sink | Watering and cleaning without carrying buckets |
| Light | Dimmable warm LED for the evenings; optional grow light for winter | Short winter days |

## How it connects

- Door from the kitchen and a second door to the south terrace, so it works as a breakfast room and an outdoor-room link.
- Visible from the mudroom path so you can check on it without entering.
- Placed on the garden side of the U, in the courtyard fold, so it is sheltered from the prevailing wind. Wind direction is UNVERIFIED for the parcel.
- Kept out of the lower-level hitting bay and walkout patio, which want a different kind of light.

## Risks and questions

| Item | Note |
|---|---|
| Frost and snow load | Roof glass must carry the local snow load; needs a structural engineer and the county's design values. UNVERIFIED |
| Energy | Large glass areas lose heat in winter; the room is a heated greenhouse, not a free sunroom. Costs UNVERIFIED |
| Pests and humidity | Citrus brings scale and spider mites; keep the room separable from the house air if possible |
| Cost | A custom glass room runs far above standard framing per sq ft. No figure in the seed files; need a quote. UNVERIFIED |
| Code | Whether it counts as habitable space, a conservatory or a greenhouse changes energy-code treatment. Ask the county and the building official |

## Assumptions to confirm

1. Main level, attached to the kitchen/living wing, not the lower level.
2. Hydronic heat is available from the main house plant.
3. The room is for growing plus sitting, not a year-round bedroom or office.
4. Citrus in tubs (movable), not planted in the ground.
