## Executive summary

Bridger can run the Operations Intern track from this file alone. Day 1 takes about an hour of his time, a normal week about 45 minutes, and the week-12 review about 45 minutes. Because three leaders will ask for work, Bridger is also the traffic cop.

- **Before Day 1:** 17 steps. Bridger does 4 himself (rows 1, 12, 15, 17). The rest need a person (Joel, Docket, Kev, Nick, Q), and rows 2, 7, 8, 9 and 13 can involve spend, so Kev says yes first.
- **Every week:** Monday priorities with the three leaders, a 15-minute check-in, the log review and the timesheet.
- **Hard stops:** nothing leaves without a leader's Approved mark, no screening result or employee private detail is ever opened or written down, and nothing is booked or paid.

**Key.** PERSON means a human must act and Bridger cannot. SPEND means money is involved and Kev says yes first. SELF means Bridger or the intern does it with no one else. Gap IDs (IG) point to `projects/eden-university/intern-gaps.md`; G IDs point to `projects/eden-university/stand-up-your-desk/gaps.md`.

### The 15-minute check-in

Bridger sets a recurring 15-minute slot himself on Day 1, in person or on a call already on his calendar. The intern brings the Progress Sheet.

| Minutes | Agenda | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 3 | One thing that went right, with the evidence | Lead with what went right |
| 3 to 8 | Proofs: open the sheet and sign what is ready | Sign only what you saw |
| 8 to 12 | Numbers and blockers: hours this week, the work counts, anything stuck, any "UNVERIFIED" step that differed | Counts, not feelings |
| 12 to 15 | Next week: the modules, the one change, the pillar to live into | Write it in the sheet |

After each check-in write three lines in the intern's notes: **Went right. One change. Next rep.** Work only. Health, family, age and pay never go in a note. If one comes up and matters to the work, write "Bridger to discuss with Kev in person" and nothing more.

## How to sign off a module

| # | Step | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open the intern's Progress Sheet row | Status should read "proof in". If it reads "not started", there is nothing to sign |
| 2 | Compare the proof with the module's "Show me" line | Same wording in `course.md` and `track.json` |
| 3 | Checked by Bridger: watch it or read it | Watch live where the module says so (the sheet notes which) |
| 4 | Checked by Jenny: confirm her reply exists | Her note in the pack or the Drive file says OK. You record it; you do not redo her review |
| 5 | Checked by Self: skim it | A skim, not a review. Pull a thread if something looks off |
| 6 | Sign | Status "signed off", your initials, the date |
| 7 | Or send it back | Status "redo" with one line: what is missing. A redo is practice, not a reprimand |

| Status in the sheet | Meaning |
|---|---|
| not started | No work yet |
| in progress | Working on it |
| proof in | Intern says it is ready; waiting on the checker |
| signed off | Checked, initialled, dated |
| redo | Sent back with one line |
| waiting on gap | Blocked by an IG item; the intern is not at fault |

**Never sign** a module because the week ran out, or because the intern is liked, or on a proof that shows a password, number, client or candidate detail (send it back for a retake).

## What "behind" looks like, and what to do

| Signal | What it means | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| Asks more than two days past their due date, twice in a month | Capacity or clarity | Look at the log together. Cut the list, or ask the leader what "done" means. Do not take on more without moving something |
| Two or more modules in the current phase unsigned a week after it ends | Behind on pace | Pick the one that unblocks the rest (usually M07 or M15) and clear it first |
| STOP · Anything sent or created without an Approved mark | Approval rule broken | Recall or cancel it with the intern. Tell the leader at once. Tell Kev. Re-teach M05 and M07 |
| STOP · A screening report or result opened, saved or forwarded | Privacy rule broken | Close it. Tell Nick and Docket by ticket the same day. Do not forward or delete anything yourself |
| STOP · Employee health, claims, pay or ID numbers written in a tracker, log or message | Privacy rule broken | Remove it from the file. Tell Nick and Docket by ticket. Re-teach M03 |
| STOP · Anything booked or paid | Spend rule broken | Tell Kev the same hour; he decides whether to keep or cancel |
| Hours not recorded, or a week heading past 40 | Wage-and-hour risk | Fix the timesheet the same day and tell the intern in writing. The 40-hour OK is yours to give, in writing and in advance. Repeats go to Docket |
| Two check-ins missed in a row | Blocked or drifting | Call the same day and ask about the work. No reply by the next day: tell Kev |
| The intern shares something about health, family or age | Not for any file | Listen. Write nothing. If the work is affected, tell Kev in person |

**The ladder.** First time: coach in the check-in. Second time in a month: write the fix in the sheet and tell Nick. Third time, or any row marked STOP: tell Kev in person.

Never promise a job, a reward or an extension to fix a gap. Those are not yours to give.

## Escalation path

| If it is about | Go to | How and how fast |
|---|---|---|
| Two leaders ask for the same hour or the same thing | Bridger decides the order | Same day. He tells both leaders |
| A question about a screening, background or drug-test process or result | Nick, with Docket by ticket | The intern never answers or looks |
| Insurance, benefits or any employee's private matter | Nick or Joel; Kev in person for the personal | The same day. Nothing written in the log |
| Pay, hours, classification, a school form, student-visa paperwork | Docket (words and law) and Joel (Gusto), by Command Center ticket | Never answer from memory |
| An account, calendar or tracker not working | Q by ticket; Kev grants access | The same day |
| Anything that costs money: travel, a vendor, a seat | The leader, then Kev through a QC spend item | Stop and wait |
| Conduct or performance beyond the ladder | Kev, in person | The same day. No written promises |

Command Center tickets (Bridger already works them) are how every row above reaches Radar. Radar carries it to Kev as a QC item. You never decide a spend, a send or a policy.

### Words to use with the intern

| Avoid | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "Stipend", "unpaid", "volunteer", "trial shift", "shadow day" | "Hourly pay". Any work time is paid time |
| "You will be full time after this", "path to full time" | "Strong interns may be considered for open roles" |
| "Young", "digital native", questions about age, graduation year, faith, family plans | Ask about the work: "Tell me about a time you..." |
| Any preference about the person's sex or looks | Describe the work, not the person |
| "Just finish it tonight, don't clock it" | "Log every hour; ask me first if you will pass 40" |

Slack and texts are evidence. Write as if Docket will read it.
