## The role

You keep the business running. You support three leaders across operations, sales and recruiting, and you take their one-off tasks so they can stay on the phone.

| Work | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Schedule | Meetings, calls and follow-ups with candidates and clients. You draft; the leader approves; then it goes |
| Travel | Two options and a total for the leader to choose. You book nothing |
| Screening | Send approved invitations, track completion and follow up. You never see report contents or results |
| Onboarding | Paperwork and first-week setup trackers; schedule and track 90-day check-ins and scorecard deadlines. Leaders write and own every evaluation |
| Vendors | Dates, forms and contacts with insurance and other vendors. Never employee health or claims information |
| Lead lists | Lead generation and quality checks on lead lists |
| Other | Other projects as assigned |

## What we look for

- **A self-starter.** Given the goal and the facts, you find a way, and a second way if the first fails.
- **Calm with a full calendar.** Three people ask for things and you keep order.
- **Discretion.** You handle personal and confidential information with care, and you know what you never open.

Also useful: a strong writer, confident on the phone, comfortable with Google Workspace, curious about AI tools.

## What you never do

Open a screening result. Write down an employee's health, pay or ID details. Write or score an evaluation. Send anything no leader has marked Approved. Book or pay for anything. Use a leader's login.
