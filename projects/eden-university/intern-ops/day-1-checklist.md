## The three things to know

| Rule | In practice |
|---|---|
| Nothing leaves without a leader's Approved mark | You draft. A leader sets Approved. Then you send or create |
| You track status only | You never open a screening result or write down a private detail about a person |
| Every hour is recorded and paid | Clock in and out. Over 40 hours needs Bridger's written OK first |

## Never

| Do not | Instead |
|---|---|
| Book or pay for travel or anything else | Prepare two options. The leader decides |
| Write, edit or score an evaluation | Schedule and track the deadline. Leaders write them |
| Use a leader's login, or share yours | Ask for delegate access |
| Put a password, code or private detail in a screenshot | Crop it out and retake |
