## Executive summary

Bridger can run all four tracks by himself once the gates below are open. 28 items need a person, a spend or a decision. Nothing here has been done, bought, sent or booked.

Four gates stop everything, and only Joel, Docket and Kev can open them: **(1)** Idaho payroll (IG1); **(2)** the base hourly rate and the posting (IG2); **(3)** the signed paper (IG3, IG20); **(4)** Bridger's own time (IG28).

- **One trigger.** When Kev names a hire (name, track, start date, school, hours), Dean opens one parent ticket on the Command Center and one child per row in each runbook. None is created today.
- **One yes per hire, not per seat.** Kev approves the spend and access rows once, as one batched QC item.
- **Where a person stays by design:** payroll and the signed paper (Joel, Docket), the in-person review, and every Approved mark.

The "Kev's yes" column reads: spend (a seat, a licence, a premium), access (who can reach what), send (a message to a hire), decision, or none. Recruiter gaps (G and C IDs) stay in `stand-up-your-desk/gaps.md`.

## What stays human by design

| Step | Who | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Idaho payroll, the signed paper, the school and visa forms | Joel, Docket | Legal and identity steps. The tracks hold no pay, rate or contract terms |
| Every Approved mark on a post, deck, invite or message | Kev, or the leader who owns the ask | No intern sends, posts, spends or edits a shared record alone |
| The end-of-program review | Bridger, in person | A review is a conversation. Nobody creates an invite for it |
