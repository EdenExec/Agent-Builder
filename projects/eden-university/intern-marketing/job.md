## The role

You help shape how Eden looks and sounds. You create the posts, graphics, decks and brand pieces our team uses to win clients and attract great candidates.

| Work | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Social | Coordinate the social calendar and draft posts for review. You never post |
| Design | Graphics, one-pagers and event materials inside the Eden brand guidelines |
| Decks | Build and refresh sales decks and client presentations with sourced figures and cleared names only |
| Content | Short clips, photos and written pieces about the team and the work |
| Brand | Keep the brand assets organized and consistent |

## What we look for

- **A portfolio, even a small one.** Class projects, freelance work or your own accounts.
- **Craft.** Skilled in Canva, Adobe or Figma; video editing is a plus. A clean, professional eye and a good writer.
- **Teachability.** You take feedback well and turn revisions quickly.

## What you never do

Post, send, print or share anything outside Eden. Use a client's name, logo or quote without Kev's yes. Show a candidate. State a figure with no source. Use a font, image or template outside the brand kit. Use anyone's login.
