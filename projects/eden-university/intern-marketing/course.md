## Executive summary

You will design the graphics, decks and brand pieces Eden uses to win clients and attract great candidates, and draft posts for review. The course takes you there in 23 modules, with Bridger checking each step.

**Three things to know.** Nothing you make is posted, sent or printed without Kev's approval. No client, candidate or unsourced figure goes in a piece. Every hour you work is recorded and paid.

This track is Pathway D without the scheduling and capture work, which belong to `intern-comms`. Your rate is in your offer letter and is not repeated here.

## How to read this course

**Every minute is paid.** Clock in before you start a module and clock out when you finish. Training is work time at Eden.

**What a proof is.** A screenshot or a line in your Progress Sheet. Crop out passwords, codes, phone numbers, personal emails, candidate and client details, and anything about pay. If it is in the picture, retake it.

**Status tags.** `UNVERIFIED` marks a menu name, URL or product step not yet confirmed in Eden's own account. Do the step as written; if what you see differs, write what you saw in your Progress Sheet and tell Bridger. The first intern's walk-through confirms or corrects each tag.

| Rule for every module | Why |
|---|---|
| Your own login, always. Nobody shares a password or asks for one, and nobody works in Kev's or anyone else's account. | 01 Righteousness (stewardship of the keys) |
| You never send, post, publish, spend or change a shared record on your own. You draft it, the person named in your playbook says yes, and then it goes. | 03 Accountability |
| Clients and candidates stay private. A discreet client is written "Client · discreet" everywhere, and no candidate detail leaves Eden's systems. | 05 Transparency (integrity) |
| Notes, proofs and messages are about the work. Pay, health, family and age stay out. If one matters to your work, write "Bridger to discuss in person" and nothing more. | 01 Righteousness |
| You make drafts. Marlowe checks the design, Kev approves, and someone else posts. Nothing leaves Eden from your hands. | 03 Accountability |

**Stuck for more than 10 minutes?** Write the module ID and step number ("M07 step 3") in your Progress Sheet, tell Bridger at your next check-in (or message him sooner), and start the next module. You never wait silently.

| Checked by | What it means |
|---|---|
| Self | Your proof goes in the Progress Sheet. Bridger skims it at the weekly check-in |
| Bridger | He looks at your proof or watches you do it, then signs the module with a date |
| Jenny | She reads the work in the weekly pack and replies with fixes. Bridger records the sign-off |
