## The three things to know

| Rule | In practice |
|---|---|
| A call that is not logged does not count | Log every call, type and job, the moment you finish it |
| You touch only your own activity in Crelate | No stage, owner, tag or job-order change. Ask the owner |
| Every hour is recorded and paid | Clock in and out. Over 40 hours needs Bridger's written OK first |

## Never

| Do not | Instead |
|---|---|
| Talk about pay, fees or your commission with a candidate | Say the recruiter will cover it |
| Send anything written to a candidate | Draft it and show Bridger first |
| Call after 4:00 PM in the lead's time zone | Check the Time Zone column |
| Write a discreet client's name outside the Team Hub | "Client · discreet" |
| Put a password, code, candidate phone or email in a screenshot | Crop it out and retake |
