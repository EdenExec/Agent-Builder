## The role

You learn executive search by doing it. Half your time is research: finding the right people for live searches. Half is on the phone: getting them interested. When your work leads to a placement, you earn under the written plan.

| Work | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Source | Candidates for live construction leadership searches, from LinkedIn, Eden's database and research tools |
| Call | Outbound calls: introduce the role, qualify interest, book the next conversation |
| Log | Every call and contact, cleanly, in Eden's CRM. A call not logged does not count |
| Learn | Weekly headhunter training and your own call goals |

## How you earn

- **Hourly pay** for every hour worked, including training, at the rate in your offer letter.
- **Commission** under Eden's written Sales Intern Commission Plan, which you receive with your offer and sign before you start.
- **Nothing else is promised.** Strong Jr. Headhunters may be considered for a full-time recruiter role when one is open. An internship does not promise later employment.

## What we look for

- **Competitive and coachable.** You want a scoreboard and you want to win it.
- **Resilient on the phone.** Comfortable making 50-plus calls on a full shift and hearing no without losing your energy.
- **Curious.** You like finding out what makes a person or a company tick. Interest in sales, business or construction helps; experience is not required.

## What you never do

Send written outreach without Bridger's yes. Quote pay or fees. Change a stage, owner or record you do not own. Call after 4:00 PM in the lead's time zone. Name a discreet client outside the Team Hub. Record a call. Spend anything.
