A new recruiter follows this alone: once the account exists, nobody at Eden does a setup step for them. Desk live in 5 days, running by day 30, full by day 90.

Kev, 28 Oct 2026: "start building a course in how to set this up for new hires to use to stand up their recruiting desks."

- **Three phases, 27 modules.** Each has a goal, click-path steps, a 2-minute "show me" proof and a time. About 7 hours of setup in week one; the rest of each day is Power Hour shadowing.

## How to read this course

**The self-serve contract.** If a step needs a person, it is a ticket that fires on its own (see `gaps.md`), never a favour you wait for. Stuck for more than 10 minutes: file a ticket that names the module ID (for example "M07 step 3") and keep going with the next module.

**Status tags.** `UNVERIFIED` means a URL, menu name or product step that is not confirmed in Eden's own tenant or any Eden file. Do the step as written; if what you see differs, put what you saw in your Proof Sheet. The first hire's walk-through confirms or corrects every tag and stamps a `last_verified` date.

**What a proof is.** A screenshot or one line in your Proof Sheet. Crop out passwords, codes, API keys, candidate phone numbers, emails, home addresses and anything pay-related. If it is in the picture, retake it.

| Rule for every module | Why |
|---|---|
| Your own login, from the welcome message Kev sends. Passwords never go in a Doc, Sheet, chat or screenshot. | 01 Righteousness (stewardship) |
| Work only inside your Eden account. No candidate data on personal accounts or devices. | Privacy by design |
| A discreet client shows as "Client · discreet" everywhere outside the Team Hub. | Client rule |
| Training here is a resource and a standard to meet, not a management control. Wording is for Docket to confirm (gap C5). | Eden App principle: optional, not managed |
