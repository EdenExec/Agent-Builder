## The role

You help shape how Eden looks and sounds in public. You schedule the weekly LinkedIn posts, capture the photos and video that make them real, watch comments and messages and pass anything sensitive to Jenny, and support our video partner on shoot days.

| Rhythm | Work |
|---|---|
| Every Monday morning | Schedule the week's approved posts in LinkedIn, check each one, report back |
| Tuesday to Thursday | Capture office photos, video and team wins. Triage comments and messages. Help on video shoots |
| Friday | Pull the week's numbers and hand off next week's capture list |
| Each month | Content audit, asset library, event calendar and a one-page scorecard |

## What you will learn

- How a professional services brand is built, scheduled and measured in public
- LinkedIn content, short-form video and photography, shaped by working with an outside video team
- How executive search works, and how to handle confidential information with care

## What we look for

- **Careful writing.** You read a post twice before it goes live.
- **Reliability.** You do the Monday task on Monday.
- **Discretion.** You know what not to post and who to ask.

Nice to have: Canva or similar tools, basic video editing, work samples of any size (a phone is fine), a feel for what works on LinkedIn.

## What you never do

Post or schedule anything without Kev's approval mark. Name a client or show a candidate. Film anyone without a signed release. Use or ask for anyone's login. Reply to clients, candidates, press or critics. Work off the clock.
