# 600 SE 101st · short-term rental brief

## Executive summary

- **Goal:** 600 SE 101st Ave, Vancouver, WA 98664 is legal, insured, furnished and listed as a short-term rental, earning more than it costs, with a local team running it while the family lives in Florida.
- **Owner:** Keys does the research, expense tracking and back-end work; Kev decides, signs and approves every spend, send and publish.
- **First gate:** confirm the jurisdiction and whether Vancouver lets a whole home be rented when the owner lives elsewhere. Nothing is bought until that answer is SETTLED and Docket has reviewed it.

## What Kev asked for

"Hire somebody to start working on turning 600 SE 101st into a VRBO. All research and expense tracking and backend work we'll need to do." (Kev, 19 October 2026.) This is a KDTW Group project.

## Open questions for Kev (ranked)

| # | Question | Keys' assumption until answered |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is the goal: maximise income, cover the carrying cost while keeping the house, or test before deciding to sell or lease long-term? | Cover the carrying cost and keep the house (A) |
| 2 | When does the family move out, and when could the first guest arrive? | Move-out in the next 60 to 90 days; first guest after permits (A) |
| 3 | Who is the local person for emergencies: a full-service manager, a co-host plus cleaner, or someone you know nearby? | Compare all three; no pick until the market read (A) |
| 4 | Whose name is the house in, is there a mortgage, and who is the lender? | Kev's name, with a mortgage (A); Docket reads the occupancy clause |
| 5 | Is the house in an HOA or under recorded covenants? | No HOA; check the title report (A) |
| 6 | Bedrooms, bathrooms, sleeping capacity, parking, and any standout feature? | Pull from the county record first |
| 7 | Short stays only, or open to 30-plus-night guests (nurses, relocations)? | Model both |
| 8 | What furniture stays, and what is the setup budget ceiling? | Most furniture moves; budget set after the pro forma (A) |
| 9 | Who is the current homeowner's insurer? | Unknown; read the policy first |
