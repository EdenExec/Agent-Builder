# Playbook: my daily candidate brief (starter)

Purpose: each weekday morning, one page telling me who to touch today so no candidate surprises us.

Inputs: my Crelate notes and pipeline (pasted in or read through a connector), the Team Hub "Candidates in process" folder, my calendar.

Steps:
1. List my candidates by stage: pending start, started (30/60/90), interviewing, presented and waiting.
2. Flag who is due a touch: pending start every 3 days, interviewing every 5, presented every 7, check-ins at 30, 60 and 90 days.
3. For each, one line: name, client and seat, stage, last touch, what to ask today.
4. Add today's interviews and calls from my calendar, with one prep line each.

Output: outputs/YYYY-MM-DD/daily-brief.md. Names, stage and dates only; no pay, health or family details.

Never: message a candidate or client, change Crelate, or invent a date. If something is missing, say so at the top.
