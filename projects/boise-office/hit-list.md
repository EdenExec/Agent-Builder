# Boise office hit list, draft 3

Direction: Danish, sustainable, sleek and calming. Budget $2,500 to $3,000 for the office, excluding Kev's own desk and furniture. Nick and Bridger bring their own.
All prices below are my planning ranges and are UNVERIFIED. None comes from a page opened this session.

## The room (from Kev)
- Large rectangle. The far wall is all windows. A big glass window sits on the same wall as the door.
- Plan: Kev's desk at one end with two guest chairs, a large conference table for closed-door meetings, then the bullpen and waiting areas.

## The space as found (from Kev's walkthrough video stills, 9 frames, 29 Oct 2026)
- Window wall faces west onto N. Milwaukie St: floor-to-ceiling black-framed glazing with roller shades, trees outside. Expect strong afternoon glare.
- Floors: grey basket-weave carpet tile in the offices and hall; grey plank-look vinyl in the kitchenette.
- Walls: cool grey and white, black baseboards and black door and window frames, warm recessed downlights.
- Interior glass lites in black frames between hall and rooms. Closed-door meetings will be seen, so film matters.
- Kitchenette: white shaker cabinets, light stone-look counter, black pulls.
- Palette is cool and dark at the edges. Warm oak, wool, linen and soft white light will do the calming. Avoid black or grey furniture, which would stack on the room.

## Concern: glass and glare
West glazing means hot, bright afternoons. The existing roller shades help; add sheer linen drapes over them rather than replacing them, and keep the conference table and screens off the direct sun line.

## Concern: privacy
The glass on the door wall means closed-door meetings are visible and audible from the bullpen. Frosted film on the lower two-thirds of that glass costs little and does more for calm and privacy than any piece of furniture. I would put it first. UNVERIFIED cost.

## Sustainability checks
- Buy second-hand first. FSC or PEFC wood where new, low-VOC finishes, and no flat-pack where a used solid piece is available.

## Open questions
1. How many seats in the bullpen, and are they in or outside this budget?
2. Move-in date and lease rules on film, paint and wall fixings.
