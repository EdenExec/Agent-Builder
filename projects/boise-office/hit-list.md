# Boise office hit list, draft 2

Direction: Danish, sustainable, sleek and calming. Budget $2,500 to $3,000 for the office, excluding Kev's own desk and furniture. Nick and Bridger bring their own.
All prices below are my planning ranges and are UNVERIFIED. None comes from a page opened this session.

## The room (from Kev)
- Large rectangle. The far wall is all windows. A big glass window sits on the same wall as the door.
- Plan: Kev's desk at one end with two guest chairs, a large conference table for closed-door meetings, then the bullpen and waiting areas.

## Where I would put the money

| Zone | What | Planning range | How to buy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conference table | Large oak or ash top, simple legs, seats 6 to 8 | $500 to $800 | Used Danish or mid-century first; IKEA as fallback |
| Conference chairs | 6, quiet and comfortable, one family of shape | $400 to $700 | Used office chairs or a matching IKEA set |
| Guest chairs at Kev's desk | 2, wood and wool, the one Danish moment | $300 to $400 | Vintage pair, or a Mater or HAY look-alike |
| Waiting area | Bench or small sofa, side table | $400 to $600 | Secondhand sofa, new linen throw |
| Storage | One low credenza, closed doors | $200 to $300 | IKEA with oak fronts or vintage |
| Rug and acoustics | Wool rug under the table, felt panels | $300 to $450 | Rug first; panels only if the room echoes |
| Window treatment | Linen or sheer curtains or film on the glass wall | $150 to $300 | See concern below |
| Lighting and plants | Warm 2700K lamps, low-light plants | $200 to $300 | IKEA, local nursery |
| Total | | about $2,450 to $3,450 | Trim the sofa or conference chairs to land under $3,000 |

## Concern: glass
The glass on the door wall means closed-door meetings are visible and audible from the bullpen. Frosted film on the lower two-thirds of that glass costs little and does more for calm and privacy than any piece of furniture. I would put it first. UNVERIFIED cost.

## Sustainability checks
- Buy second-hand first. FSC or PEFC wood where new, low-VOC finishes, and no flat-pack where a used solid piece is available.

## Open questions
1. Which direction does the window wall face? West glare changes the curtain and chair choices.
2. How many seats in the bullpen, and are they in or outside this budget?
3. Move-in date and lease rules on film, paint and wall fixings.
