# Boise office hit list, draft 1

Direction: Danish, sustainable, sleek and calming, on a tight budget. Recruiting HQ, so Eden Partner Group.
No prices below are verified. Every figure is UNVERIFIED until a page is opened and dated.

## Approach (my recommendation)
1. Spend on the three things people touch all day: chair, desk, lighting. Go plain on everything else.
2. Buy second-hand Danish and mid-century pieces for storage and meeting tables; buy new for chairs and sit-stand frames.
3. Calm comes from a palette, not from brands: warm white walls, oak or ash, one muted green or clay, soft light.

## Sustainability checks
- FSC or PEFC wood, low-VOC finishes, GREENGUARD-labelled seating where offered.
- Buy second-hand first; ask for a take-back or repair option on chairs.
