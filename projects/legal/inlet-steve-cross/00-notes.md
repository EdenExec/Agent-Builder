## Fee (Kev, 30 Oct)
- Steve gets 50/50 of the fee Eden actually invoices PB South. The draft quotes no rate.
- Why: the Rev Report shows PB South at **18%** (Willard, Ouellette and Navarette, net 45), and the 2025 RPO framework sets 18%. Quoting Steve "20%" would have promised him 10% of base against an 18% invoice.
- PB South's $7,500 monthly retainer, and any retainer credit applied to an invoice, are kept out of the Fee. Steve's half is not cut by credits that come from retainer money Eden already received.

## Tailored for PB South
- **One way.** Eden is client-side and Inlet is candidate-side. Other Eden clients only by a signed addendum.
- **Submittals go through Eden only.** A candidate Steve presents to PB South any other way earns no share. Steve's 21 Oct email pitched a PM straight to Jeff Rosser; the agreement covers candidates sent to Eden since 21 Oct, so that PM counts once Steve sends Eden the resume.
- **Ownership.** Steve has to be first to Eden for the role. If Eden already has the candidate (active in Eden's database within 12 months, or known to PB South), Eden tells him within 2 business days.
- **Non-circumvention.** Steve acknowledges PB South is Eden's client and that he has no pre-existing relationship with them. 12 months after the agreement ends. It also binds Eden: Eden can't go around Steve with his candidates.
- **Payment.** Eden invoices on the start date and pays Steve within 5 business days of receiving PB South's payment. If PB South pays late, the two sides work it out together.
- **Refunds.** If PB South gets a refund or credit, it is shared in proportion to the split. Eden shares PB South's guarantee terms when it sends a job order.
- Confidentiality is built in, so there's no separate NDA. W-9 before the first payment. Washington law.
