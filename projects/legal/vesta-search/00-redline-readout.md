## Risk notes

- **Washington restrictive-covenant law (UNSURE).** RCW 49.62 covers independent contractors, but non-solicitation of customers is carved out of its definition of "noncompetition covenant". The non-solicit here is a narrow client non-solicit between two LLCs, so it is LIKELY fine. Risk register R-010 (the 2027 Washington noncompete bill) is the reason to re-check this clause before 30 June 2027. I haven't verified the bill text this session.
