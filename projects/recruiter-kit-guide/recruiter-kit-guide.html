Your recruiting assistant

Your own Claude, connected to Crelate, that builds Eden submittal packets the way we do them. Screen someone, tell it who and for which job, and it hands you a packet to review. It never sends anything: you review, Kev reviews, you send.

Once · about 15 minutes

Set it up

  1. 1
    Turn on your Crelate API key. In Crelate: top corner → Your Profile & Preferences → API Access → Enable API Access on → copy your key. Keep it to yourself.
  2. 2
    Open Claude Code. Go to claude.ai/code, signed in with your edenexec.com email (never a personal Claude account). Start a new session on the EdenExec/recruiter-kit repository. Can't see it? Ask Kev or Nick to add you on GitHub.
  3. 3
    Save the key in settings, not the chat. In the session's title bar: the cloud environment menu → Edit → Network secrets (or Environment variables). Name it CRELATE_API_KEY, paste the key as the value, save.
  4. 4
    Let it reach Crelate. Same screen, Network access: add app.crelate.com under Allowed domains, leave "Allow package managers" ticked, save.
  5. 5
    Test it. Start a new session (it picks up the key) and paste the first prompt below with a candidate you know. If the record comes back, you're connected. "Blocked" means step 4 isn't saved; "key missing" means step 3, or you're still in the old session.

Keep it one click away

Pin it and come back to it

  1. 1
    Pin this page. In Claude's left sidebar, pin this guide so it sits at the top every time you open Claude.
  2. 2
    Bookmark claude.ai/code in your browser bar. On your phone, open the Claude app and go to Code; your sessions are there too.
  3. 3
    One session per job. New screen or new candidate: start a new session on recruiter-kit. Going back to one? It's in the session list on the left; click it and keep talking.
  4. 4
    Packets go to your Drive, in Submittals / client / job / candidate. Claude makes the folder and tells you to drag the PDF in.

The deal

How we use it

  • Nothing leaves without your yes. Claude drafts; you and Kev review; you send. No emails, uploads to client portals, posts or spending, ZoomInfo credits included.
  • Crelate is read-only for Claude. Stage moves, notes and tags are yours to do in Crelate.
  • Your key, your account. Never share your key, never paste it into a chat, and keep Eden work in your Eden Claude, never a personal account.
  • Desired pay only on a cover page, and no made-up facts: if it isn't in the resume or your notes, it says "confirm with candidate".
  • Discreet clients show as "Client · discreet". No thumbs-up/down feedback on chats with candidate or client details.

Stuck? Tell Claude "I'm stuck on step [n], here's what I see." Or ask Kev or Nick.
