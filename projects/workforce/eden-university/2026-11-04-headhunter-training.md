## Construction concept (15 min): what a VDC Manager actually does
Live today: VMG VDC Manager (Reston) and Pence Kelly layout and VDC.
| | BIM / VDC Coordinator | VDC Manager |
|---|---|---|
| Job | Builds and cleans models, runs clash reports | Owns the BIM execution plan; runs MEP coordination; sets standards across projects |
| In the room | Trade modelers | PM, superintendent, MEP trade leads, sometimes the owner |
| Tools | Revit, Navisworks, ACC/BIM 360 | Same, plus laser scanning, 4D scheduling, field layout (Trimble) |
| Ladder | Coordinator, VDC Engineer | VDC Manager, then Director of VDC |
Ask on the call: "Do you run the coordination meetings or attend them?" · "Who writes the BIM execution plan?" · "How many projects at once?" · "Field layout: do you own it or hand it off?" A "VDC Manager" who only cleans models is a coordinator.

## Recruiting concept (17 min): the offer that goes quiet
Real this month: a pending start took a counter-offer and was saved by a touch call.
1. What it usually means: a counter-offer, a competing offer, or a worry they won't say out loud.
2. First 4 hours: call, then text. Ask an open question: "What would make you hesitate?" Listen.
3. By 24 hours: you know which it is; tell Kev and the client what you heard.
4. Prevent it: pre-close the counter-offer at the screen ("If your boss matches, what do you do?"), and touch every pending start every 3 days until day one.
Role-play (pairs, 5 min): one plays the quiet candidate with a counter in hand; switch.
