2 · Why a news sweep (talking points)
  • News is a reason to call. An award means hiring in 60 to 90 days. A new office means a leader to hire. A layoff or lost bid is the best week to call that firm's people. A leadership move opens a seat.
  • It makes you the consultant. "Here's what we're seeing in your market this month" only works if you know.
  • It feeds the RP count, it doesn't replace it. Every item you act on becomes RPs logged in Crelate. Not logged, didn't happen.
  • Your market only: your states, sectors, top five firms and clients.
  • Open the link before you quote a number.

Set up your construction news sweep

0:08 to 0:30 · laptops
Fill the brackets from your Name Game sheet

Paste this into a new scheduled task The prompt

Every [weeknight at 9pm Pacific / Sunday at 6pm Pacific], run my construction news sweep. I am a recruiter at Eden Partner Group, an executive search firm for commercial construction. I use this to find reasons to call and talking points for client and candidate calls.

My market:
- States and metros: [e.g. South Florida, Texas, DC and Baltimore]
- Sectors: [e.g. data centers and mission-critical, healthcare, high-rise, multifamily]
- Roles I recruit: [e.g. precon managers, estimators, PMs, supers, MEP leaders]
- Firms I recruit from: [your top 5 from the Name Game]
- Firms that are our clients (never recruit from them): [Kev reads the list]

1. Search the web for news from the last [24 hours / 7 days] only: project awards and groundbreakings, GC and CM contract wins, new offices and market entries, leadership hires and departures, layoffs and lost bids, mergers and acquisitions, and new reports on backlog, spending, costs or labor (ENR, ABC, AGC, Dodge, ConstructConnect, the Business Journals, local press).

2. Give me the top [5 / 10] items for my market, most useful first. For each:
   - Headline, date, and the source link
   - Why it matters for hiring, in one line
   - Who to call: the role at the firm (e.g. "VP Precon at <firm>"), never a phone number or email
   - One talking line I can use on a call, under 25 words

3. Then give me:
   - "3 numbers this week": three market numbers, each with source and date
   - "My intel line": one number, one observation and one recommendation I can give my top client this week

Rules:
- Every item has a link and a date inside the window. If you can't find a date, leave it out.
- If a firm on the client list appears, mark it "Client: don't recruit" and use it only as client intel. Never mention one client's news in a line meant for another client.
- Moss is a client: referral asks only. Baker is a client: mark "Client · discreet".
- No personal details about anyone: no age, family, health, pay or home location.
- Never send, post or email anything. Just give me the brief.
- If nothing new is worth calling about, say "quiet night" in one line and stop.

  1. Open Claude (app or claude.ai). Turn on web search. Optional: Google Drive, to save each brief to your folder 04 Autopsies and notes.
  2. Open Scheduled tasks, new task, name it "Construction news sweep".
  3. Set the schedule from the table below. Never Saturday.
  4. Paste the prompt and fill the five lines in brackets.
  5. Hit Run now once, here at the table.

The Must Do submittal packet

0:30 to 0:55 · laptops
Nobody sends anything today

Talking points Kev · 5 min

  • Why: clients want better quality, better match and more references. The packet shows the match before they read the resume.
  • No Must Dos, no scorecard. Every JO gets 7 to 10 from the intake call. If the client has none, we draft them and get approval first.
  • Kev reads every packet before it goes. Hand it over with "Review before it goes to the client." Kev sends.
  • Pay: desired only (base, bonus, vehicle, PTO). Never current base unless Kev says so.
  • Log the submittal in Crelate after it goes. That's where the scorecard counts it.
The packet, in order
#Part
1Eden cover page: title, years, location, desired pay, availability, interviewing elsewhere, concerns
2Must Do scorecard: each rated meets, partial or gap, one line of evidence
3Original resume, untouched
4References (consent line) and screening notes

A · Crelate in Claude Once · 10 min

  1. Get your own Crelate API key (account settings, or Kev issues one).
  2. Open claude.ai/code, start a session on EdenExec/Agent-Builder.
  3. Environment settings: add a secret named exactly CRELATE_API_KEY. Under network access allow app.crelate.com. Never paste the key in the chat.
  4. New session: "Read projects/recruiter-desk/crelate-in-claude.md and use Crelate read-only."
  5. Test: "What did I log this week: calls, submittals, first interviews?"
After Claude hands you the draft
  1. Fill the gaps with the candidate: desired pay, availability, reference consent.
  2. Run it again without --draft. It refuses until the checklist passes.
  3. Hand the PDF to Kev. Kev reads it and sends.
  4. Save to Drive: Submittals / Client / JO / Candidate. Log the submittal in Crelate.

B · Build a packet Per candidate

Build a submittal packet for [candidate name] on the [client] [role] JO.
1. Read projects/team-hub/submittal-packet/workflow.md and follow it.
2. From Crelate, read-only: pull the candidate's title, years, location and past states, their resume attachment, and my screening notes on this JO.
3. Use the approved Must Dos for this JO: [paste them, or the file in projects/team-hub/must-dos/].
4. Fill packet-template.json outside the repo, rate each Must Do meets, partial or gap with one line of evidence from my notes, and run npm run submittal -- <file> --draft.
5. Tell me exactly what's missing (desired pay, availability, reference consent, any Must Do with no evidence) and hand me the PDF.
Never commit the packet or the filled JSON. Desired pay only. No other client's name in the packet.
The drill · 10 min

Pick one live candidate you've screened. Build a DRAFT packet and the list of what's missing before the wrap.
