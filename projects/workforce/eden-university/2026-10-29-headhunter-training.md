## 2 · The construction news sweep (22 min)

### Talking points for Kev (5 min)

- **News is a reason to call.** A project award means hiring in 60 to 90 days. A new office means a leader to hire. A layoff or a lost bid is the best week to call that firm's people. A leadership move means a seat opened somewhere.
- **It makes you the consultant.** "Before we get into the role, here's what we're seeing in your market this month" only works if you actually know. The sweep hands you the line.
- **It feeds the RP count, it does not replace it.** The sweep is not activity. Every item you act on turns into RPs logged in Crelate, on the active JO or the lead bucket. If it isn't logged, it didn't happen.
- **Your market, not the whole country.** Each of you sets the sweep to your states, your sectors, your top five firms (from your Name Game sheet) and your clients.
- **Check before you quote.** Every item comes with a link and a date. If you quote a number to a client, you've opened the link.

### Nightly or weekly (Kev's call per person)

| | Nightly | Weekly |
|---|---|---|
| When | Weeknights 9:00pm PT, ready for Power Hour | Sunday 6:00pm PT (never Saturday) |
| Window | Last 24 hours (Monday's run covers Fri to Sun) | Last 7 days |
| Best for | Recruiters on a live, competitive search | Recruiters building a book, BD calls |
| Length | Top 5 items | Top 10 items plus "3 numbers this week" |

Recommendation (A): nightly for anyone on a retained search (PB South, Triton); weekly for everyone else to start. Everyone can switch after two weeks.

### Setup, laptops open (12 min)

1. Open Claude (the app or claude.ai). Turn on **web search**. Optional: turn on **Google Drive** so it saves each brief to your Eden folder `04 Autopsies and notes`.
2. Open **Scheduled tasks**, create a new one, and name it "Construction news sweep".
3. Set the schedule from the table above. Never Saturday.
4. Paste the prompt below and fill the five lines in brackets from your Name Game sheet.
5. Hit **Run now** once at the table, so we all see a brief before we leave.

### The prompt to paste

```
Every [weeknight at 9pm Pacific / Sunday at 6pm Pacific], run my construction news sweep. I am a recruiter at Eden Partner Group, an executive search firm for commercial construction. I use this to find reasons to call and talking points for client and candidate calls.

My market:
- States and metros: [e.g. South Florida, Texas, DC and Baltimore]
- Sectors: [e.g. data centers and mission-critical, healthcare, high-rise, multifamily]
- Roles I recruit: [e.g. precon managers, estimators, PMs, supers, MEP leaders]
- Firms I recruit from: [your top 5 from the Name Game]
- Firms that are our clients (never recruit from them): [Kev gives you this list]

1. Search the web for news from the last [24 hours / 7 days] only: project awards and groundbreakings, GC and CM contract wins, new offices and market entries, leadership hires and departures, layoffs and lost bids, mergers and acquisitions, and new reports on backlog, spending, costs or labor (ENR, ABC, AGC, Dodge, ConstructConnect, the Business Journals, local press).

2. Give me the top [5 / 10] items for my market, most useful first. For each:
   - Headline, date, and the source link
   - Why it matters for hiring, in one line
   - Who to call: the role at the firm (e.g. "VP Precon at <firm>"), never a phone number or email
   - One talking line I can use on a call, under 25 words

3. Then give me:
   - "3 numbers this week": three market numbers, each with source and date
   - "My intel line": one number, one observation and one recommendation I can give my top client this week

Rules:
- Every item has a link and a date inside the window. If you can't find a date, leave it out.
- If a firm on the client list appears, mark it "Client: don't recruit" and use it only as client intel. Never mention one client's news in a line meant for another client.
- Moss is a client: referral asks only. Baker is a client: mark "Client · discreet".
- No personal details about anyone: no age, family, health, pay or home location.
- Never send, post or email anything. Just give me the brief.
- If nothing new is worth calling about, say "quiet night" in one line and stop.
```

### The drill (5 min, at the table)

Each recruiter reads one item from their first run out loud and turns it into an action: "That's three RPs to <role> at <firm> in Power Hour tomorrow." Kev picks the best line in the room.

## 3 · The Must Do submittal packet (25 min)

Source: `projects/team-hub/submittal-packet/workflow.md` (Kev, 24 and 27 Oct). Command: `npm run submittal`.

### Talking points for Kev (5 min)

- **Why:** clients are asking for better quality, better match and more references. The packet shows the match on paper before they read the resume.
- **The packet, in order:** (1) Eden cover page: title, years, location, desired pay, availability, interviewing elsewhere, concerns. (2) The Must Do scorecard: the client's 7 to 10 Must Dos, each rated with one line of evidence from our screen. (3) Their original resume, untouched. (4) References (with the consent line) and our screening notes.
- **No Must Dos, no scorecard.** Every JO gets 7 to 10 Must Dos from the intake call. If the client has none, we draft them and get approval first. Examples on file: the three PB South lists in `projects/team-hub/must-dos/`.
- **I read every packet before it goes.** Build it, hand it to me with "Review before it goes to the client." I send. Then you log the submittal in Crelate. That log is where the scorecard counts it.
- **Pay:** desired pay only (base, bonus, vehicle, PTO). Never current base unless I say so.

### What Kev does today, and what the guys do (the automated pull)

Kev pulls the candidate record, resume and notes out of Crelate with Claude and the packet builds itself. The guys get the same setup in two parts.

**Part A · Crelate in Claude (once, 10 min).** Follow `projects/recruiter-desk/crelate-in-claude.md`:
1. Your own Crelate API key (Kev issues it if it's not in your account settings).
2. Claude Code on the web (https://claude.ai/code), repo `EdenExec/Agent-Builder` (Kev adds you).
3. Environment settings: secret named exactly `CRELATE_API_KEY`; allow `app.crelate.com` under network access. Never paste the key in the chat.
4. New session: "Read projects/recruiter-desk/crelate-in-claude.md and use Crelate read-only." Test: "What did I log this week: calls, submittals, first interviews?"

**Part B · Build a packet (per candidate, 5 to 10 min).** In that session, say:

```
Build a submittal packet for [candidate name] on the [client] [role] JO.
1. Read projects/team-hub/submittal-packet/workflow.md and follow it.
2. From Crelate, read-only: pull the candidate's title, years, location and past states, their resume attachment, and my screening notes on this JO.
3. Use the approved Must Dos for this JO: [paste them, or the file in projects/team-hub/must-dos/].
4. Fill packet-template.json outside the repo, rate each Must Do meets, partial or gap with one line of evidence from my notes, and run npm run submittal -- <file> --draft.
5. Tell me exactly what's missing (desired pay, availability, reference consent, any Must Do with no evidence) and hand me the PDF.
Never commit the packet or the filled JSON. Desired pay only. No other client's name in the packet.
```

Then the recruiter fills the gaps from the candidate (pay, availability, consent), runs it once more without `--draft` (it refuses until the checklist passes), and hands the PDF to Kev. After Kev's yes: Kev sends, the recruiter saves it to Drive `Submittals / <Client> / <JO> / <Candidate>` and logs the submittal in Crelate.

**Kev, before tomorrow:** paste me the exact prompt you use for your Crelate pull. If yours differs from Part B, the guys should use yours word for word, so every packet comes out the same.

### The drill (10 min, at the table)

Each recruiter picks one live candidate they've screened and builds a draft packet. Goal: a DRAFT PDF on every screen before the wrap, and a list of what's missing. Nobody sends anything today.
