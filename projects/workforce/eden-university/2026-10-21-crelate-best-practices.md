# Wednesday Headhunter Training · Wed 21 Oct 2026 · Crelate best practices

Kev leads. 15 minutes, laptops open at the table.

Kev, 20 Oct 2026: "The growth of the company depends on being operationally excellent in Crelate. We can sell RPOs if our JOs are all being worked effectively. Let's take 15 mins with our laptops at the table to make sure we are pushing activity to the right jobs. Let's make sure the JO boards are all consistent."

## Why (Kev, 2 min)
- Clients buy RPO when they can see every job order being worked. Crelate is that proof.
- Activity logged to the wrong job, or to no job, is work the client never sees and we can't bill for.
- Today's goal: every recruiter leaves with clean boards on their active JOs.

## Laptops (10 min)
1. **Open your active JOs.** Use the Hub's Job Orders folder for who's on what. Check that each JO is open in Crelate with you on it.
2. **Push activity to the right job.** This week's RPs, PTCs, screens and submittals should each be logged against the JO they were for. Re-link anything filed to the contact only or to the wrong job.
3. **Make the board consistent.** Check each JO against the list below.

| Check | Standard |
|---|---|
| Stages | Same stage names on every JO, in pipeline order |
| Every candidate | Sits in exactly one stage, current as of today |
| Owner | Recruiter set on the JO and on each candidate in it |
| Next step | Every active candidate has a next step and a date |
| Notes | Every submittal carries the why-submitted note the client sees in the portal |

## Close (3 min)
- Each recruiter names the one fix they'll finish by end of day.
- Nick audits the boards Friday. Dean folds the result into Friday's scorecard.
- Dean records whether the session ran and who fixed what in the Wednesday training log.
