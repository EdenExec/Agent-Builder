# Bottom line: not defensible. Hire Sales Interns as W-2 part-time hourly employees.

## Executive summary

Trained, scheduled, supervised, in-office interns using Eden's tools on Eden's searches are employees under every test that applies: DOL, IRS, Idaho unemployment and Idaho workers' comp. A stipend paid for for-profit work is the classic sign of an employee, not a contractor. The compliant structure costs Eden roughly the employer payroll tax (7.65%) plus Idaho unemployment and workers' comp premiums. Misclassification costs that same amount anyway, plus penalties, interest, and an audit that would reach the existing 1099 recruiting team.

## 1. The four tests, applied to the Sales Intern facts

| Test | What decides it | Sales Intern facts | Result | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **DOL, FLSA economic reality** | 2026 proposal (91 FR 9932, 27 Feb 2026, not final as of 23 Oct 2026). Two core factors: control over the work, and opportunity for profit or loss through initiative or investment. If both point the same way, that is very likely the answer. Field staff currently apply Fact Sheet #13 (2008); the 2024 rule (29 CFR 795) is still on the books for lawsuits. | Eden sets hours, assigns searches, trains, sets call goals, supervises weekly and supplies LinkedIn, the CRM and the database. The intern invests nothing and cannot lose money. Earning more commission by working more hours is not "profit or loss". | **Employee** under the 2024 rule, the 2026 proposal and Fact Sheet #13 alike. The two core factors both point to employee. | LIKELY (rule status from DOL rulemaking page and law-firm summaries) |
| **IRS common law** | Behavioral, financial and relationship control (irs.gov, "Independent contractor (self-employed) or employee?"). | Behavioral: Eden instructs and trains. Financial: no investment, no business expenses, no other clients. Relationship: ongoing, integral to Eden's core business, with a stated path to full time. | **Employee** | SETTLED (framework); the application to these facts is clear |
| **Idaho unemployment** (Idaho Code 72-1316(4)) | Paid services count as covered employment **unless Eden shows both** that the worker is free from control or direction **and** that the worker runs an independently established trade or business. | Fails both. A student being trained has no independent recruiting business. | **Employee** | SETTLED |
| **Idaho workers' comp** (Idaho Code 72-102(16)) | A contractor is controlled "as to the result of his work only and not as to the means". | Eden controls the means: scripts, tools, call counts and schedule. | **Employee** | SETTLED |

**Section 530 safe harbour will not rescue it.** Relief requires treating similar workers consistently (IRS, "Worker reclassification – Section 530 relief"). Eden will pay Client Success interns as W-2 employees for overlapping work (sourcing, outreach), which fails "substantive consistency". There is also no reasonable basis: no prior audit, no case law, no industry practice. **LIKELY.**

## 2. Realistic risk if Eden used 1099s anyway

**Most likely trigger.** An intern files for Idaho unemployment when the semester ends. The Idaho Department of Labor finds no wage record and audits. The auditor asks who else is paid on a 1099, and that question reaches the current 1099 recruiting team (already on the risk register).

| Exposure | Rule | Illustration: one Sales Intern, one year |
|---|---|---|
| Employer FICA never paid | 7.65% of wages (IRS Pub. 15) | $1,530 |
| Employee FICA and income tax Eden failed to withhold, at the reduced rate for unintentional errors | 20% of employee FICA plus 1.5% of wages (26 USC 3509(a)) | $606 (rising to $1,212 at the doubled 3509(b) rates if information returns were not filed) |
| FUTA and Idaho unemployment tax | Eden's rates, plus interest and penalties | [Joel to supply Eden's Idaho rate] |
| Workers' comp | An injured, uncovered worker can claim full benefits from Eden plus 10% (Idaho Code 72-210). Failing to insure is a misdemeanor, including for the managing member (72-319). | Uncapped. One back injury can exceed the year's payroll. |
| Wages | FLSA back pay plus an equal amount as liquidated damages, and the worker's attorney fees (29 USC 216(b)); 2 years back, 3 if willful (29 USC 255(a)). DOL civil penalty up to $2,515 per repeated or willful violation (29 CFR 578.3; the 2025 figure, unchanged for 2026). | Small unless overtime or commission went unpaid. |
| Commission | Unpaid commissions are wages. A court may award 3x the unpaid amount plus fees (Idaho Code 45-601, 45-615). | 3x any disputed commission |

The illustration assumes a [rate] of $15 an hour, 1,000 hours and $5,000 of commission, so $20,000 of pay. These figures are assumptions only, sent to Tally through the numbers gate. Across five interns, the base-case tax alone is about $10,680. The money is modest. The real costs are the audit spotlight, an uninsured injury and the precedent it sets for the existing 1099 team.

## 3. The structure I recommend

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Status and pay | W-2, part-time, non-exempt, hourly at [rate], at or above $7.25 (Idaho Code 44-1502(1)); every hour paid, training included |
| Time and overtime | Gusto timekeeping with weekly supervisor approval; 1.5x the regular rate over 40 hours, commission included (29 CFR 778.117) |
| Commission | Written plan (Exhibit A), signed before day one; earned only when the fee is collected and the guarantee has passed, so there are no clawbacks or deductions (45-609); paid the next regular payday |
| Never | Draws, stipends, commission-only weeks |
| Term and setup | Fixed end date, renewable in writing; workers' comp and Idaho unemployment in the hiring entity's name (Joel confirms) |

**Overtime example (gate item).** One summer week: 45 hours at $15, plus $450 of commission paid for that week.
Regular rate = (45 x $15 + $450) / 45 = $1,125 / 45 = $25.00.
Overtime premium = 0.5 x $25.00 x 5 = $62.50. Pay for the week = $1,187.50, not $1,125.

## 4. Offer letter differences at a glance

| Term | Sales Intern | Hourly Intern (Operations, Client Success, Marketing) |
|---|---|---|
| Title | Sales Intern (Jr. Headhunter) | [Pathway] Intern |
| Pay | Hourly [rate] **plus** commission under Exhibit A | Hourly [rate] only |
| Commission or bonus | Written plan: earned when the fee is collected and the guarantee has passed | None promised. "Eden may award discretionary bonuses." No criteria announced. |
| Overtime | Regular rate includes commission | Regular rate is the hourly rate (plus any non-discretionary pay) |
| At separation | Earned commission paid per Idaho Code 45-606. Unearned commission is not owed (counsel to confirm). | Final wages per 45-606 |
| Systems access | CRM, LinkedIn Recruiter, database | Role-limited. Operations gets no access to screening report contents. |
| Restrictive terms | Confidentiality and a candidate/client non-solicit for [months], limited to people the intern worked with | Confidentiality only |

## TEMPLATE A: Sales Intern offer letter

> EDEN PARTNER GROUP
> ***Offer of Employment: Sales Intern (Jr. Headhunter)***

[Date]

[Name]
[Address]

Dear [First name],

Eden Partner Group LLC (**"Eden"**) is pleased to offer you a paid internship as a **Sales Intern (Jr. Headhunter)** in our Boise, Idaho office, on the terms below.

**1. Position.** You will report to [supervisor name, title]. You will source candidates for Eden's searches, make outbound calls and record your work in Eden's CRM. You will work in person at [office address].

**2. Employment status.** You will be a part-time, non-exempt, W-2 employee of Eden. You are not an independent contractor. Eden will withhold taxes and cover you under its workers' compensation insurance.

**3. Term.** The internship begins on [start date] and ends on [end date] (the **"Internship Term"**), unless it ends earlier under section 10. It may be extended only in a writing signed by Eden.

**4. Hours.** You will work about [hours] hours a week on a schedule agreed with your supervisor and built around your classes. You must record all time worked in Eden's timekeeping system, and you may not work off the clock. You need your supervisor's written approval before working more than 40 hours in a workweek.

**5. Pay.** Eden will pay you **$[rate] per hour** for all hours worked, including training, meetings and calls. Eden pays [weekly / biweekly / semi-monthly] on [payday rule]. Overtime is paid at one and one-half times your regular rate for hours over 40 in a workweek, as federal law requires. Your regular rate includes commission.

**6. Commission.** You may also earn commission under the **Sales Intern Commission Plan** attached as Exhibit A (the **"Plan"**). The Plan governs when commission is earned and paid. If this letter and the Plan conflict, the Plan controls on commission. Eden may change the Plan for future periods by giving you [30] days' written notice. A change never reduces commission you have already earned.

**7. Training.** You will attend Eden's weekly headhunter training. It is paid working time.

**8. Academic credit.** If your school offers credit for this internship, Eden will complete the school's employer paperwork. Credit is decided by your school. Your pay and duties are the same whether or not you receive credit.

**9. Confidentiality.** Before you start, you must sign Eden's Confidentiality and Non-Solicitation Agreement. It protects candidate, client and business information during and after your internship.

**10. At-will employment.** Your employment is at will. You or Eden may end it at any time, with or without cause or notice. Nothing in this letter, in a posting or in any conversation promises employment for a set period or a full-time role after the Internship Term. Strong interns may be considered for open full-time roles, but no role is guaranteed. Only a writing signed by [Kev Williams, Managing Member] can change this section.

**11. Conditions.** This offer depends on proof of your eligibility to work in the United States (Form I-9) and on your signing the agreement in section 9. [If a background check applies: It also depends on a background check that you authorise on a separate disclosure form.]

**12. Whole agreement.** This letter, Exhibit A and the agreement in section 9 are the entire agreement on these terms. They replace any earlier statements, including job postings.

Please sign and return this letter by [date].

Eden Partner Group LLC

By: ______________________ [Name, title]   Date: __________

Accepted: ______________________ [Name]   Date: __________

### Exhibit A: Sales Intern Commission Plan (skeleton; figures to Tally, terms to counsel)

| Term | Wording |
|---|---|
| **Qualifying Placement** | A candidate whom you sourced first, as shown in the CRM with a date stamp, who starts work for an Eden client under a fee agreement during the Internship Term or within [days] days after it. |
| **Commission rate** | [percent]% of the **Net Fee** (the fee Eden collects from the client, less refunds, credits and third-party splits). [Or a flat $[amount] per Qualifying Placement.] |
| **Earned** | Commission is earned only when both (a) Eden has collected the Net Fee in full and (b) the client's guarantee period for that placement has ended without a refund or credit. |
| **Paid** | On the first regular payday at least [7] days after the commission is earned. |
| **Fall-off** | If the candidate leaves before the guarantee period ends, no commission is earned on that placement. Nothing is deducted from your pay. |
| **Activity bonus** (optional) | $[amount] for each [defined activity, for example a candidate interview that the client confirms], paid on the next regular payday. It is non-discretionary and counts in your overtime regular rate. |
| **Splits** | Where two or more Eden people source the same placement, the split is [rule], recorded in the CRM before the client submittal. |
| **After separation** | Commission earned on or before your last day is paid with your final pay (Idaho Code 45-606). Commission earned after your last day on a Qualifying Placement is paid when earned under this Plan. [Counsel to confirm the tail period.] |
| **Changes** | Only prospectively, with [30] days' written notice. Never reduces commission already earned. |
| **Disputes** | Raise them in writing to [title] within [days] days of the pay statement. Eden answers in writing within [days] days. |
