### Pathway A: Operations Intern (Executive and Administrative Support)

**Hourly, [rate range] · Boise, in person · Reports to the Head of Operations**

You keep the business running. You support three leaders across operations, sales and recruiting, and you take the one-off tasks off their plates so they can stay on the phone with clients and candidates.

What you will do:
- Schedule meetings, book travel and set up calls and follow-ups with candidates and clients
- Run new-hire logistics: background check and drug test submissions and verification, onboarding paperwork, first-week setup
- Support our 90-day onboarding process, early-employment check-ins and employee scorecards
- Act as liaison with insurance and other vendors
- Help with lead generation and quality checks on our lead lists
- Take on whatever comes up, and figure it out

What you look like:
- A self-starter who needs little direction: given the goal and the facts, you find a way and try a second way if the first one fails
- Organized and calm with a full calendar and three people asking for things
- Discreet with personal and confidential information
- Strong writer and confident on the phone
- Comfortable with Google Workspace or Microsoft Office; curious about AI tools

Where it can go: the intern who owns onboarding and our 90-day process can grow into an Operations Coordinator role.

### Pathway B: Sales Intern (Jr. Headhunter)

**Training stipend [rate] per hour plus commission · Boise, in person · Reports to a senior recruiter**

You learn executive search by doing it. Half your time is research, finding the right people for live searches; half is on the phone, getting them interested. When your work leads to a placement, you earn on it.

What you will do:
- Source candidates for live construction leadership searches using LinkedIn, our database and research tools
- Make outbound calls to candidates: introduce the role, qualify interest and book the next conversation
- Log every call and contact cleanly in our CRM
- Join our weekly headhunter training and work your own call goals

How you earn:
- An hourly training stipend for every hour you work
- Commission on activity and placements you source, under a written plan: [commission terms]

What you look like:
- Competitive and coachable; you want a scoreboard and you want to win it
- Comfortable making 50-plus calls a day and hearing no without losing your energy
- Curious: you like finding out what makes a person or a company tick
- Interest in sales, business or construction is a plus; experience is not required

Where it can go: the best Jr. Headhunters move into a full-time recruiter seat with a full commission plan.

### Pathway C: Communications and Client Success Intern

**Hourly, [rate range], plus performance incentives · Boise, in person · Supports our top recruiters**

You are the right hand on our busiest desks. You take on the work that keeps searches moving, so our top recruiters spend their day with clients and candidates. Think sales coordinator for an executive search team.

What you will do:
- Manage inbound messages and InMails, and run structured outreach campaigns
- Source resumes and build candidate lists for live searches
- Prepare candidates for interviews and follow up after every call and interview
- Build and send candidate submittals to each client's guidelines
- Keep our CRM and data clean, and use AI tools to speed up research and writing
- Support our operations leaders on team communication and projects

What you look like:
- Clear, warm and fast in writing; detail matters to you
- Organized enough to support two or three busy people at once
- Comfortable on the phone with senior professionals
- Curious about AI and quick with new software

Where it can go: strong interns can step into a Jr. Headhunter seat with commission.

### Pathway D: Marketing Intern

**Hourly, [rate range] · Boise, in person · Reports to our communications and design lead**

You help shape how Eden looks and sounds. You create the posts, graphics, decks and brand pieces our team uses to win clients and attract great candidates.

What you will do:
- Coordinate our social media calendar and draft posts for review
- Design graphics, one-pagers and event materials inside our brand guidelines
- Build and refresh sales decks and client presentations
- Create content: short videos, photos and written pieces on our team and our work
- Keep our brand assets organized and consistent

What you look like:
- A portfolio, even a small one: class projects, freelance work or your own accounts
- Skilled in Canva, Adobe or Figma; video editing is a plus
- A clean, professional eye and a good writer
- Takes feedback well and turns revisions quickly

To apply for this pathway, include a link to your portfolio or three work samples.
