## Lines to keep exactly as written

Docket rewrote these lines for legal reasons. Please don't change them back.

| Keep | Instead of | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Open to anyone starting or building a career in business. Students are welcome" | "Current students or recent graduates" | Age-coded wording in an ad violates federal age-discrimination rules |
| "Hourly pay ... for every hour worked, including training" | "Stipend" | Interns are paid hourly W-2 employees, never 1099 or stipend |
| "Commission under Eden's written ... Commission Plan" | Commission terms, "uncapped", "every placement" | Terms in an ad become a promise; the plan is TBD |
| "Strong interns may be considered for open roles. An internship does not promise later employment." | "A path to full time" | A written promise of a job can become a contract |
| No bonus or "performance incentives" line | "Plus performance incentives" | An announced incentive is wages and raises overtime pay |
| "You will not see report contents or results" | Interns verifying background checks or drug tests | Screening results stay with leaders (FCRA and privacy) |
| "Leaders write and own every evaluation" | The intern owns scorecards and 90-day evals | Confidentiality |

## Three rules for everyone, from the training

1. Every intern is a paid, hourly W-2 employee of Eden Partner Group. Pay every hour, track every hour, nothing off the clock.
2. Promise nothing in a posting, Slack, text or conversation that is not in a signed letter or plan: no jobs, no bonuses, no commission terms.
3. Describe the work, never the person: no age, gender, faith or family questions or preferences.
