# Friday scorecards as Google Forms

Two forms for the Friday Round Table (Kev, 5 Nov 2026; first run Fri 6 Nov, 10am PT). Content owner: Dean. Script: Q. DRAFT for Kev and Nick's review tonight. Nothing is sent or shared by building it.

- **Recruiter scorecard:** the 43-box card as a form. 35 daily boxes (a grid, Mon to Fri) and 8 weekly boxes, the Seven Pillars rated 1 to 5, win of the week, what got in the way, one change, anything needed from Kev or Nick. Names: Max, Evan, Noah, Andrew, Bridger, Kunji, Kyle.
- **Leadership scorecard:** for Nick and Kev. Team work done (one-on-ones, Round Table, unblocking, training, cards), who is rising or needs help, new JOs, client moves, the one deal to push, the pillars, decisions, next week's priority.
- Crelate counts (RPs, PTCs and so on) are not asked. Dean's Friday pull has them.

## Set it up (about 5 minutes, from kev@edenexec.com)

1. Open https://script.google.com, click New project, paste `build-scorecard-forms.gs`, and click Save.
2. Choose `buildScorecardForms`, click Run and approve the permissions. The execution log prints each form's edit link and send link, and the one responses Sheet ("Eden · Friday scorecards (responses)", a tab per form).
3. Brand each form: palette icon, custom colour `#151515` (Eden ink), the lightest grey background, Montserrat if offered. The pre-screen's `../../hiring/prescreen-form/header.png` works as the header.
4. Send: Kev sends the recruiter link to the team and the leadership link to Nick, his call on when. The same links work every Friday.

## Notes

- Email is collected, and people can edit their answers after submitting.
- The responses Sheet stays in Kev's Drive. Dean reads it on Friday afternoon into the Round Table pack in place of the Eden Desk card, if Kev chooses the form over the Desk card.
- No questions about health, family, age or pay.
