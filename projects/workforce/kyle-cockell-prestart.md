## What the assessments are for

- **Self-awareness first.** The Enneagram, Kolbe and Working Genius give Kyle and his future manager a shared language for how he works under pressure, where he gets energy and where he needs a partner.
- **Team fit second.** They show where Kyle complements Max, Evan, Noah, Andrew and Bridger, and what a good first coach looks like. Results go to the coaching plan, not the score.
- **Never a filter.** None of the three is validated for hiring decisions, and a pass/fail use invites bias and legal exposure. Kyle shares his results in his own words; nobody is "the wrong type."

## Culture interview guide

Ask every question in the same words and ask for one real example. Score 1 to 5 (1 = red flag, 3 = adequate, 5 = strong with specifics), each interviewer alone before comparing. Built on the Seven Pillars (Kev, 20 Oct 2026). Because Kyle has recruited before, ask for examples from real desks and real numbers.

**Legal guardrail.** Assess behaviors and values in action only. Do not ask about, note or score religious belief, faith practice, age, family status or health, including mental health. Righteousness and Transparency are scored on conduct, never on belief or church.

If Kyle volunteers any of it, do not write it down. If it matters to the work, record "Kev to discuss in person" and nothing more. **Docket should review this question set before use.**

| Pillar | Ask | Strong answer sounds like | Red flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 Righteousness (stewardship) | Tell me about a time you were trusted with something that was not yours: a client, a candidate, a boss's name. What did you do to protect it? | Names what he protected, owns the details, did more than was asked | Talks about credit, stays vague, blames |
| 01 Righteousness | Describe a time doing the right thing cost you something, such as a deal or a relationship. | A real cost, took it without bitterness | No example, or "everyone cuts corners" |
| 02 Winning | Tell me about a stretch when results were flat for weeks. What did you do each day? | Kept the routine, changed the method, never stopped | Quit, blamed the market, lost the routine |
| 02 Winning | What are the rules of your game, and how did you decide where to spend your time on your last desk? | Knows the rules and his daily and weekly numbers, a clear strategy | "I just work hard"; cannot give numbers |
| 03 Accountability | Tell me about a mistake that was yours. Who knew, and when did they find out? | Told early, fixed it, changed a habit | Blames, late disclosure, spin |
| 03 Accountability | When did you commit to a person and a time and it got hard to keep? What did you do? | Showed up or warned early, followed through | Chronic lateness with reasons |
| 04 Discipline | What do you do every working day that nobody checks? | A specific routine kept for months | Works only when watched |
| 04 Discipline | Tell me about a task you disliked and how you did it anyway. | Same quality as the work he likes | Rushed it, avoided it, delegated it away |
| 05 Transparency | Tell me about a time you told someone what they did not want to hear. | Direct, kind, said to their face, specific | Avoided it, went around, or was harsh without care |
| 05 Transparency | Tell me about hard feedback someone gave you to your face. How did you take it? | Thanked them, changed something, shows it | Defensive, discounts the source |
| 06 Growth | What have you read, heard or practiced in the last 90 days to get better at recruiting, and what did you change? | A named source and a changed behavior | Nothing recent; "I learn on the job" |
| 06 Growth | What is something you taught yourself from scratch outside your job? | A process: sources, practice, then used it | Cannot name one |
| 07 Execution | How do you plan a week? What happens when Tuesday goes off the rails? | A written plan, evening calendar, recovers fast | Inbox-driven, no system |
| 07 Execution | Tell me about something you carried to the finish after the excitement was gone. | Follow-through details, the last 10% done | Starts strong, trails off |
