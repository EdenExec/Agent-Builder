## The three layers

| Layer | Who | Owns |
|---|---|---|
| Operators | Bridger, Noah (with Kev) | Run the agents day to day: work the Command Center, turn the agents' ideas and questions into yes/no asks for Kev, and keep the machine fed |
| Execution | The recruiting team (Max, Evan, Andrew, and Kyle once he starts) | RPs, MPs, screens, submittals, closes. Agents hand them lead lists, depth charts and drafts; they make the calls |
| Leadership | Nick | Hourly staffing (Eden Staffing), high-level recruiting, team accountability (scorecards with Dean, the Round Table) |

## The operator role (proposed)

**Daily, about 45 minutes, split morning and afternoon:**
1. Open the Command Center. Clear anything an agent is blocked on that doesn't need Kev (find a file, confirm a fact, chase a recruiter).
2. Read the agents' new ideas and questions. Group them, kill the weak ones, and put the best two or three in front of Kev as one-tap asks, each with the agent's recommendation.
3. Feed the machine: new job orders to Casey, lead-list asks to Scout, client follow-ups to Jenny. Tell Radar what changed.

**Weekly:**
- Monday: check each agent's two or three improvement ideas from last week and report which ones Kev said yes to and whether they shipped.
- Friday: the credit dashboard (Eden Credit Burn). Flag any paid tool that's behind pace and suggest work to use it.

**What operators don't do:** approve spend, send client email, sign anything or move a QC item. Those stay with Kev. Operators prepare; Kev decides.

**Split between the two (proposed):** Noah takes the recruiting agents (Scout, Casey, Dean, the Team Hub); Bridger takes the back office (Q, Tally, Docket, Pam, the Command Center itself). They cover for each other.

## What it changes

- Bridger and Noah's own recruiting desks: does their RP target drop while they operate, and by how much? (Kev's call; their scorecard would need an operator row.)
- Nick's seat: "Leadership · Operations" becomes hourly staffing + high-level recruiting + accountability. Crelate admin and account set-up for new hires would move to the operators.
- Agents bring ideas weekly (added to every agent's standing duties on 25 Oct), so the operators have a steady queue to work.
