# DRAFT FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW

## Executive summary

**Verdict: SIGN after the five MUST edits (now applied). Counsel reads it once.** Confidence: LIKELY.

- **It now sits cleanly beside PB South.** The NDA adds to every split agreement and never amends one. On anything a split agreement covers, the split agreement controls. Both papers use Washington law and Washington courts, so a dispute can't split across two forums.
- **The real exposure was "accept".** RCW 49.62.010(4) treats a clause that "directly or indirectly prohibits the acceptance or transaction of business with a customer" as a noncompete. That is current law, not just ESHB 1155. Such a clause is void for an independent contractor earning under $317,147.09 (RCW 49.62.030). The partner-side duty is now a non-solicit plus a non-use promise, and confidentiality covenants fall outside the statute. Grade: SETTLED (text read 2 Nov 2026); whether it reaches Steve UNSURE.
- **No fee without a signed split.** Under the old section 4, "the Parties' split terms" left room for Steve to claim an implied 50/50 on any client. Now a fee is owed only under a signed split partner agreement or addendum.

Kev's numbers (12 months, 2 years, 5 business days, 30 days) and clause 11 are unchanged. No new numbers were added, so no numbers gate was opened.

## Answers to Dean's questions

- **(a) Entire agreement:** fixed by edit 1. The NDA adds to every split agreement, and each split agreement keeps its own merger clause. Grade: LIKELY.
- **(b) DTSA and protected rights:** keep both, unconditional (edit 7). Grade: SETTLED on the DTSA text; LIKELY on its value against a business partner.
- **(c) ESHB 1155 and RCW 49.62:** 12 months is inside the 18-month cap that starts 30 June 2027. The live problem is today's RCW 49.62.010(4), fixed by edit 3. From 30 June 2027 a customer non-solicit with a worker must be limited to customers the worker dealt with directly. "Introduced or disclosed" may be broader than that. Grade: UNSURE whether a business partner (as opposed to an individual 1099) counts at all. Register R-024.
- **(d) Governing law:** pick Washington (edit 2).
- **(e) Missing items:** the no-fee rule (edit 4, applied); candidate privacy (edit 11); candidates both sides already know (edit 9; ownership for a placement is decided by the split agreement, per section 7).
