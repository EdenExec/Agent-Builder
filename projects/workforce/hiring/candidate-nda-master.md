## Executive summary

**Verdict: FIX FIRST, then use it.** Nick's template ("[Template] EPG & ____ - NDA", Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement, 3 pages) is a good base for candidates. It needs 7 MUST edits before 9 November. Have counsel read the edited version once. Confidence: LIKELY.

- **It is not a noncompete, and it should not be called one.** It is a confidentiality and non-circumvention agreement. That is the right tool. A candidate is not a "key employee" under Idaho Code 44-2701 and 44-2702 (read 2 Nov 2026), so a true noncompete would not hold. Calling this one a noncompete only scares good candidates. Dean's naming is correct.
- **Biggest gaps for a candidate:** the Purpose clause does not cover hiring. Non-circumvention protects clients only, not candidates or job orders, and its 3 years runs from a termination that may never happen. Exclusive Washington venue is likely void against an Idaho resident.
- **No whistleblower or NLRA carve-out, and no DTSA notice.** Applicants count as "employees" under the NLRA (*Phelps Dodge v. NLRB*, 313 U.S. 177 (1941); LIKELY). Add one short section that covers all of it.
