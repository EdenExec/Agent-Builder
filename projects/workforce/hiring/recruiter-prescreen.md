## The document (copy from here to send)

> **EDEN PARTNER GROUP**
> **Recruiter pre-screen · [Candidate name] · [Role]**
>
> Thanks for your time on our first call. Before we meet again, we'd like to see how you think. Answer in your own words, in writing. There are no trick questions, and short is fine when short is true. Please return it by [date] to [email].
>
> Eden is an executive search firm for commercial construction. We place project managers, superintendents, estimators, preconstruction and operations leaders with general contractors and specialty firms across the country. Our recruiters run their own desks: they source, screen, present and close.

### 1 · Your recruiting story

1. How did you get into recruiting (or sales, if you're new to it)? What kept you in?
2. Walk us through your best placement or win. The role, how you found the person, what almost went wrong, and what you did about it.
3. Walk us through a loss: a placement that fell through, a client you lost, or a search you didn't fill. What happened, and what would you do differently?
4. What does a great week look like for you, in numbers? (Calls, conversations, submittals, interviews, or your own measures.)

### 2 · How you source

1. You get a search for a Senior Preconstruction Manager in Miami for a general contractor building high-rise and mixed-use. Where do you look first, second and third? Be specific: tools, searches, titles, companies.
2. Write the Boolean string or LinkedIn search you'd start with.
3. You've called 40 people and nobody is interested. What do you change?
4. How do you find someone who isn't on LinkedIn and isn't looking?

### 3 · How you work today

1. Walk us through your daily workflow from first log-in to last call. When do you dial, when do you research, when do you do admin?
2. What CRM or ATS have you used, and how disciplined are you at logging every call? Be honest.
3. How do you keep track of candidates who aren't right today but might be in six months?
4. What tools (AI or otherwise) do you use to work faster? Give one example of something you automated or sped up.
5. How do you like to be managed, and what kind of manager gets the worst out of you?

### 4 · Think like the hiring authority

Answer as if you were the VP of Operations at the client.

1. The client wants a project manager with "10 years, healthcare, local, no job-hoppers." You have a strong PM with 7 years, data centers, 40 minutes away, who changed jobs twice in four years for good reasons. Do you submit them? What would you say to the client to make the case, or why not?
2. Write 7 "Must Dos" you'd expect a general contractor to require for a Superintendent on a $60M ground-up project. Rank them.
3. A candidate interviews well, gets an offer, and goes quiet. What do you think is happening, and what do you do in the next 24 hours?
4. The client has had a role open for four months with three agencies on it. What do you ask on your first call that the other three didn't?
5. What would make you tell a client not to hire a candidate you submitted?

### 6 · Values and how you work

Eden runs on seven pillars: Righteousness, Winning, Accountability, Discipline, Transparency, Growth and Execution. Tell us about your work, not your beliefs.

1. **Accountability:** a time you missed something you owned. What did you say, and to whom?
2. **Discipline:** what do you do every working day whether you feel like it or not?
3. **Transparency:** a time you told a client or a boss something they didn't want to hear.
4. **Growth:** the last thing you taught yourself for work, and how.
5. **Execution:** a time you finished something hard that others had dropped.
6. **Winning:** what does winning mean to you at work, a year from now?
7. Which pillar is your strongest, and which needs the most work?

### 7 · Enneagram

Take the free Enneagram test (about 15 minutes): https://www.eclecticenergies.com/enneagram/test

Send us your result (type and wing) and one sentence on whether it describes how you work.

### 8 · Anything else

What should we know that this document didn't ask?

## How we read it (internal, not sent)

| Section | Strong | Pass |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Story | Specific names of roles, numbers, owns the loss | Vague, blames others, no numbers |
| 2 Sourcing | Real titles, companies, a working Boolean string, a plan B | "LinkedIn and job boards" |
| 3 Workflow | Time-blocked day, honest about logging, a follow-up system | No system, "I just hustle" |
| 4 Hiring authority | Pushes back with reasons, Must Dos read like a superintendent's job, acts within 24 hours on the quiet candidate | Says yes to everything, generic Must Dos |
| 5 Structure | Clear on what they need, flexible with reasons | Won't engage, or numbers only |
| 6 Values | Real stories with an outcome | Slogans |
| 7 Enneagram | Done, with a sentence of reflection | Skipped |
