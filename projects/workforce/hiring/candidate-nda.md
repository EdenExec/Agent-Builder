# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (CANDIDATE AND TEAM EDITION)

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is effective as of the date of the last signature below (the "Effective Date"), between Eden Partner Group LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company (the "Company"), and [Candidate name], an individual (the "Candidate").

**1. Purpose**
The Company and the Candidate (the "Parties") are exploring, and may carry on, a working relationship in which the Candidate works with, for or on behalf of the Company in any capacity: as an employee, contractor, intern or otherwise, in any role, and including any later change of role, title or classification. This includes interviews, conversations, onboarding and the work itself (together, the "Relationship"). Each Party may share confidential information with the other for the Relationship (the "Permitted Purpose"). This Agreement protects that information from unauthorized use or disclosure and keeps the Parties from going around each other's business relationships.

**2. Definition of Confidential Information**
"Confidential Information" means any information, technical data or know-how about research, business models, financial data, Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), intellectual property, training materials, products, services, client lists, job orders, fees and pricing, candidate and pipeline data, depth charts, playbooks and scripts, Eden University material, AI tools, prompts and workflows, customer and prospect data, market analysis, software, processes or marketing strategies. It may be shared orally, in writing, in electronic form, on screen, or through access to a shared data platform (for example Google Drive), and it should be reasonably understood to be confidential. Information shown on screen or spoken counts.

Confidential Information does not include information that: (i) the receiving Party rightfully had before the disclosing Party shared it; (ii) becomes public through no wrongful act of the receiving Party; (iii) the disclosing Party approves for release in writing; or (iv) the receiving Party develops independently without using the disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

**3. Non-Disclosure Obligations**
The Parties will not use Confidential Information for any purpose other than the Permitted Purpose. Neither Party will disclose Confidential Information to any third party, except to its employees or trusted advisors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality duties at least as strict as these. Each Party will take reasonable measures to protect Confidential Information. The Candidate will not copy, photograph, record, screenshot or download Confidential Information, or enter it into any AI tool or outside service, without the Company's written permission, except as the Company directs for work in the Relationship.

**4. Non-Circumvention**
The Candidate will not use the Company's Confidential Information to solicit or accept recruiting business from any client or prospect, or to present, refer or place any candidate, that the Company disclosed to the Candidate in the Relationship, except on the Company's behalf. This lasts for 12 months after the Relationship ends. Nothing in this Agreement stops the Candidate from applying for, accepting or holding any job with any employer, including a Company client, or from working in recruiting.

**5. Return of Materials**
When the Relationship ends, or within 5 business days of a written request from the disclosing Party, the receiving Party will return or, at the disclosing Party's direction, destroy all documents and materials (and any copies) containing the disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

**6. No License or Warranty**
This Agreement grants no license under any trademark, patent, copyright or other intellectual property right. All Confidential Information is provided "AS IS," and the disclosing Party makes no warranty about its accuracy or completeness.

**7. Term**
This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for as long as the Relationship lasts, in any role or classification. It does not need to be signed again if the Candidate's role, title or classification changes. Confidentiality duties last 2 years after the Relationship ends, and for any trade secret, for as long as it remains a trade secret. Non-circumvention lasts as stated in section 4. A later employment, contractor or offer agreement adds to this Agreement and controls only where the two conflict.

**8. No Offer**
This Agreement is not an offer of employment or engagement, and it creates no employment, contractor, partnership or joint-venture relationship.

**9. Governing Law and Courts**
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Idaho, without regard to its conflict of law principles. Either Party may bring a dispute in the state or federal courts in Ada County, Idaho, or in the county where the Candidate lives.

**9A. Protected Rights**
Nothing in this Agreement stops either Party from reporting possible violations of law to, or taking part in an investigation by, any government agency (including the NLRB, EEOC or SEC); from discussing wages, hours or other terms and conditions of employment; from disclosing conduct it reasonably believes is unlawful, including harassment, discrimination or sexual assault; or from testifying truthfully.

Notice under 18 U.S.C. 1833(b): An individual will not be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for disclosing a trade secret (a) in confidence to a federal, state or local government official, directly or indirectly, or to an attorney, solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law; or (b) in a complaint or other document filed under seal in a lawsuit or other proceeding. An individual who sues an employer for retaliation for reporting a suspected violation of law may disclose the trade secret to their attorney and use it in the court proceeding, if they file any document containing the trade secret under seal and do not disclose it except under court order.

**9B. General**
This is the entire agreement on its subject. Changes must be in writing and signed by both Parties. If any part is unenforceable, a court may narrow it, and the rest stays in effect. Electronic signatures and signed copies count as originals.

**10. Injunctive Relief**
Each Party acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm for which money damages would not be enough. The non-breaching Party may seek injunctive relief to prevent or stop a breach, in addition to any other remedy.

**11. "Be a Good Partner" Clause**
Both Parties agree to operate in good faith, engage in transparent communication ("radical candor"), and act with each other's best interests at heart. Both Parties acknowledge that revenue and wealth are byproducts of working the correct way and agree that the love of money is the root of all evil.
