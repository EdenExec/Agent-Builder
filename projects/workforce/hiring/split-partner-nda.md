# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AND NON-CIRCUMVENTION AGREEMENT (SPLIT PARTNER EDITION)

This Mutual Non-Disclosure and Non-Circumvention Agreement ("Agreement") is effective as of the date of the last signature below (the "Effective Date"), between Eden Partner Group LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company (the "Company"), and [Partner legal name], [entity description, for example "a Washington limited liability company" or "an individual doing business as ___"] (the "Partner").

**1. Purpose**
The Company and the Partner (the "Parties") are independent recruiting businesses. They are exploring, and may carry on, a working relationship in which they share searches, clients, job orders and candidates and split fees on placements, in any capacity and on any client or job order, including under any split partner agreement or addendum they sign now or later (together, the "Relationship"). Each Party may share confidential information with the other for the Relationship (the "Permitted Purpose"). This Agreement protects that information from unauthorized use or disclosure and keeps the Parties from going around each other's business relationships.

**2. Definition of Confidential Information**
"Confidential Information" means any information, technical data or know-how about research, business models, financial data, Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), intellectual property, training materials, products, services, client lists, job orders, fees and pricing, candidate and pipeline data, candidate resumes and compensation, depth charts, playbooks and scripts, Eden University material, AI tools, prompts and workflows, customer and prospect data, market analysis, software, processes or marketing strategies. It may be shared orally, in writing, in electronic form, on screen, or through access to a shared data platform (for example Google Drive), and it should be reasonably understood to be confidential. Information shown on screen or spoken counts.

Confidential Information does not include information that: (i) the receiving Party rightfully had before the disclosing Party shared it; (ii) becomes public through no wrongful act of the receiving Party; (iii) the disclosing Party approves for release in writing; or (iv) the receiving Party develops independently without using the disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

**3. Non-Disclosure Obligations**
The Parties will not use Confidential Information for any purpose other than the Permitted Purpose. Neither Party will disclose Confidential Information to any third party, except to its employees or trusted advisors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality duties at least as strict as these. Each Party will take reasonable measures to protect Confidential Information. Neither Party will copy, download or enter the other's Confidential Information into any AI tool or outside service except as needed for a placement in the Relationship, and then only into tools that keep it private and do not train on it. Candidate information is used only with the candidate's consent and only for the role it was shared for.

**4. Non-Circumvention**
Neither Party will go around the other. The Partner will not solicit recruiting business from any client or prospect the Company introduced or disclosed to the Partner in the Relationship, and will not use the Company's introductions or Confidential Information to win or work recruiting business with that client or prospect, or to present a candidate to it, other than through the Company. The Company will not present, refer or place any candidate the Partner introduced to the Company in the Relationship except under a signed split partner agreement or addendum that covers that candidate. Both duties apply directly or through anyone else. This lasts for 12 months after the Relationship ends. It does not apply to a client, prospect or candidate the receiving Party can show, by its own dated records, it already had an active relationship with before the disclosure. Nothing in this Agreement stops either Party from running its own recruiting business, or any candidate from taking any job they choose.

**5. Return of Materials**
When the Relationship ends, or within 5 business days of a written request from the disclosing Party, the receiving Party will return or, at the disclosing Party's direction, destroy all documents and materials (and any copies) containing the disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

**6. No License or Warranty**
This Agreement grants no license under any trademark, patent, copyright or other intellectual property right. All Confidential Information is provided "AS IS," and the disclosing Party makes no warranty about its accuracy or completeness.

**7. Term**
This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for as long as the Relationship lasts, across every client, job order and split agreement. Either Party may end the Relationship with 30 days' written notice; placements already in progress finish under their split terms. Confidentiality duties last 2 years after the Relationship ends, and for any trade secret, for as long as it remains a trade secret. Non-circumvention lasts as stated in section 4. Fees, splits, candidate ownership and payment are set only in the Parties' signed split partner agreements and addenda. Neither Party owes the other any fee, split or other payment under this Agreement, and no fee is owed on any client, job order or candidate unless a signed split partner agreement or addendum covers it.

**8. Independent Businesses**
The Parties are independent businesses. This Agreement creates no employment, partnership, joint venture or agency, and neither Party may sign for or bind the other. This Agreement is not exclusive.

**9. Governing Law and Courts**
Washington law governs this Agreement, without regard to conflict of law principles. Any lawsuit will be brought in the state or federal courts located in Washington, unless the law of the state where the Partner is based requires otherwise.

**9A. Protected Rights**
Nothing in this Agreement stops either Party from reporting possible violations of law to, or taking part in an investigation by, any government agency (including the NLRB, EEOC or SEC); from discussing wages, hours or other terms and conditions of employment; from disclosing conduct it reasonably believes is unlawful, including harassment, discrimination or sexual assault; or from testifying truthfully.

Notice under 18 U.S.C. 1833(b): An individual will not be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for disclosing a trade secret (a) in confidence to a federal, state or local government official, directly or indirectly, or to an attorney, solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law; or (b) in a complaint or other document filed under seal in a lawsuit or other proceeding. An individual who sues an employer for retaliation for reporting a suspected violation of law may disclose the trade secret to their attorney and use it in the court proceeding, if they file any document containing the trade secret under seal and do not disclose it except under court order.

**9B. General**
This Agreement adds to, and does not replace or amend, any split partner agreement or addendum between the Parties, whether signed before or after it. Each split partner agreement remains the entire agreement on its own subject. On any matter a split partner agreement covers, including confidentiality and non-circumvention for its client, that agreement controls; on everything else, this Agreement applies. Together they are the entire agreement between the Parties on their subjects. Changes must be in writing and signed by both Parties. If any part is unenforceable, a court may narrow it, and the rest stays in effect. Electronic signatures and signed copies count as originals.

**10. Injunctive Relief**
Each Party acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm for which money damages would not be enough. The non-breaching Party may seek injunctive relief to prevent or stop a breach, in addition to any other remedy.

**11. "Be a Good Partner" Clause**
Both Parties agree to operate in good faith, engage in transparent communication ("radical candor"), and act with each other's best interests at heart. Both Parties acknowledge that revenue and wealth are byproducts of working the correct way and agree that the love of money is the root of all evil.
