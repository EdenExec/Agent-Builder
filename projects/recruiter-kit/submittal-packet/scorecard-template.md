# Pre-screen scorecard template

Sits directly after the cover page. Scores the candidate against the client's 7 to 10 Must Dos for this JO (from `must-dos-intake-template.md`, client-approved). Template only: no real candidate data in the repo.

Scale (default, Kev to confirm): **Meets** (clear evidence), **Partial** (some evidence or adjacent), **Gap** (no evidence), **Not asked** (never a final state; ask before submitting).

Rules: evidence is a fact the candidate said or the resume shows, never a feeling. A Gap is shown, not hidden; the client asked for match quality. If fewer than 7 Must Dos are approved, stop and finish the Must Do list first.
