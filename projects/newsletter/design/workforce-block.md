# Workforce block: design

**Result:** one block, four fixed parts, about 140 words, readable in 30 seconds. Dean writes it as `projects/newsletter/<date>/dean.md` after the Friday Round Table pull (about 4pm PT); Jenny designs and queues it. Nothing publishes until Kev says yes.

## 1. Layout

| Order | Part | Form | Length cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Team scoreboard | 4-row table: measure, this week, 4-week avg, direction | 4 rows |
| 2 | Training recap | Two concepts, 3 lines each, then one call question | 7 lines |
| 3 | Word of the week | Term, plain definition, the ladder as one line | 4 lines |
| 4 | Eden University | Table: module, sign-offs, date | 5 rows, then "and N more" |

- Header band: WORKFORCE in bold caps, the week-ending date beneath in italic. Hairline rule above and below.
- Fixed order and headings every week so readers learn where to look.
- Page budget: the block fits one third of a page; the newsletter as a whole stays under two.

## 2. Tone

- Result first: line one under each heading is the point (for the scoreboard, "Team activity up on the 4-week average").
- Dean's voice is plain and practical; the lift is mine, one bright line at most, no puns.
- Shout-outs name the win and the module, never anyone's numbers.
- No exclamation marks, emoji or hashtags.

## 3. Brand notes

- Montserrat throughout (Helvetica Neue or Arial only if it cannot load). White or cream ground, black type, greys for rules and the direction column.
- Direction uses words with a plain arrow-free marker: "Up", "Flat", "Down" in grey, bold only when Down so it reads as information, not alarm. Muted semantic colour is allowed for state but not needed here.
- Tables over bullets; no part has more than 3 bullets.
- Recruiting audience, so the footer carries edenpartnergroup.com and EDEN PARTNER GROUP.
- Marlowe design check before Kev sees the rendered version.

## 4. Guard rails (Dean's rules, enforced at design)

- Team totals only. No individual numbers, ranking or per-person charts.
- No pay, health, family or age, anywhere, including in training examples.
- Eden University names: name and module only, no scores. **Pending Kev's yes** (ask 1). Until then the block shows counts ("3 sign-offs this week") and no names.
- Team totals vs direction only is **pending Kev's yes** (ask 2). Template below shows totals; the direction column survives if Kev picks direction only.
- Scorecards stay in person; the block never links to one.
- No client names, no candidate names, no comp or phones.
