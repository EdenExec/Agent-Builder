# Eden Partner Group: Accountable Plan for Vehicle and Business Expense Reimbursement

DRAFT for Kev's review, then Chris Catalano (CPA) and outside counsel as needed. Not adopted until signed by the company. Prepared by Tally; Docket review pending.

## 1. Purpose
Eden Partner Group (the "Company") reimburses employees, including owner-employees, for business expenses under an accountable plan that meets Treas. Reg. 1.62-2. Reimbursements under this plan are not wages and are not reported on Form W-2.

## 2. Who is covered
All W-2 employees of the Company, including shareholder-employees. Independent contractors are not covered (they are paid under their own agreements).

## 3. What is reimbursed: business vehicle use
When an employee uses a personally owned or personally leased vehicle for Company business, the Company reimburses business miles at the IRS standard business mileage rate in effect on the date of travel. The rate is set by the IRS and applies equally to every covered employee. Charging, tolls and parking for business trips are reimbursed on top of that at cost if the mileage rate method is not used for them (Chris to confirm; the standard rate already includes operating costs, so default is mileage only plus tolls and parking).

Business miles are travel for Company purposes: client, candidate and partner meetings, job fairs, offices and job sites other than the employee's regular workplace, banks, supply runs.

Not business miles (never reimbursed): commuting between home and a regular workplace (including the Boise office once it opens), personal errands, and any trip with no Company purpose. If the employee's home is their principal place of business, trips from home to a business destination are business miles; Chris to confirm treatment once the Boise office opens.

## 4. Substantiation (required)
For every trip: date, starting point, destination, business purpose (who and why), and miles. Odometer reading at the start and end of each year. Logs are kept at the time of travel (a mileage app or the template in this folder). Reimbursement is paid only against a submitted log.

## 5. Timing
Logs are submitted monthly, within 60 days of the trip. The Company reimburses within 30 days of an acceptable submission.

## 6. Return of excess
No advances beyond reasonable expected expenses. Any amount paid that is not substantiated is returned to the Company within 120 days, or it is treated as wages.

## 7. No recharacterizing pay
Reimbursements are never paid in place of, or by reducing, salary or other pay. A flat monthly car allowance without logs is not part of this plan.

## 8. Records and review
The Company keeps logs and reimbursement records for at least seven years. The CPA reviews the log and the rate each quarter, and the plan is reviewed each January.

## 9. Equal treatment
The same rule applies to every covered employee. Owner-employees are paid under this plan exactly as other employees are, and not through distributions.
