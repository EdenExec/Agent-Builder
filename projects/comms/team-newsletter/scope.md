# The Eden Brief · team newsletter scope (draft for Kev, 5 Nov 2026)

Status: SCOPE ONLY. Nothing built, sent or published. Kev's ask (5 Nov, via Radar): a weekly team newsletter with talking points, once or twice a week, that recruiters can send to candidates and clients and use in their own conversations. Scout and Dean produce the content; Jenny owns assembly, design and publishing, with little oversight from Kev once it runs.

## The call

Start with one issue a week: **The Eden Brief, Tuesday 7:00am PT**, emailed to the team and archived on the Team Hub. Each issue has an internal half and one ready-to-forward "market note" that has been cleared for clients and candidates. Add a Thursday "two talking points" mini once four Tuesdays have landed on time with no brand-guard miss.

## What's in an issue (one screen, about 400 words)

| Block | Owner | For | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 numbers this week | Scout | Internal + forwardable | Three sourced construction-market numbers with a one-line "so what" |
| Talking points: candidates | Scout, Jenny writes | Internal | Two lines a recruiter can say on a call ("Specialty contractors grew 14.7% and 62.6% are short on craft") |
| Talking points: clients | Scout, Jenny writes | Internal | Two lines for a hiring-manager call: market pressure, comp-free |
| Headhunter's craft | Dean | Internal | One tip from training or the scorecard trend: an opener, an objection, a Boolean string. No names, no individual numbers |
| Market note (forward this) | Jenny | Clients and candidates | 120 words, three numbers, sources, Eden sign-off. Sent by each recruiter from their own inbox |
| This week on LinkedIn | Jenny | Internal | The approved post to like and share |

## How it goes out

1. **Monday:** I assemble the issue as an HTML email plus the market note as plain text. Marlowe checks the template once, then only when the design changes.
2. **Tuesday 7:00am PT:** one email to the team list, plus the same issue on the Team Hub as an archive row, so the last issues stay searchable.
3. **The market note:** each recruiter forwards or pastes it from their own inbox. I keep it as a Superhuman snippet ("Market note") for anyone on Superhuman, and as a copy block in the email for everyone else.

Two things this needs from the team side: a team mailing list (Radar or Nick confirms one exists, for example team@), and a sender. My pick is Eden Partner Group from Kev's inbox, scheduled, so the team sees it as his.

## QC: what Kev approves and what runs on its own

| Stage | Kev's touch | Opens when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0, first 4 issues | Each issue is one QC send item Monday evening. Kev approves the exact email and the market note | Now |
| 1 | The issue goes on schedule unless Kev vetoes it by 6:30am Tuesday. He gets a one-line Friday report: opens, forwards recruiters logged, any replies | Four on-time issues with no brand-guard miss, and Kev's yes |
| 2 | Thursday mini added; same veto rule | Kev's yes |

**Always a QC item, at every stage:**
- a client, candidate or competitor named;
- any comp, fee or rate figure;
- a claim about Eden's own results;
- a forecast;
- anything legal or about hiring law;
- Scripture;
- a new external recipient list, or anything Eden itself sends outside the team.

The team email is internal; the market note goes out only through recruiters' own hands.

## Measures (first 8 weeks)

- On time 8 of 8.
- At least half the recruiters forward the market note at least once a week (they log the forward on the candidate's Crelate record).
- Replies or calls traced to a note: count them and share the wins in the next issue.
- A two-question pulse from the team at week 4: useful, and what's missing.
