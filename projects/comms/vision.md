## Who Eden is to the people who matter

Eden Partner Group places the leaders who build what holds up a city. The public image should feel prepared, discreet and on time, which are the Seven Pillars shown in public form.

| Audience | What they should think | What they see | What we want them to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clients (construction hiring leaders) | "They know my market and they never embarrass me." | Sourced market insight, craft lessons, calm tone, nothing about other clients | Reply to a post, take a call |
| Candidates (passive construction leaders) | "They respect people and they're straight about the role." | Role spotlights with no hype, team and culture posts, fast replies | Message us, say yes to a call |
| Recruits (recruiters, interns, Boise State talent) | "I would learn and win here." | The office, the Seven Pillars at work, the intern program, training | Apply, refer a friend |

## Voice pillars

| Pillar | Sounds like | Never |
|---|---|---|
| Specific | A number, a source, a named project (only as the source reports it) | "Industry-leading", "passionate" |
| Prepared | Calm, on time, ready before the ask | Rush, "just circling back" |
| Generous | Teaches, credits others by name (with their yes) | Taking credit for a client's work |
| Discreet | Open about craft, silent on clients and candidates | Any tell that exposes a search |
| Plain | Result first, short, no exclamation marks, no emoji | Hype, slang, hashtag stacks (three at most) |

| Kev | Says yes or no, and opens each stage | |

**Stages of trust.** Nothing posts or sends without Kev's yes until he opens a stage.

| Stage | Rule | Opens when |
|---|---|---|
| 0 (now) | Every post is its own QC item, approved by Kev | Already in force |
| 1 | Kev approves the week's batch once, Monday | Four straight weeks with no brand-guard miss |
| 2 | Pre-approved formats (event thanks, role spotlights from public postings) go out on Jenny's OK, Kev sees a Friday report | Kev's explicit yes per format |

Never at any stage: client names without Kev's yes for that post, anything about a candidate, compensation, paid spend, press, or a reply to a complaint.

## Roadmap with gates

"Day 1" is the intern's start date. Calendar months assume a late-November start (A).

| | Phase 1: Foundation | Phase 2: Voice and video | Phase 3: Presence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Window | Weeks 1 to 8 (Dec to Jan) | Weeks 9 to 24 (Feb to May) | Week 25 on (summer and fall) |
| Ships | Company page and Kev's page on a rhythm, brand sheet, asset library, weekly scorecard | AM video series, Instagram, monthly newsletter, website refresh | Events and job fairs, Boise State campus brand, second intern, optional paid boosts |
| Staff | One intern, 10 to 20 hrs a week | Same intern, more capture hours | Two interns |
| Spend | Wages only | Video production per AM plan (QC spend), newsletter tool | Event costs and boosts (QC spend, Tally gate) |

| Gate | To enter Phase 2 | To enter Phase 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery | 18 of 24 planned posts shipped | 2 videos a month for 3 straight months |
| Quality | Zero brand-guard misses; Kev sends 70% of drafts unedited | Newsletter sent on time three months running |
| Signal | 3 or more real inbound signals (DMs, applications, "let's talk") traced to posts | A measurable campus signal (applications, Handshake views) |
| Kev | Kev's yes | Kev's yes and a second-intern budget |
