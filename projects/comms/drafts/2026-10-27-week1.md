## Post 2: ENR Top 600 talent squeeze (suggested Wed 29 Oct)

Contractors aren't short on work. They're short on people.

ENR's Top 600 specialty contractors grew revenue 14.7% to $275.1 billion. 62.6% of them now report a craftworker shortage, up from 57.4%. About three in four electrical contractors are short.

AI is driving the data center boom. It isn't wiring it, pouring it or running the crew.

The leaders who can staff and run that work are the constraint. If that's you and you're open to what's next, let's talk.

## Post 3: Una Residences, Miami (suggested Fri 31 Oct)

A 47-story tower in Miami that reaches up to 33 feet past its own footprint.

Columns that kink up to 25 degrees through a custom steel node. 100,000 sq ft of parking across three levels below grade, sealed by about 800 overlapping soil-mix shafts.

No model sequenced that on site. Superintendents and engineers who had done hard things before did.

South Florida doesn't have many people who have built a cantilever like this. If you're one of them, let's talk.

## Post 4: Abilene data center (suggested Tue 3 Nov)

Steel ordered in 30 days. Hot-aisle containment assembled on the ground to save 20 days. 140 prefab panels enclosed the building in 10 days, through wind and snow.

That's the Abilene data center campus, built for up to 100,000 GPUs.

AI will run in those halls. People got them built on schedule.

The project leaders who can hold a schedule like that are rare. If you've done it, let's talk.
