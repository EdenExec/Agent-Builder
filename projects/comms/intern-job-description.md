## Posting text

**Brand and Communications Intern** · Eden Partner Group · Boise, ID

**Paid, hourly, part-time W-2 position · Hybrid in Boise · 10 to 20 hours a week, built around your classes · Hourly pay: [Tally to propose], for every hour worked including training**

### About Eden

Eden Partner Group places leaders across the construction industry: the project executives, superintendents and estimators who build what holds up a city. We are opening our Boise headquarters and building the brand to match. Our values are Righteousness, Winning, Accountability, Discipline, Transparency, Growth and Execution.

Eden is a values-led, faith-friendly workplace; belief is never part of hiring, and no religious practice is required.

### The role

You help shape how Eden looks and sounds in public. You schedule our weekly LinkedIn posts, capture the photos and video that make them real, keep an eye on comments and messages, and support our video partner on shoot days. You report to [human supervisor] and work from a weekly plan written by our comms lead.

### What you will do

| Rhythm | Work |
|---|---|
| Every Monday morning | Schedule the week's approved posts in LinkedIn, check each one, and report back |
| Tuesday to Thursday | Capture office photos, video and team wins; triage comments and messages and pass anything sensitive to us; help on video shoots |
| Friday | Pull the week's numbers and hand off the next week's capture list |
| Each month and quarter | Help build our newsletter, event materials and campus presence |

### What you will learn

- How a professional services brand is built, scheduled and measured in public
- LinkedIn content, short-form video and photography, shaped by working with an outside video team
- How executive search works, and how to handle confidential information with care

### What we look for

- Clear, careful writing; you read a post twice before it goes live
- Organized and reliable: you do the Monday task on Monday
- Judgment and discretion: you know what not to post and who to ask

### Nice to have

- Canva or similar design tools; basic video editing
- Photography or video samples of any size (a phone is fine)
- An active LinkedIn profile and a feel for what works there

### Hours and where

Monday morning is your anchor shift (about three hours). The rest is flexible around classes, with on-site days in Boise for shoots and events. All hours are recorded in our timekeeping system and paid. Start date [start date].

### Credit and growth

If your school offers credit for internships, we will complete the school's employer paperwork. Credit is decided by your school, and your pay is the same either way. Strong interns may be considered for open roles. An internship does not promise later employment.

### How to apply

Send your resume, three work samples or links (class projects, a portfolio or your own accounts), and two sentences on a brand you admire and why. [application link] Shortlisted applicants have a 20-minute call, a short writing exercise and a conversation with [human supervisor].

You must be authorized to work in the United States.
