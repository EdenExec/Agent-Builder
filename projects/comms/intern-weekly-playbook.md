## Executive summary

**Result:** Monday you schedule the week's approved posts. Tuesday to Thursday you capture, triage and support video. Friday you hand Jenny the numbers. Core week is 10 hours; a full week is 20.

Times are Mountain (Boise). Jenny's clock is Pacific, one hour earlier. No post goes live from Friday sunset to Saturday sunset.

| Day | Core (10 h) | Added in a full week (20 h) |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | 3 h: schedule posts, review comments, plan capture | +1 h: Canva touch-ups on approved graphics |
| Tue | 2 h: capture, triage | +2 h: shoot day or office capture block |
| Wed | 2 h: capture, triage | +2 h: video logistics, asset library |
| Thu | 2 h: capture, triage | +2 h: newsletter or event prep |
| Fri | 1 h: metrics snapshot and handoff | +1 h: next week's capture list |

## Monday: start of week

Jenny's drafts are ready at 8:00am PT (9:00am MT) in the week's pack. Scout's picks arrived the night before. Kev's yes arrives as an approval mark on each row.

| Time (MT) | Step | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 9:00 | Open the week's pack (Drive: Comms / Weekly pack / date). Check every row for "Approved by Kev" and a QC id | List of what you may schedule |
| 9:15 | Read each post and its graphic. Stop on anything that names a client, a candidate or a figure not in the source line, and tell Jenny | Questions logged |
| 9:30 | Schedule each approved company-page post in LinkedIn's native scheduler (Start a post, clock icon, date and time). Paste the exact text; attach the checked image; put the source link in the first comment if the row says so | Posts in the Scheduled tab |
| 10:30 | Verify: preview, time zone, page selected, no typo. Screenshot the Scheduled tab into the pack | Proof saved |
| 10:45 | Mark rows "Scheduled" and message Jenny. Unapproved rows stay unscheduled | Done report |
| 11:00 | Review last week's comments and DMs against the triage table | Triage log |
| 11:30 | Read the week's capture asks from Jenny and pick shooting windows | Capture plan |

**Standard slots (A):** Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday at 7:30am PT (8:30am MT). Kev's personal posts are scheduled by Kev; you copy his text and image into his queue file so it takes him two minutes. You never use or hold Kev's login.

## Tuesday to Thursday

| Block | What | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Capture (45 to 60 min) | Office photos and video, team wins, event moments, candidate-safe stories | Anyone on camera signs a release first. Keep raw files in the shared drive |
| Triage (20 min a day) | Comments and DMs on the Eden page | Use the table below |
| Video support (as booked) | Shot list, location, releases, B-roll, file naming for Artificial Marketing shoots | AM contact runs through Jenny until Kev names a direct line |

**Candidate-safe stories** are about craft, culture and wins with no candidate named, no client named and no figures. Jenny writes them; you capture the supporting images.

| Triage tier | Looks like | You do |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Thanks, congratulations, likes | React only. Replies are drafted by Jenny and approved until Kev opens that step |
| Yellow | A question about a role, an application, the intern program | Log it, send the person to the posted application link, tell Jenny within the day |
| Red | Anything from or about a client or candidate, compensation, a complaint, press, a legal or safety issue | Do not reply. Screenshot, tell Jenny within the hour. She flags Radar and Kev |

## Friday: snapshot and handoff

Done by 1:00pm MT, well before sunset. Jenny reads it Sunday evening.

| Item | What |
|---|---|
| Scorecard | Per post: impressions, reactions, comments, shares, link clicks. Page: visitors, new followers |
| Signals | Every "let's talk" reply, DM or application you traced to a post |
| Notes | Three observations and one thing to try next week |

Also hand off the capture inventory (files shot, where saved) and next week's capture asks.

## Approvals

| Needs Kev | Jenny can OK |
|---|---|
| Every new post, until he opens a stage | Moving an approved post within the same week |
| Any named person or company | Fixing a typo that does not change meaning |
| Any video, newsletter, email or event invitation going out | Swapping between Marlowe-checked templates |
| Any live reply or comment on Eden's behalf | Choosing which approved graphic goes with which post |
| Any spend, boost or tool purchase | Capture priorities for the week |
| Any new channel or press contact | |

## Never do

| Never | Instead |
|---|---|
| Post or schedule anything without Kev's approval mark | Leave it unscheduled and tell Jenny |
| Name a client, unless public and Kev approved that post | Ask Jenny |
| Share anything about a candidate: name, photo, story or résumé | Use candidate-safe stories only |
| Mention compensation, fees, rates or phone numbers | Reference the posted application link |
| Use or ask for anyone's login | Ask Kev for page-role access |
| Photograph or film anyone without a signed release | Get the form first |
| Reply to clients, candidates, press or critics | Hand it to Jenny as Red |
| Work off the clock or post Friday sunset to Saturday sunset | Record every hour; schedule Sunday to Friday daytime |
