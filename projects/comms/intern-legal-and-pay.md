# Verdict: W-2, hourly, non-exempt, $18 to $20 an hour. FIX FIRST on the posting; one counsel read on the agency paper.

## Executive summary

Hire the intern as a part-time W-2 employee of Eden Partner Group, paid hourly through Gusto. At $18 and 15 hours a week, the cost is about **$1,281 a month** with employer taxes and comp. The risk sits in the three must-dos below.

| # | Must-do before the first offer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open Idaho payroll: EPG registered in Idaho, IDOL unemployment account, Idaho withholding account, an Idaho workers' comp policy, all in Gusto (Joel) | No comp policy is a misdemeanor and leaves an injury claim uncapped (Idaho Code 72-301, 72-210, 72-319) |
| 2 | Fix the posting: "current students" not "recent grads", pay range shown, EEO line, values-welcome line, Idaho work location | "Recent college graduate" in an ad is named as an age violation (29 CFR 1625.4(a)) |
| 3 | Sign the paper: offer letter, IP assignment with confidentiality, a social-media rule, a release for everyone filmed, and a written agreement with Artificial Marketing | Without it, ownership of footage and use of faces and candidate data are open questions |

## 1. Classification and payroll (Idaho, Ada County)

| Item | Rule | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| W-2, not 1099 | Eden sets the hours, trains, supervises, supplies tools and owns the output. That is an employee under DOL, IRS, Idaho unemployment (72-1316(4): free from control **and** an independent business) and Idaho comp (72-102(16)). Same answer as the Sales Intern review of 23 Oct. | SETTLED |
| Non-exempt | Hourly pay fails the salary-basis test, so no exemption applies, the creative one included (29 CFR 541.600). Track every hour: training, travel between shoots (29 CFR 785.38) and after-hours replies to comments and DMs. No off-clock posting. | SETTLED |
| Wage and overtime | Idaho minimum $7.25 (44-1502); do not use the under-20 training wage. Idaho has no overtime law of its own; FLSA 1.5x over 40 hours a week applies. Final pay by the next payday or 10 days, whichever is first (45-606). | SETTLED |
| New-hire report | Report to IDOL within 20 days of hire. Gusto should file it (third-party sources say it does); Joel confirms on the first hire. | SETTLED (rule); LIKELY (Gusto) |
| I-9 and E-Verify | I-9 section 2 by the third business day. E-Verify is optional for Idaho private employers: HB 704 (2026) passed the House and stalled in Senate committee. Optional is the pick: if Eden enrolls, it must run it for every new hire. | LIKELY |
| If the intern works from another state | That state's law follows the worker. Washington: $17.13 minimum (2026), $17.73 (2027), L&I state-fund comp only, paid sick leave, PFML and WA Cares premiums, a pay range in any posting open to WA (RCW 49.58.110), all-party audio consent (RCW 9.73.030). Rule: work is performed in Idaho unless Kev approves another state in writing 30 days ahead, so Joel can register. | SETTLED (WA); LIKELY (OR) |

## 2. Student specifics

| Item | Rule | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| Handshake and Boise State | Eden registers on Handshake; Career Services approves the connection. Postings carry duties, hours and pay rate; no commission internships; EEO and NACE principles apply. Semester interns typically work 15 to 20 hours a week. Boise State reviews **third-party recruiters** case by case and bars them from keeping student resumes for future placement. So say the role is on Eden's own team, and never load Handshake applicants into the candidate database. | SETTLED |
| Academic credit | Changes nothing that matters. Boise State says credit is not an alternative to pay and employers do not grant it; Eden signs the school's learning agreement and pays the same rate. A narrow unemployment-tax exemption covers students under 22 in a school-run credit program (Idaho 72-1316A(3); 26 USC 3306(c)(10)(C)). It saves about $22 a month: do not claim it unless Joel confirms. | LIKELY |
| F-1 students | CPT: the school's DSO authorizes this employer, the dates and part-time status on the I-20 before day one (8 CFR 214.2(f)(10)(i)). Pre-completion OPT: EAD in hand, 20 hours a week cap in term ((f)(10)(ii)(A)). Post-completion OPT: at least **20 hours** a week, or the days count toward the 90-day unemployment limit (SEVP guidance, quoted by universities). Eden need not sponsor; national origin stays protected. | SETTLED (rule); LIKELY (20-hour guidance) |
| Faith-based posting | Title VII's religious-organization exemption is unlikely to cover a fee-for-service LLC (*Spencer v. World Vision*, 633 F.3d 723, 9th Cir. 2011). The Idaho Human Rights Act covers religion at five or more employees (67-5902(6)). Line: *"Our faith shapes how we work: stewardship, integrity, excellence. No religious belief or practice is required to work here, and we welcome applicants of every background."* Any prayer or devotion at work is optional, and saying no costs nothing. | LIKELY |

## 4. Content, IP and privacy

| Item | What the paper says | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| Ownership | Work made within the job is Eden's as a work made for hire (17 USC 101, 201(b)). Add an assignment for edge cases: off-hours shoots of Eden events, drafts, raw files, presets. Give the intern a licence to show published work in a portfolio. | SETTLED |
| Confidentiality and social media | No client names without the client's written OK. No candidate names, faces, resumes, salaries, fees, screens, whiteboards or CRM views, ever. Eden owns the accounts and passwords. When the intern posts about Eden on a personal account, the post discloses the job (FTC, 16 CFR 255.5). Include the trade-secret immunity notice (18 USC 1833(b)). Carve out talk about the intern's own pay and conditions (NLRA s.7; *Stericycle*, 2023, under challenge). | SETTLED; NLRB standard CONTESTED |
| Releases | A signed release from every identifiable person filmed: staff, clients, candidates, guests. A parent signs for anyone under 18. Post notice signs at events. A candidate is never filmed without separate written consent, since the search may be confidential. Idaho has no publicity statute, and the common-law reach is UNSURE, so get releases anyway. Washington's RCW 63.60 applies to shoots there. | UNSURE (Idaho); SETTLED (WA) |
| Artificial Marketing | A signed SOW before any shoot: (1) deliverables, raw footage and project files assigned to Eden (a signed work-for-hire clause works for audiovisual work, 17 USC 101(2), with an assignment as backup); (2) the agency warrants releases for its crew and talent, licensed music and stock covering paid social and web, without a time limit; (3) a list of AI-generated elements, which may not be copyrightable (*Thaler v. Perlmutter*, D.C. Cir. 2025), and no cloned voice or face without written consent; (4) confidentiality, with no candidate data on camera; (5) agency portfolio use only after Eden publishes. The intern takes direction from Eden, not the agency. | SETTLED (statute); OUTSIDE COUNSEL (template) |

**OUTSIDE COUNSEL** (IP, one review): ask (1) does the agency SOW give Eden clean ownership of raw footage and AI elements; (2) is our release enough for paid ads and for Washington shoots; (3) is the social-media rule narrow enough for the current NLRB standard.

## 5. What Kev must decide

My picks: $18 an hour, $20 ceiling for a portfolio-ready graduate; 15 hours a week in term, 20 in summer or on OPT. Idaho only, Eden-owned gear, all work on the clock, one named Eden supervisor. No E-Verify yet, the faith line as written, the agency SOW signed before any shoot. Joel confirms EPG as employer and Idaho accounts live before posting.
