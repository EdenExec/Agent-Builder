### 2. Award of Merit: Una Residences (ENR Texas & Southeast Best Projects)
- **Date:** 14 Oct 2026
- **URL:** https://www.enr.com/articles/63915-award-of-merit-renovation-restoration-una-residences
- **Story:** A 47-story, 414,000 sq ft Miami tower that widens as it rises, projecting up to 33 ft beyond its footprint from the 19th floor. Below it sit 100,000 sq ft of parking over three levels, sealed by about 800 overlapping soil-mix shafts, and columns that kink up to 25 degrees through a custom steel node tied with 40 bars.
- **Talent angle:** Cantilevers, kinked columns and a deep wet excavation are built by supers and PMs who have sequenced exactly that before, and South Florida has few of them.
- **Companies named:** DeSimone Consulting Engineering, OKO Group LLC, Adrian Smith + Gordon Gill Architecture, Civic Construction & Ant Yapi JV, VSN Engineering Inc., HNGS Engineers, ENR.
- **Client flag:** none.
- **Note:** same city as Kev's Waldorf post, so Jenny may want it later in the series.

### 3. Award of Merit: Abilene Data Center Buildings 1 & 2 (ENR Texas & Southeast Best Projects)
- **Date:** 14 Oct 2026
- **URL:** https://www.enr.com/articles/63851-award-of-merit-manufacturing-abilene-data-center-buildings-1-and-2
- **Story:** Two buildings, four data halls each, built for up to 100,000 GPUs on one network, with a 340-MW gas turbine plant for backup and 25 MW of IT load per hall. Steel was ordered inside 30 days, hot-aisle containment was assembled on the ground and lifted in to save 20 days, and 140 prefab panels closed the enclosure in 10 days, through wind delays and multiday snow.
- **Talent angle:** Pre-ordering MEP before drawings are final and holding a schedule through weather is a field-leadership call, and mission-critical supers who can make it are the scarcest hire in the market.
- **Companies named:** DPR Construction, Crusoe Energy Systems Inc., AlfaTech Consulting Engineers, ENR.
- **Client flag:** none.

### 4. AOM Excellence in Safety: Bighorn Data Center (ENR Southwest Best Projects)
- **Date:** 26 Oct 2026
- **URL:** https://www.enr.com/articles/63970-aom-excellence-in-safety-bighorn-data-center
- **Story:** A five-building, 2.5M+ sq ft data center campus in Sparks, Nev., where steel crews set nearly 3M sq ft of structural steel and over 1M sq ft of insulated metal panels in 217,000+ work hours with no lost-time accidents and one recordable. They did it in steady wind, on a congested site, with complex crane logistics and just-in-time delivery.
- **Talent angle:** A zero lost-time record across 217,000 hours is a foreman and superintendent outcome, which is the point of the series.
- **Companies named:** BZI, Switch, Stantec Architecture, Whiting-Turner, Paradigm Structural Engineers, InnovaTech, Gardner Watson Studs, ENR.
- **Client flag:** none.
- **Caveat:** Nevada, outside Eden's named markets; it is the best crane-and-steel data center story in the window.
