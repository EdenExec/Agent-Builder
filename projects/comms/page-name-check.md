# Pick: Eden Partner Group, once Joel confirms EPG is the entity that signs search agreements and pays the intern. LIKELY.

## Executive summary

- **The rule.** The page should carry the name of the company that hires, contracts and invoices. Kev's rule (10 Oct) puts recruiting, clients, candidates and invoices under Eden Partner Group, and the intern will be EPG's employee.
- **The problem.** "Eden Executive Search" is the legal name of a separate, live corporation. Using it for EPG's work blurs who the counterparty is and weakens the two-entity separation.
- **The move.** Rename the existing page (followers and URL stay); do not start a new one. One filing is needed first: EPG's Idaho registration, already on the intern list.

## Why not keep Eden Executive Search

- **Wrong party.** Job posts and the intern ad attach to the page. If EPG is the employer and contracting party, a page in a sister company's name misstates who the candidate or client is dealing with.
- **Filings that cut the wrong way.** EPG would need an Idaho assumed business name for "Eden Executive Search" before doing business under it (Idaho Code 30-21-805; without it, no lawsuits in Idaho, 30-21-810) and a Washington trade name (RCW 19.80, LIKELY). Filing a sister corporation's legal name as your own trade name invites exactly the confusion the two sets of books exist to avoid.
- **What you keep anyway.** Renaming keeps the followers and the edenexec URL. Put the search description in the tagline ("Executive search for construction") so the search identity survives.
