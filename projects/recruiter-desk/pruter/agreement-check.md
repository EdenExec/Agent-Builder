## Verdict: FIX FIRST

The arithmetic is right and every number in the draft agrees with every other. Before it goes out, fix five things:

1. The retainer is called non-refundable and refundable in back-to-back sentences.
2. The guarantee requires payment within 20 days. The balance is not due until 30 days after start.
3. Nothing protects Eden if Pruter fills the role another way during the search.

The other two: the paper says "two installments" where the call said "thirds", and both legal entity names are missing or inconsistent.

**Fee formula:** Fee = 25% x Annual Base Salary. Estimated $37,500 on $150,000. Retainer $12,500 (one-third of the estimate) on signing, credited. Balance = Fee - $12,500, due 30 days after start.

## Redlines

**1. MUST: installments. Thirds or two? Kev decides; I recommend thirds.**
- **Why thirds:** It matches what the client heard and is standard practice for retained search (Darden guide, LIKELY). It also closes the real exposure: under two installments, if Pruter stalls or hires elsewhere after Eden's shortlist, Eden keeps $12,500 for four or five weeks of work. Thirds also brings $12,500 in about week 3 instead of about week 12 (start plus 30 days).
- **Wording:** "Client will pay the Fee in three installments, each credited against the Fee: (a) Retainer: $12,500, due when Client signs. (b) Shortlist installment: $12,500, due within 10 business days after Eden presents at least three Qualified Candidates. (c) Final installment: the Fee less $25,000, due within 30 days after the Start Date."
- **If Kev keeps two installments:** use (a) and (c) with "the Fee less $12,500", and redline 6 becomes essential.

**2. MUST: the retainer refund (it is incoherent as drafted).** "This is a non-refundable retainer ... the client may request a refund." Under Cal. Civ. Code 1654 an ambiguity is read against the drafter (SETTLED), so a court would likely read it as refundable on loose terms. The draft also has no deadline, no amount and no notice. Replace the bullet with:

> "Progress report. Eden will deliver a written progress report by the 10th business day after it receives the Retainer. Refund. If by that day Eden has not presented at least [three] Qualified Candidates, Client may end this agreement by written notice within 5 business days after that day, and Eden will refund the Retainer in full within 10 business days after it receives the notice. Otherwise the Retainer is non-refundable. 'Qualified Candidate' means a person who meets the must-have criteria in Schedule A and has confirmed interest to Eden."

Kev's alternative: a no-questions-asked refund in the first 10 business days, which matches the call word for word but gives the client a free option. Either way, the cover email should say plainly how the paper reads.

**3. MUST: Phase II contradicts the fee terms.** "Assuming the Market Research Report indicates a realistic talent pool exists, the engagement fee is collected" conflicts with the retainer being due on signing. Replace with: "The search begins when Eden receives the Retainer." Add under the table: "Timing is an estimate, not a commitment."

**4. MUST: define the fee base and the trigger.** "First-year base salary" could be read as what is actually paid in year one, which is prorated if the person leaves. The call also opened up bonus, profit share and a sales incentive (lines 1397, 1562), so pay could move out of base. Wording:

> "'Annual Base Salary' means the annual base salary rate in the offer the Candidate accepts, without proration. It excludes bonus, profit share, commission, sales incentives, sign-on and relocation payments, and benefits. The final installment is earned on the Candidate's first day of work (the 'Start Date'). If the Candidate accepts but never starts, no final installment is due. If Client withdraws an accepted offer for a reason other than the Candidate's conduct, the full Fee is due."

**5. MUST: make the payment term and the guarantee condition agree.** Delete "within the Net 20 terms" and "within twenty (20) days of the invoice date". Use: "Eden will invoice the final installment on the Start Date. The guarantee applies only if Client has paid every installment in full by its due date."

**6. MUST: a fee when Pruter hires outside Eden during the search, and a cancellation rule.** As drafted, Pruter can fill the role through a posting, a referral or another recruiter, owe nothing past the retainer, and keep the guarantee language. Wording:

> "Exclusivity. Eden is Client's only outside recruiter for the role during the Search Period. If Client hires anyone [other than a current Client employee] into the role or a substantially similar role during the Search Period or within 90 days after it ends, the full Fee is due, less installments paid. 'Search Period' runs from signing to the earlier of the Start Date and the end of this agreement. Client may end the search by written notice; installments already due stay due, except under the Refund clause."

LIKELY enforceable in California: it is a fee the client owes, not a restraint on any worker.

**7. MUST: tighten the one-year tail (California restraint-of-trade risk).** "Any introduction" and "affiliates" are vague. Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code 16600 voided an overbroad no-hire in a business contract (VL Systems v. Unisen, 2007), although the court said a narrower clause "might pass muster" (LIKELY, secondary sources only). A fee limited to people Eden actually presented is the defensible version. Wording:

> "Candidate fee. If Client, or a company Client controls, hires or engages a Presented Candidate as an employee, contractor or consultant within 12 months after Eden presents that person, Client will pay the Fee calculated on that person's Annual Base Salary (for a contractor, the annualised contract rate), less installments already paid for this search. 'Presented Candidate' means a person whose name or resume Eden sends Client in writing."

**8. MUST: the guarantee, merged into one clause.** The draft has three bullets with two different remedies (a "replacement search" against "three or more suitable alternatives"), two different exclusion lists, and the vague "employment contract disputes (whether written or oral)". Wording:

> "Replacement guarantee. If the Candidate's employment ends for any reason within 120 days after the Start Date, Eden will run one replacement search for the same role at no further fee, if Client notifies Eden in writing within 10 business days after employment ends. It does not apply if employment ends because of lack of work, elimination or material change of the role or its pay, the Candidate's death or disability, or Client's breach of the Candidate's employment terms. Extended guarantee: if employment ends on day 121 through day 365, Eden will run one replacement search for 50% of the Fee calculated on the replacement's Annual Base Salary. A replacement search is Eden's only obligation under this guarantee; there is no cash refund."

**9. MUST: parties and entity names.**
- **Client:** the client's legal name is missing. "Pruter Construction" is a trade name, and they incorporated in July 2022 (line 335). Ask Serena for the exact name on the California Secretary of State record, the entity type and the signer's title. I have not guessed it.
- **Eden:** the draft names two entities. The letter is signed "Eden Executive Search, Headhunter & Founder" and the signature block reads "Eden Partner Group, Co-Founder / Chief Headhunter". EPG and EES are separate S-corps with separate books. Recruiting paper carries Eden Partner Group; Joel or Tally should confirm which books take this fee.
- **Parties line:** "This agreement is between [Eden Partner Group LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company] ('Eden') and [exact legal name], a California [corporation] ('Client')."

**10. MUST: governing law, venue, collection.** Wording:

> "Washington law governs this agreement. Any lawsuit about it will be brought in the state or federal courts for Clark County, Washington, and each party consents to those courts. The prevailing party in an action to enforce this agreement may recover reasonable attorney fees and costs. Amounts unpaid after their due date bear interest at the lesser of 1% a month and the highest lawful rate."

- **Choice of Washington law:** California enforces a chosen state's law where there is a reasonable basis, here Eden's base, unless it offends a fundamental California policy (Nedlloyd, 3 Cal.4th 459, 1992: LIKELY). The 16600 limit in redline 7 applies either way.
- **Attorney fees:** Cal. Civ. Code 1717 makes a one-sided fee clause mutual (SETTLED), so write it mutual.
- **Interest:** I did not verify whether California usury limits reach late charges on invoices, hence the "highest lawful rate" hedge.

**11. MUST: take the sales page out of the signed document.** Everything after the signature block can be read as part of the agreement.
- "Guaranteed Candidate Traction ... within the first two weeks" reads as a warranty wider than the refund clause.
- "Nearly 90% success rate, compared to just 26%" is unsourced, and I could not verify it. Source it or cut it.
- "We become JV Partners" implies a joint venture, which brings shared profit, shared loss and fiduciary duty. Cut "JV".
- **Fix:** move the comparison into the cover email, and add: "This agreement consists of the Fee Structure, Safeguards, Replacement Guarantee and Schedule A. The cover letter, process table and comparison describe our approach and are not terms."

**12. NICE: a fee floor; no cap unless asked.** "The Fee will be no less than $35,000," which is 25% of the bottom of Pruter's own $140K range. If Pruter asks for a cap, offer $55,000 (25% of the $220K top).

**13. NICE: mutual confidentiality, with a term.** "Each party will keep the other's non-public information confidential and use it only for this search. Client will not share a Presented Candidate's resume or identifying information outside Client without Eden's written consent. This clause survives two years after the agreement ends." Making it mutual costs Eden nothing and earns trust.

**14. NICE: California hiring compliance.**
- **Pay scale:** "Client will give Eden a good-faith pay scale for the role for any job posting." If Pruter has 15 or more employees, a third party posting the job must include the pay scale (Cal. Lab. Code 432.3(c), SETTLED).
- **Salary history:** that statute also bars seeking salary history "personally or through an agent" (432.3(b), SETTLED), so Eden's recruiters should not ask California candidates their current pay.
- **Background checks:** "Client is the employer and is responsible for its hiring decisions and for complying with background-check laws."

**15. NICE: clean-up.**
- "Innitial" should read "Initial".
- Use "Eden" and "Client" throughout, replacing "we", "Headhunter" and "Headhunters".
- Use "business days" everywhere, replacing "working days".
- The founder title should match in both places.

## Kev's decisions (23 Oct 2026) and what changed

1. **Two payments**, not thirds: a deposit to do the search, the remainder on successful completion. Standard for all retained searches now.
2. **Refund:** not automatic. After the 10-business-day progress report, the client may ask for the retainer back if they don't think Eden is performing.
3. **Hire outside or promote within:** the retainer is not refundable once Eden has performed in line with expectations in the first 10 days.
4. **No fee floor:** bill 25% of the final salary less the deposit.

Radar applied 1, 2 and 4 to the Pruter draft and the master template, plus redlines 3 (Phase II wording) and 5 (guarantee tied to "paid by its due date"). Still open: Pruter's legal name (Serena), which Eden entity takes the fee (Joel), governing law and venue, and moving the sales page out of the signed document.
