# Pruter Construction: Senior Project Manager, Structural Concrete (Southern California)

Kev's intake call, Fri 23 Oct 2026, 9:00am PT (Zoom). On the call: Serena Riordan and Kristi Pruter (pruterinc.com). Kev's handwritten notes and the Zoom transcript are the sources. This brief is work only.

## The company

- Pruter Construction, Southern California concrete subcontractor, four years old, non-union. Started in decorative and site concrete; first big win was a Burnham Ward project.
- The field team is strong and stays because of the culture the late co-founder built. They want the office to match the field: camaraderie, passion, having fun at work.
- Competitors named on the call: Shaw and Sons, Trademark. Internal only; never in client or candidate material.

## Why they are hiring

GCs hesitate to award Pruter structural work. They doubt the office's structural experience, though the field team has it. Pruter needs a senior PM whose resume gives GCs that confidence and helps win structural RFPs.

## The person

- Senior Project Manager or Sr. PM with deep structural concrete experience: podiums, shear walls, complex structural elements. Tilt-up is a different specialty and not the focus.
- "Gray hair": a wise old owl who mentors, solves problems rather than arguing, decides quickly and negotiates change orders.
- Comfortable in a non-union shop.
- Based in, or able to work in, Southern California.
- Fits the culture: camaraderie, passion, fun.
- Passive candidates, not active job seekers.

## Terms Kev proposed

- Retained search: 25% of first-year base salary.
- On an estimated $150,000 base, the fee is $37,500. A one-third retainer of $12,500 is due on signing.
- The client's base range on the call was $140,000 to $220,000; bonus and profit share are still open.
- Six-week timeline. Progress report inside two weeks; if no suitable resumes have been presented, the retainer can be returned.
- Interviewers: Elias (Director of Field Operations), Serena and Kristi. Kev needs a call with Elias first.
- The client contact had a bad experience with a headhunter before. Earn trust with a fast, honest first two weeks.
