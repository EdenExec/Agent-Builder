- **The goal is $300k total, and it's reachable, but only at VP or SVP with a bigger base.** VP of Development bases run about $200k to $275k in multifamily postings and $226k to $323k in H Two's guide. Data center Senior Director and VP roles post $260k to $325k. Today he is at about $200k base, which is the bottom of the VP band. The realistic offer is a $225k+ base plus a 25 to 50% bonus. Promote is upside on top, and it usually pays out 3 to 7 years later.
- **Data centers are where the growth and the money are, and his background crosses over.** Owner-side delivery of power-heavy, tightly coordinated buildings (labs, vivariums) plus PE-grade pro formas is the pitch. The most capital and the most hiring are in Northern Virginia and Maryland, which he can reach without moving. Charlotte is next.
- **Why he's looking (from Kev's notes):** he has been searching since February. A VP offer at Mill Creek Residential was being put together when a company-wide hiring freeze ended it. His current role is likely to be cut by year end. The market has plenty of roles for very senior people and for analysts, but few in between.
- **Where he'd go:** Florida first (Fort Lauderdale/Miami, Orlando, Jacksonville), then North Carolina (he grew up there), or stay in D.C., Maryland or Virginia. He will relocate.
