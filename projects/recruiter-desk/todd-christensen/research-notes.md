### Life science
- No 2026 life-science developer VP/Director posting with a published range was found. The sector is in a "reset" in 2026 ([Area Development Q2 2026](https://www.areadevelopment.com/Biotech/q2-2026/from-reset-to-readiness-navigating-the-life-science-real-estate-market-in-2026.shtml)), and spec lab starts in Montgomery County are thin (search found mostly 2021-2024 projects: [Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/washington-dc/life-sciences/stonebridge-plans-800k-sf-of-life-sciences-space-in-montgomery-county-112192)). Growth is in biomanufacturing in NC, where the state booked about $3.79B of life-science investment in 2025 ([NCBiotech](https://www.ncbiotech.org/news/north-carolina-booked-nearly-4-billion-life-sciences-investments-2025)).
- Life-science and data-center work both sit in the top owner's-rep fee tier ([iRecruit](https://www.irecruit.co/guides/development-manager-owners-rep-salary)). There is no separate published developer pay band for life science. **[Inference]** Expect pay to track multifamily VP bands.

### What it takes to clear $300k total
- **Multifamily VP/SVP:** base of $225k-$275k plus a 25-50% cash bonus clears $300k. Promote is upside, often vested and paid 3-7 years out, so treat it as separate from the $300k cash target ([iRecruit](https://www.irecruit.co/guides/development-manager-owners-rep-salary); [H Two](https://htwonational.com/2026-compensation-guide/)).
- **Data center:** Director titles at land/power developers post $175k-$230k base, so $300k needs a VP/Senior Director title or a meaningful bonus/LTI ([Tract](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/tract/jobs/4372165009); [Crusoe $260k-$325k](https://inferencejobs.com/companies/crusoe/jobs/senior-development-director); [Glassdoor Vantage VP median $304k](https://www.glassdoor.com/Jobs/Vantage-Data-Centers-Jobs-E676007.htm)).
- **Conclusion:** $300k+ total is realistic at the VP/SVP level. The candidate's current $200k base is at or below the low end of posted VP bands.
