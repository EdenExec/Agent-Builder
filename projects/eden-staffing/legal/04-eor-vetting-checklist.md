# EOR vetting checklist

## Executive summary

- **Four knockouts:** takes Eden's trades' class codes in all three states; registered as an employer in FL, ID and OR; proves current tax deposits; lets Eden leave with its workers and claims history at no conversion fee.
- **Two terms the plan depends on:** Eden-specific loss runs (for the BaaS switch) and certificates a general contractor will accept within one Business Day.
- **One trap:** the EOR must not be able to contract with Eden's clients or workers directly.
