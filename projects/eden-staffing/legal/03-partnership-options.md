# Skilled trades, on site when you need them

**Eden Staffing** places skilled tradespeople and site staff with construction teams in Florida, Idaho and Oregon. You direct the work. We recruit, screen, employ through our payroll partner, and keep the crew showing up.

## Three ways to work with us

| | **Straight temp** | **Temp-to-hire** | **Project crew** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Best for** | Peaks, absences and short scopes | Trying a tradesperson on the job before you hire | A defined phase that needs a full crew |
| **How it works** | You order the trades and dates; we fill them | Same as straight temp, with a planned path to your payroll | We staff a crew to an agreed headcount and schedule for the phase |
| **You pay** | An hourly bill rate per trade, for hours worked | The same hourly bill rate | An hourly bill rate per trade, set for the whole phase |
| **Commitment** | None beyond each shift's notice | None; hire when you are ready | Headcount and duration agreed up front |
| **Hiring the worker** | Conversion fee on the published schedule | No fee after [NUMBER] hours worked | Conversion fee on the published schedule |

## What every option includes

- **Recruited and screened for the trade.** Skills check, certifications you require, and background or drug screens where you ask for them and the law allows.
- **Employed and insured.** Workers are W-2 employees of our employer-of-record partner, which runs payroll and taxes and carries workers' compensation.
- **One weekly invoice.** Hours your approver signs off, billed weekly on [NUMBER]-day terms.

## How we keep people safe

- **We walk your site** before the first person starts.
- **You run the site and direct the work;** your orientation and PPE come first on day one.
- **Every hour is paid,** including orientation and safety meetings, so crews stay and records stay clean.

## Getting started

1. **Call:** trades, headcount, site and start date.
2. **Paper:** sign the Staffing Services Agreement once and one short SOW per site.
3. **Site walk,** then crews start on the agreed date.
