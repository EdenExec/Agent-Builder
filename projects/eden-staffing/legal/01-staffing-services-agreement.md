## Executive summary

*Cover note for Kev and counsel; it comes out of the final.*

- **Control drives risk.** Client directs the work and runs the site (Section 6); indemnities follow fault only (Section 11), to survive Florida, Oregon and Idaho anti-indemnity law.
- **Money is tight and testable.** Weekly timesheets with deemed approval, weekly invoices, a suspension right for non-payment, and a conversion fee framed as liquidated damages (Sections 4, 5, 8).
- **Structure is explicit.** The EOR employs; only Eden Staffing LLC signs; joint-employer exposure is acknowledged, not denied (Section 3). Governing law follows the Site (Section 16, Option A).

# Staffing Services Agreement

This Staffing Services Agreement (the **Agreement**) is between **[Eden Staffing LLC]**, a [STATE] limited liability company (**Eden**), and **[CLIENT LEGAL NAME]**, a [STATE] [ENTITY TYPE] (**Client**). It is effective on [DATE] (the **Effective Date**).

*Drafting note: Eden Partner Group LLC and Eden Executive Search do not sign this Agreement or guarantee it. The plan's liability ring-fence depends on the staffing LLC being the only contracting party.*

## 1. Definitions

| **Assigned Worker** | An individual employed by the EOR whom Eden assigns to Client under an SOW. |
| **Assignment** | The period during which an Assigned Worker performs work for Client under an SOW. |
| **Authorised Duties** | The duties listed for a position in an SOW. |

## 2. Services and SOWs

2.1 Eden shall recruit, screen and assign Assigned Workers to Client for the positions, Sites and dates stated in each SOW.

2.2 Each SOW becomes part of this Agreement when both parties sign it. An SOW may change Exhibit A rates for that SOW only. An SOW may not change any other Section of this Agreement unless it names that Section and states that it overrides it.

2.3 If this Agreement and an SOW conflict, this Agreement governs, except as Section 2.2 allows. Terms in Client's purchase orders, vendor portals, subcontract forms or site rules do not change this Agreement, even if Eden or an Assigned Worker accepts or signs them, unless an amendment signed under Section 18.3 adopts them.

*Drafting note: general contractors often ask a labour supplier to sign their subcontract form with prime-contract flow-downs. Eden is not performing construction work and should not sign one; see issues memo, item 6.*

## 3. Employment structure

3.1 **EOR employs the workers.** Each Assigned Worker is a W-2 employee of the EOR. The EOR (a) pays wages, (b) withholds and pays payroll taxes, (c) provides workers' compensation coverage, (d) maintains unemployment insurance accounts, and (e) completes Form I-9 and, where required, E-Verify. Eden is responsible to Client for the EOR's performance of these duties.

3.2 **Client is not the payroll employer.** Client shall not pay wages, bonuses, per diem or any other compensation directly to an Assigned Worker. Assigned Workers are not eligible for Client's benefit plans, and Client shall not enrol them.

3.3 **Shared legal duties.** The parties acknowledge that applicable law may treat Client, Eden and the EOR as joint employers for wage-hour, discrimination and workplace-safety purposes regardless of this Section 3. This Agreement allocates responsibility between the parties; it does not change what the law requires of each of them.

3.4 **Classification information.** Client states that each position description in an SOW accurately describes the work the Assigned Worker will perform. Workers' compensation classification and pricing depend on those descriptions. Client shall not change an Assigned Worker's duties, Site or hours outside the SOW without a change order under Section 2.

## 4. Timesheets and approval

4.1 Assigned Workers record time [by electronic timekeeping system / on paper Timesheets] showing, for each day, start time, end time, and meal and rest breaks taken.

4.2 Eden shall deliver each Workweek's Timesheets to the Approver by [TIME] on the [first] Business Day after the Workweek ends.

4.3 The Approver shall approve or dispute each Timesheet on or before [TIME] on the [second] Business Day after delivery. A dispute must be in writing, identify the worker, date and hours disputed, and give the reason.

4.4 A Timesheet that the Approver neither approves nor disputes by that deadline is deemed approved.

4.5 Client shall not direct or permit any Assigned Worker to work off the clock, to skip a meal or rest break that applicable law requires, or to record fewer hours than worked. Eden pays Assigned Workers for all Hours Worked whether or not Client approves the Timesheet, and Client shall pay for all Hours Worked as recorded, subject only to a dispute made under Section 4.3 and resolved under Section 5.5.

## 5. Bill Rates, invoicing and payment

5.1 **Rates.** Client shall pay the Bill Rates in Exhibit A or the SOW for all Hours Worked. The Overtime Bill Rate applies to each hour for which applicable law requires overtime pay; the Double-Time Bill Rate applies where applicable law or the SOW requires double time.

5.2 **Changes in statutory cost.** If a law, a tax rate, a workers' compensation rate, a prevailing-wage determination or a required benefit increases the cost of employing an Assigned Worker, Eden may increase the affected Bill Rates by the amount of the increase, effective on the later of the date the increase applies and [NUMBER] days after Eden gives Client written notice with a calculation.

5.3 **Invoices.** Eden shall invoice Client [weekly] for the Hours Worked in the prior Workweek, plus any pass-through charges the SOW lists.

5.4 **Payment.** Client shall pay each invoice in full within [NUMBER] days after the invoice date. Amounts unpaid when due bear interest at [RATE]% per month, or the highest rate applicable law allows, whichever is lower, from the due date until paid.

*Drafting note: the plan doc proposes "net 15–30 terms". Kev decides the number; the late-interest rate needs a usury check in the governing state.*

5.5 **Disputes.** Client may withhold only the disputed part of an invoice, and only if it gives Eden a written dispute notice within [NUMBER] Business Days after the invoice date. The parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within [NUMBER] Business Days. Client shall pay the undisputed part when due and shall not set off amounts it claims under any other agreement.

5.6 **Suspension for non-payment.** If any undisputed amount is unpaid [NUMBER] days after it is due, Eden may, after giving [NUMBER] Business Days' written notice, remove Assigned Workers from Client's Sites and stop accepting new SOWs until Client pays all past-due amounts. Removal under this Section is not a breach by Eden.

5.7 **Credit.** Eden may set a credit limit for Client, require a deposit of [AMOUNT], or require payment in advance, on [NUMBER] days' written notice.

5.8 **Taxes.** Bill Rates exclude sales, use and gross-receipts taxes. Client shall pay any such tax that applies to the services.

5.9 **Collection costs.** Client shall pay Eden's reasonable costs of collecting past-due amounts, including attorneys' fees.

## 6. Site safety and supervision

6.1 **Control.** Client directs and supervises the work of each Assigned Worker at the Site, controls the Site and its conditions, and is responsible for the quality and results of the work. Client shall provide a competent supervisor at each Site during all Hours Worked.

6.2 **Host-employer duties.** Client shall, for each Assigned Worker, at Client's cost unless the SOW says otherwise:

(a) give site-specific safety orientation and hazard communication training before the worker starts work, and count that time as Hours Worked;

(b) provide the personal protective equipment the SOW assigns to Client, and ensure that all required PPE is worn;

(c) provide the same safety protections, equipment and training that Client provides its own employees doing similar work;

(d) comply with all occupational safety and health laws that apply to the Site, including federal OSHA standards and, in Oregon, Oregon OSHA rules; and

(e) record each Assigned Worker's recordable injuries and illnesses on Client's OSHA 300 log while Client supervises the worker day to day, as 29 CFR 1904.31 requires.

6.3 **Injuries and incidents.** Client shall (a) arrange immediate medical care for an injured Assigned Worker, (b) notify Eden by phone within [NUMBER] hours, and (c) give Eden a written incident report within [NUMBER] hours. Client shall not discourage any Assigned Worker from reporting an injury.

6.4 **Prohibited Duties.** Unless the SOW lists the duty as an Authorised Duty for that position, Client shall not require or allow an Assigned Worker to:

(a) operate any motor vehicle, on or off the Site;

(b) operate powered equipment or machinery, including forklifts, aerial lifts and cranes;

(c) work at a height that requires fall protection under the applicable safety standard;

(d) enter a permit-required confined space;

(e) handle cash, negotiable instruments or other valuables; or

(f) handle hazardous materials outside the hazard communication training Client has given.

6.5 **Eden's rights.** Eden may (a) inspect the Site before the first Assignment and at any reasonable time during it, on [NUMBER] hours' notice; and (b) remove an Assigned Worker immediately if Eden believes in good faith that the Site, the work or the worker's treatment is unsafe or unlawful. An Assigned Worker may refuse work he or she believes in good faith to be unsafe, and Client shall not retaliate against the worker or Eden for that refusal.

## 7. Selection, screening and conduct

7.1 Eden shall screen candidates against the requirements the SOW lists. Client shall not ask Eden to select, reject or remove a worker on a basis that applicable anti-discrimination law prohibits, and Eden shall refuse any such request.

7.2 Background checks, drug tests and motor-vehicle records are run only as the SOW lists and as applicable law allows. Client receives only a pass or fail result, unless the law allows more and the worker has authorised it.

7.3 Client shall maintain and enforce a policy prohibiting harassment and discrimination that covers Assigned Workers, and shall notify Eden within [NUMBER] Business Day after any complaint by or about an Assigned Worker.

7.4 **Replacement.** If Client notifies Eden that an Assigned Worker is unsatisfactory within the first [NUMBER] Hours Worked of an Assignment, Eden shall try to provide a replacement and shall not bill Client for up to [NUMBER] of that worker's Hours Worked. This Section 7.4 states Client's only remedy for an unsatisfactory worker.

## 8. Conversion and buyout

8.1 If a Conversion occurs during an Assignment, or within [NUMBER] days after the Assigned Worker's last day of an Assignment, Client shall pay Eden the Conversion Fee in Exhibit A.

8.2 If a Conversion of a Referred Candidate who was never assigned occurs within [NUMBER] days after Eden disclosed the candidate, Client shall pay Eden the Direct-Hire Fee in Exhibit A.

8.3 No Conversion Fee is due for an Assigned Worker who has completed [NUMBER] Hours Worked for Client, unless the SOW states otherwise.

8.4 The Conversion Fee is due within [NUMBER] days after the Conversion. Client shall notify Eden in writing within [NUMBER] Business Days after any Conversion.

8.5 The parties agree that Eden's loss from a Conversion (recruiting, screening and onboarding cost, and lost margin) is hard to measure at signing, and that the Conversion Fee is a reasonable estimate of that loss and not a penalty.

8.6 Nothing in this Agreement prevents an Assigned Worker from accepting employment with Client or anyone else. Section 8 sets the fee Client pays when it hires; it is not a restriction on the worker.

## 9. Non-solicitation

9.1 During an Assignment and for [NUMBER] days after it ends, Client shall not place or engage an Assigned Worker through another staffing company or labour supplier. If it does, Section 8 applies as if a Conversion had occurred.

9.2 During the term of this Agreement and for [NUMBER] months after it ends, Eden shall not knowingly solicit for employment any Client employee with whom Eden dealt under this Agreement. General advertisements not aimed at Client's employees are not solicitation.

*Drafting note: Section 9 is written as a fee trigger, not a ban, to keep it narrow under state restrictive-covenant law and the DOJ/FTC Antitrust Guidelines for Business Activities Affecting Workers (January 2025). Counsel confirms for each Site state.*

## 11. Indemnities

11.1 **By Eden.** Eden shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Client and its officers, directors and employees from third-party claims, losses and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, to the extent caused by:

(a) Eden's or the EOR's failure to pay Assigned Workers' wages, or to withhold and pay payroll taxes, for Hours Worked that Client has paid for;

(b) Eden's breach of this Agreement;

(c) the negligence or wilful misconduct of Eden or its own employees; or

(d) Eden's violation of anti-discrimination law in selecting or removing Assigned Workers.

11.2 **By Client.** Client shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Eden, the EOR and their officers, directors and employees from third-party claims, losses and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, to the extent caused by:

(a) Site conditions, or Client's direction or supervision of Assigned Workers;

(b) Client's assignment of a Prohibited Duty that the SOW did not authorise;

(c) the negligence or wilful misconduct of Client or its employees, agents or other contractors;

(d) Client's violation of law, including safety, wage-hour (including off-the-clock work under Section 4.5) and anti-discrimination law;

(e) the work product, workmanship or results of work performed under Client's direction; or

(f) Client's breach of this Agreement.

11.3 **Fault only.** No party is required to indemnify another for that other party's own negligence or fault. If both parties' fault contributes, each indemnifies only in proportion to its own fault.

11.4 **Florida monetary limit.** For any Site in Florida, each party's total indemnity obligation under this Section 11 for each occurrence is limited to [$AMOUNT], which the parties agree bears a reasonable commercial relationship to this Agreement, and this limit is also stated in each Florida SOW.

*Drafting note: Fla. Stat. 725.06 voids an indemnity in a construction contract that lacks a monetary limit; ORS 30.140 (Oregon) and Idaho Code 29-114 limit indemnity for the indemnitee's own negligence. Counsel confirms whether a staffing agreement is a "construction contract" under each.*

11.5 **Procedure.** The party seeking indemnity shall notify the indemnifying party in writing within [NUMBER] days after receiving notice of a claim, give it control of the defence and settlement, and cooperate at its expense. A late notice reduces the indemnity only to the extent the delay prejudiced the indemnifying party. The indemnifying party shall not settle a claim in a way that admits fault by, or imposes an obligation on, the indemnified party without its written consent.

## 12. Limitation of liability

12.1 Neither party is liable to the other for lost profits or for indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages arising out of this Agreement.

12.2 Eden's total liability arising out of this Agreement in any [12-month period] is limited to [the greater of $AMOUNT and the amounts Client paid Eden under the SOW giving rise to the claim in the [NUMBER] months before the claim].

12.3 Sections 12.1 and 12.2 do not apply to (a) a party's indemnity obligations under Section 11 for third-party bodily injury or property damage, to the extent covered by insurance this Agreement requires; (b) fraud or wilful misconduct; or (c) Client's payment obligations.

12.4 **No warranty of work.** Eden does not warrant the work product, workmanship or results of any Assigned Worker, which Client directs and supervises. Eden does not provide construction services, design or supervision.

## 13. Compliance

13.1 **Public works.** Client shall state in each SOW whether the work is public works, Davis-Bacon or state prevailing-wage work, or subject to a collective bargaining agreement, and shall give Eden the applicable wage determination. If Client fails to do so, Client shall pay any resulting back wages, penalties and rate increases.

13.2 **Federal contractor duties.** Client shall identify in the SOW any federal-contract clause (including E-Verify) that applies to Assigned Workers.

13.3 **Licences.** Each party shall hold the licences and registrations its own activities under this Agreement require in each Site state.

## 14. Confidentiality and personal data

14.1 Each party shall keep the other's non-public business information confidential and use it only to perform this Agreement, for the term and [NUMBER] years after it ends.

14.2 Client shall protect any Assigned Worker personal data it receives, use it only to manage the Assignment, and not request Social Security numbers, I-9 documents or consumer reports except as law permits.

## 15. Term and termination

15.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until either party ends it under this Section 15.

15.2 Either party may end this Agreement on [NUMBER] days' written notice to the other.

15.3 Client may end an Assignment by notice to Eden at least [NUMBER] hours before the end of the worker's next shift. Client shall pay for all Hours Worked through the end of that shift[, and for at least [NUMBER] hours for any shift the worker reports to and Client cancels].

15.4 Either party may end this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other (a) fails to cure a breach within [NUMBER] days after written notice describing it, or (b) becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy.

15.5 Each SOW in effect when this Agreement ends continues under this Agreement until the SOW ends, unless the notice ending this Agreement also ends the SOW.

15.6 Sections 4.5, 5, 8, 9, 11, 12, 14, 16, 17 and 18 survive the end of this Agreement.

## 18. General

18.1 **Independent contractors.** Eden and Client are independent contractors. Neither is the other's agent or partner.

18.2 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, its Exhibits and the SOWs are the parties' entire agreement on their subject and replace all earlier proposals and discussions.
