# Brand brief

## Result

- **Ask (Kev, 23 Oct 2026):** a new Eden logo, and a contact sheet with brand and logo suggestions for the temp-staffing line, "Eden Express, Eden People... idk brainstorm 7-10 names." Also: "this is a great time to start building our voice, brand, visual presence."
- **Delivered:** 10 names, 3 refreshed Eden Partner Group marks (1 to 3), 6 staffing marks (4 to 9) for the top three names, on one private Artifact contact sheet: https://claude.ai/artifact/7Wh9KrafNqM984oMUYWyG6
- **Status:** draft for Marlowe's design check, then Kev. Docket checks every name and mark. Nothing is registered, ordered, printed or published.

## Brief (one paragraph)

**Who it is for:** two audiences. Construction employers and general contractors who need skilled trades and site staff fast (Florida, Idaho, Oregon), and the tradespeople who would work for the line. **What they should feel and do:** feel that this is Eden, prepared, honest and on time, and call. **Where it will be seen:** a LinkedIn banner, business cards, email signatures, a one-page overview, a site decal or hard-hat sticker, and a 16 px browser tab. **Size:** every mark must hold at 32 px and at 16 px, in one colour first, on cream and on ink.

## Design rules used

| Rule | How the marks follow it |
|---|---|
| Montserrat, cream and ink, no colour | All lock-ups are bold caps with a regular-weight name word; colour is not used |
| House of brands | Eden Partner Group marks stand bare; staffing marks share one square tile and one two-line lock-up, so the family is visible at a glance |
| Evolve, not replace | Marks 1 to 3 start from the bold-caps wordmark, the bold italic title line and the Seven Pillars colonnade already in the Eden Daily |
| Original geometry only | Rectangles and circles drawn for Eden. No stock icons, no traced marks, no generated images |

## Open points

- **Guideline naming:** `docs/brand/eden-visual-identity.pdf` lists Eden Partner Group as the venture-capital division and Eden Executive Search as headhunting. Kev's ask treats Eden Partner Group as the recruiting house. Kev to confirm which name the refreshed mark carries before a brand sheet is written.
- **Legal entity:** the plan calls the entity "Eden Staffing LLC". Whether the chosen brand is the entity name or a trade name is for Docket and the employment attorney.
- **Descriptor line:** "TRADES STAFFING" is a placeholder; the EOR relationship is not named anywhere on the marks.
