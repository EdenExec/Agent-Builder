## Why these three

- **Eden Crew:** the word the buyer and the worker both use. Strongest for recruiting tradespeople and for a truck door.
- **Eden Bench:** differentiates from a commodity labor pool and implies vetting. Best story for a client's first call.
- **Eden Level:** the most brand-like and closest to the Eden pillars, but it needs "Trades Staffing" beneath it to land.
