## What this is

Noah, here is how our lead flow runs from now on, every weeknight: Scout lands the lists in Drive, you enrich them with cell phones from ZoomInfo, and the BPO team uploads them to Crelate. One sheet, the Lead Log, shows where every list stands.

## The nightly flow

1. **By 8pm PT, Landed (Scout).** New lead sheets land in the master folder, one per role, and get a Landed date in the Lead Log. Mobile is left blank for you.
2. **10pm PT, Phones added (your routine).** Your Claude routine enriches each new sheet in ZoomInfo, fills Mobile and Mobile source, and dates Phones added in the Lead Log.
3. **Overnight, In Crelate (BPO team).** They upload every sheet with Phones added dated, then date In Crelate.
4. **Next morning, Moved (Radar).** Finished sheets move to Uploaded to Crelate, so the master folder only holds work in flight.
