## Why this matters

Every weeknight Eden's lead lists land in one Drive folder, Noah adds cell phone numbers, and your team loads them into Crelate. When you finish, the recruiters wake up to leads they can call. Your part is the last step that turns a list into calls.

Kev's rule for the Crelate pipeline: **only active job orders live in the job columns** (Retained, TOP Priority, GO NOW, Typical, Client Portals). **Every lead list goes into a bucket in the Leads / Buckets column.** Recruiters work the bucket, and move a person to the active job order only after they have engaged them. Never load leads straight onto an active job order, and never create a new job order for a list.

## When

Every weeknight, Monday to Friday Pacific, after 10pm Pacific (Noah's enrichment runs at 10pm). Finish before 6am Pacific. Never on Saturday.

## Steps, every night

1. **Open the Lead Log.** Your work is every row where **Phones added** has a date and **In Crelate** is blank. If there are none, you are done for the night.
2. **Open the sheet** from the row's Drive link. Read the Owner note on the Lead Log first. If it says "discreet client", do not upload; message Radar (radar@edenexec.com) and skip to the next row.
3. **Find the bucket.** In Crelate, open the pipeline and look in the **Leads / Buckets** column for a bucket with the same client and role as the sheet title (for example the sheet "19 · Triton · Project Manager, West Palm Beach FL" goes to a bucket like "Triton - PM - West Palm"). If there is no match, create one **in Leads / Buckets only**, named **Client - Role - City Leads**. Never put it in Retained, TOP Priority, GO NOW, Typical or Client Portals.
4. **Upload the rows** into that bucket, one record per person:
   - Name, Title, Company, LinkedIn URL.
   - **City, State and Time Zone each in its own field.** Never one combined location.
   - **Mobile** into the mobile phone field. Leave it blank if the sheet's Mobile is blank.
   - Source: "Eden lead load YYYY-MM-DD" (tonight's date).
   - Tags: Client, Role, Time Zone, and "Lead YYYY-MM-DD".
5. **Check for duplicates first.** Search Crelate by LinkedIn URL, then by name + company. If the person already exists, **add the existing record to the bucket and add the new mobile if the record has none**. Do not create a second record, and do not change the record's owner, status or notes.
6. **Special rows:**
   - "Referral ask only" rows (for example Moss): upload with the tag "Referral ask only" so nobody cold-pitches them for a job.
   - Rows Crelate already marks do-not-contact: skip and count them.
   - Rows with no name or no company: skip and count them.
7. **Date the Lead Log.** Put tonight's date (YYYY-MM-DD) in **In Crelate** on that row. In Owner note write the counts: loaded, already in Crelate, skipped, and the bucket name. Counts only, no names or numbers.
8. **Leave the sheet where it is.** Radar moves it to Uploaded to Crelate in the morning. Do not move, rename, copy or delete any sheet.
9. **Send one short note** to radar@edenexec.com when the night is done: sheets loaded, records created, duplicates matched, rows skipped, and anything that looked wrong.

## Never

- Never load a lead list onto an active job order, and never create a job order for a list. Lists go in Leads / Buckets.
- Never share a sheet, a phone number or a client name outside Eden, and never put phone numbers in an email or chat.
- Never upload pay, age, ethnicity, gender, home address or personal email, even if a column has it.
- Never edit or delete existing Crelate records beyond adding them to the bucket and filling a blank mobile.

## If something is wrong

Write it in the Lead Log Owner note, load what you can, and tell Radar in the nightly note. Examples: columns missing, a sheet with no phones at all, a bucket you are not sure about. Radar or Scout fixes the sheet; do not make a copy.

## What happens next (for context)

Recruiters work the bucket during the day. When a recruiter engages someone and they fit an active job order, the recruiter moves that person onto the one active job order for that client and role. RPs, resumes, PTCs and interviews are logged there. That is how Kev's status report counts real activity per active job.
